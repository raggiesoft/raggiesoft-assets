### **2-05-the-tyrants-throne.md**

**LORE NOTE:**
Featured in Book II, Chapter 2, Part 2 (*"The Tyrant's Throne"*). Lord Malakor's theatrical progressive-metal villain anthem in the throne room of the Shadowspire, where he lays out his chilling philosophy of extinguishing the Twin Suns to impose eternal Order over Aethel.

**LYRICS:**

[Instrumental Intro: Ominous Harpsichord-Synth Arpeggios & Searing Progressive Metal Guitar Solo]

And dream of some nostalgic, sun-drenched day
You see this world, so flawed and incomplete
A chaotic dance of triumph and defeat
The golden sun, a rash and fiery breath
The silver one, a whisper soft as death
They flicker, fade, a fickle, cosmic game
But I have come to conquer and to tame!

One perfect dawn, born of unending dark!
