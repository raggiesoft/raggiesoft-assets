**LORE NOTE:**
In the *1883* psychological sanctuary, the 1981 Shap Fell tragedy never happened. Morning breaks over the slate roof as all six siblings—Alistair, Gwen, Simon, Julian, Rhys, and Claire—gather in the Victorian parlor ("four at the table, two by the fire") with Gwen's flute leading the morning song.
