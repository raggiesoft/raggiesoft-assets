**LORE NOTE:**
A slow, haunting, broken-music-box reprise of "The Grand Decree" that plays as the Grand Wizard's True Form fractures and dissolves into harmless white pixels after Ocea and Tay unite both halves of their clay medallion.

**LYRICS:**

[Intro]
(A slow, distorted, broken 16-bit music box playing the melody of The Grand Decree)
(Static and glitch noises crackling in the background)

[Verse 1]
(Weak, glitching synth voice)
The directory is falling... the bytes begin to tear...
My perfect, flawless order... vanishing in air...
I wrote the final function, I held it in my hand...
(The voice stutters and pitches down)

[Chorus]
(A slow, mournful, heavy waltz, missing beats as if the song is dying)
By the power... of the Grand... Decree...
The Dream Kingdom... is deleting me...
Every pixel, every byte... fading into white...
(The music slows down completely, like a dying battery)

[Outro]
(A final, drawn-out glitching chord that snaps into complete silence)
