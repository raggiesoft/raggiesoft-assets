# Homeward on This Day

### Lore
This track originally debuted on the 1996 symphonic rock Christmas album "A Symphony of Snow," becoming a familiar staple for Leo and his family during the holidays. Seven years later, as Leo sat in Sea-Tac Airport on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving 2003—having just escaped a harrowing ordeal—the familiar track began playing over the terminal speakers. The song's profound message of "coming home" hit him like a physical blow. The track was transformed from a simple holiday anthem into a deeply personal, overwhelming declaration of survival, love, and the unyielding devotion of his best friend, Olivia, who was finally bringing him back to the safety of his family.

## Upon This Winter's Night
### Lore

The English (United States) studio master of the legendary beacon track ('Sonic Armor'). Originally recorded in one take on December 11, 1996 in Ewell, Virginia—anchored by Maria Harrison's glockenspiel, Gabriel Rowlands' baritone and guitar, Gwendolyn Rowlands' arrangements, and Julian's wind-up Brahms 'Wiegenlied' music box—and hijacked onto the Shenandoah FM repeaters to reach 14-year-old Julian during his abduction to Ohio. Years later, this 1997 single became the sonic armor that grounded Matthew at the airport hub during his rescue flight home from Seattle.

### Lore
