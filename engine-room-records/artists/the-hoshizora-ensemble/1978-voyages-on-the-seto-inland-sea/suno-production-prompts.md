## 1. Artist Overview & The Three Release Dates (`1978` / `2002` / `2026`)
* **Artist Name:** **The Hoshizora Ensemble** (`星空アンサンブル`)
* **Primary Album (US Title):** ***Hoshizora no Uta: Voyages on the Seto Inland Sea*** (Original Japanese Title: ***星空のうた / Hoshizora no Uta***)
* **Genre:** 1970s Japanese Folk-Pop / Maritime City Pop / Acoustic Lullaby
* **Three Canonical Release Dates:**
  1. **Original Japanese Release (`1978-05-15`):** Commissioned in Osaka, Japan for the Seto Inland Sea Passenger Line's flagship overnight ferry, the **`Ferry Hoshizora`** (`フェリーほしぞら`), sailing between Osaka and Beppu across the Seto Inland Sea.
  2. **United States Release on Engine Room Records, LLC (`2002-07-12`):**
     During the Japanese leg of **The Stardust Engine**'s **2001–2002 *Midnight Drivers* World Tour**, CEO **Holly O'Connell** refused to subject her brother **Ryan O'Connell** to another exhausting short-haul domestic airport transfer and narrow aisle chair between their Osaka concert dates and Kyushu. Instead, she booked accessible cabins for Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, Tyler, and herself aboard the overnight *Ferry Hoshizora* across the Seto Inland Sea.
     When the polite four-tone chime echoed over the cabin PA and *Hoshizora no Uta* floated across the water, Ryan and Cassidy—both autistic and sensory-exhausted from months of nocturnal arena touring—felt their environmental threat level drop to zero. That serene overnight crossing on the Seto Inland Sea not only inspired the band's **2003 *Twin Moons* planetarium project (*Moon 1: Sanctuary [Zero-G]*)**, it moved Holly so deeply that before leaving Japan she tracked down the 1978 Osaka studio masters, negotiated the **American distribution rights** for *Engine Room Records, LLC*, and commissioned an official **English-language studio translation** alongside the Japanese originals.
  3. **Real-World Digital Release (`2026-10-18`):** Published to **The Listening Room** (`https://music.raggiesoft.com/the-hoshizora-ensemble`) and streaming platforms.
