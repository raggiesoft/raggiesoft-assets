Back at CPI for the Spring semester, Jordan's hard-won peace is violently interrupted when he receives an ominous email summoning him alone to Hall Director Kelsey's office at 3:00 PM. When the uniformed officer formally addresses him by his full legal name, "Aubrey Jordan Fuller," the trauma of his mother's abuse and the terrifying realization of her reach triggers a severe autistic meltdown.
