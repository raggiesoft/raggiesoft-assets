**LORE NOTE:**
The jubilant 1980s synth-rock epilogue of *The Eleventh Child*. Shedding the Fuller surname and the Sinclair protocols forever, Jordan Shawn Kelly takes his wife's family name and honors Shawn with his middle name, standing side-by-side with his best friend Mark Anderson in a double wedding that forms their new constellation.
