**LORE NOTE:**
Released in 1987 alongside the rest of *Electric Color* and buried by Apex Records at the very end of the album, this is the band's true origin anthem. In truth, *"The Stardust Engine"* was the very first song Cassidy and Ryan O'Connell came up with in 1986 when their twin cousins Evan and Tyler Wright joined them at CPI. Too scared at the time to shop the song around, the four of them kept it to themselves and decided to use that first song to name their band. It is the only track on the 1987 debut that perfectly blends the "Stardust" (synths) and "Engine" (rock) identities. Critics and chart historians agree: had Apex released this as the lead single instead of burying it, it would have spent **11 straight weeks at #1**, dethroning the existing record holder for the decade.
