**LORE NOTE:**
Released in 1987 alongside the rest of *Electric Color* and buried by Apex Records at the very end of the album, this is the band's true origin anthem. *"The Stardust Engine"* was the very first song Cassidy and Ryan O'Connell constructed in the Progress Street NE basement on December 12, 1985 after their twin cousins Evan and Tyler Wright joined them at CPI. Too scared at the time to shop the song around, the four of them kept it to themselves and decided to use that first song to name their band—celebrating their love of the cosmos ("Stardust") and the rockets to get you there ("Engine").

Musically, the heart of the song is the **chorus call-and-response** (`01:18–01:22`, `01:33–01:37`, `02:13–02:16`, `03:32–03:35`): right after *"We're burning on the fuel of a brand new light!"* and *"Turning our future on, shining so bright!"*, Cassidy sends up a soaring vocal call—the pilot calling out into the dark—and her Vektron Z-1 analog synthesizer (`Brass_Lead_04`) immediately answers her voice with the gleaming staccato brass leitmotif response (*Ba-da-DA-da-DAAA!*), the spacecraft confirming trajectory. That vocal call and synthesizer leitmotif response was Cassidy's deepest musical pride and joy (which is why its deliberate erasure from the chorus of 1990's *Live in Chicago* broke the family's heart, before being triumphantly restored on 1999's *Hard Reset Live* and answered by the entire CPI Marching Forgers trumpet section on 2016's *Live at The Crucible* as CPI's promise to always have Cassidy's back). Critics and chart historians agree: had Apex released this as the lead single instead of burying it at Track 13, it would have spent **11 straight weeks at #1**, dethroning the existing record holder for the decade.
