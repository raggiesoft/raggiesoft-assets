## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

Released in 1987 alongside the rest of *Electric Color* and buried by Apex Records at the very end of the album, this is the band's true origin anthem. *"The Stardust Engine"* was the very first song Cassidy and Ryan O'Connell came up with in 1986 when their twin cousins Evan and Tyler Wright joined them at CPI. Too scared at the time to shop the song around, the four of them kept it to themselves and decided to use that first song to name their band—celebrating their love of the cosmos ("Stardust") and the rockets to get you there ("Engine"). (Apex Records later screwed around and labeled Cassidy as their "Stardust" and Ryan as their "Engine"—a cheap 1980s corporate naming gimmick the band hated because it took away from their love of the cosmos and the rockets to get you there.) Critics and chart historians agree: had Apex released this as the lead single instead of burying it, it would have spent **11 straight weeks at #1**, dethroning the existing record holder for the decade.
