## I. Canonical Lore, Release Order & Setlist Rules for *Live in Virginia Beach*

1. **Testing the New Song in Virginia Beach (First Time Recorded for a Future Live Album):**
   - By the time the 2001–2002 *Across The World Tour* rolled into the O'Connell & Wright family's original hometown of Virginia Beach, **The Stardust Engine had been road-testing a new mystery song for the past few weeks**.
   - Placed **mid-concert (Track 08)** and designed to make concert-goers think it is simply the new Stardust Engine song, the band tests whether the Virginia Beach audience already knows the words—and because hardcore Forger Nation fans had been trading tapes from the past few weeks, **the Virginia Beach audience sings right along** (matching the Suno v6 render!).
   - This makes *Live in Virginia Beach* **the first time *"The Flight Path"* is played where it will be placed onto a (future) live album**.
   - **Strict Audience Knowledge Rule:** Even though the Virginia Beach crowd knows the lyrics from the past few weeks of tour stops, **fans attending the concert do NOT know the name of the song, and they do NOT know that it is NOT a Stardust Engine song!**
2. **Canonical 5-Step Chronological Sequence:**
   1. ***Live in Virginia Beach* (The Actual Concert Happens — Spring 2002):** Having tested the new song for the past few weeks, The Stardust Engine plays *"The Flight Path"* **somewhere in the middle of the concert (Track 08)** to see if the Virginia Beach audience knows the words (the first time the song is played where it will be placed onto a future live album; the name of the song isn't given, and fans assume it is simply a new Stardust Engine song).
   2. ***Live at the Chancellery* (The Actual Concert Happens — May 2002, Charlottesville, VA) + Holly's FM Radio Campaign:** The Stardust Engine comes to ***The* University of the Piedmont (`UP`)**'s **The Chancellery** (in-universe John Paul Jones Arena) as **FRIENDS** of Albemarle County's **Crimson Node** despite the bitter CPI vs. UP rivalry. After playing their usual closer *"Electric Color"* (Track 13), they introduce **Crimson Node** as a band to close the concert (Track 14, still without giving the song's name on stage), and **Holly O'Connell works with Crimson Node to get the studio version of *"The Flight Path"* onto FM radio**.
   3. ***Crimson Node* (`2002-09-18` — Debut Studio Album, Already Produced):** Debuts on September 18, 2002, and **fans finally get the official name of the song (`"The Flight Path"`)**.
   4. ***Live at the Chancellery* (`2002-10-25` — The Live Album Is Released):** Released after *Crimson Node*'s studio debut.
   5. ***Live in Virginia Beach* (`2002-11-22` — The Live Album Is Released):** Released after *Live at the Chancellery*, unlocking the archival recording of the first time *"The Flight Path"* was ever captured for a live album in its mid-concert placement!
3. **Opening & Closing Rule + Permanent Boundaries:**
   - **MUST open with *"The Stardust Engine"*** (Track 01) and **MUST close with *"Electric Color"*** (Track 14).
   - ***"If You Walked Away"* is strictly OFF LIMITS** (replaced in the ballad spotlight by *"Echoes in the Wire"*).
