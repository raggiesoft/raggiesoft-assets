**LORE NOTE:**
After being salvaged from the debris field in *Mayday*, the album's tempo slows into a brooding 1980s hard rock ballad. *Star City (Orbital Ward)* transposes Ryan's actual 1990 recovery at Star City General Hospital into an orbital medical bay. 

The planetarium narrator returns to clinically assess the damage, juxtaposing Ryan's visceral frustration. The lyrics adapt the strict survival routines of "Protocol T-10". The "four-hour clock" for hydration, the "Iron Dome" morning routine, the 15-minute pressure "weight shifts," and the "Freight Dog" twin-lift are all translated into the mechanical maintenance of a heavily damaged but surviving starship.
