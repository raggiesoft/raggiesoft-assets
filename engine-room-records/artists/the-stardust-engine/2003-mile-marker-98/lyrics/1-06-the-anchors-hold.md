**LORE NOTE:**
Slowing down the tempo but maintaining the heavy 1980s rock edge, *The Anchor's Hold* is a massive, emotional power ballad. Operating entirely from Ryan's perspective, this track details the early days of his recovery in the Star City Orbital Ward. 

The core of the song is a tribute to Cassidy. Refusing to leave his side, the "Anchor" grounds the broken "Engine." The lyrics explicitly reference their physical bond—the grounding comfort of a tight embrace—as the only force strong enough to hold his fragmented pieces together while the medical diagnostics run in the background. It is raw, vulnerable, and unmistakably heavy.
