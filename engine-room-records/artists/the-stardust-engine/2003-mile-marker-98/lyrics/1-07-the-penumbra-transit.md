**LORE NOTE:**
Released from the Star City Orbital Ward, Ryan and his family begin the long journey home. However, their trajectory inevitably carries them into the shadow of the parent planet. This track serves as the heavy-rock mirror to the coldest, most brooding track on *Sanctuary (Zero-G)*.

Bringing back the 1990s planetarium narrator, the track opens with the same educational but ominous warning about the nature of a celestial shadow. Operating from Ryan's point of view, he is no longer alone in the freezing vacuum of space. With Cassidy piloting the vessel, the track beautifully fuses Ryan's driving 1980s hard rock with her signature analog synthesizers and icy spatial reverb. It is a heavy, atmospheric anthem about facing the darkest part of the journey together.
