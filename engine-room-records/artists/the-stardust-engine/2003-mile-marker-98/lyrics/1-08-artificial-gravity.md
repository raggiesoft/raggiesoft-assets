**LORE NOTE:**
Positioned as the parallel to Cassidy's *Goldilocks Zone*, this track represents Ryan finding his new equilibrium. The narrative reflects the "Stalemate Year" of 1991, a brutal boot camp for survival where Ryan had to learn to operate under the strict new physical limitations of his T-10 injury. 

Musically, the aggressive, face-melting distortion of the previous tracks is muted. Ryan is learning to play the guitar from a seated position, so the track opens with a raw, acoustic 1980s rock feel before slowly building into a restrained, mid-tempo electric groove. Lyrically, it uses the sci-fi concept of generating "Artificial Gravity" by spinning a damaged ship's hull to represent Ryan finding a way to keep himself grounded when his natural legs no longer work.
