**LORE NOTE:**
After the emotional reflection of *The Anchor's Weight*, the album drastically pulls back the tempo and descends into the Sea of Tranquility. *Silent Ocean (Mare Tranquillitatis)* is the sound of absolute, crushing isolation. 

The rhythm section is stripped down to nothing but sparse, echoing electronic percussion and a deep sub-bass that rattles the listener's chest. It is deeply melancholic and beautiful, capturing the overwhelming, terrifying scale of the universe when you are floating alone in the dark. It is a true "Vacuum Ballad."
