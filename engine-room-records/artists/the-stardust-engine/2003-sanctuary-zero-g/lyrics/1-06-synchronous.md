**LORE NOTE:**
Positioned just past the halfway mark of the CD, *Synchronous* is Cassidy’s 7-minute progressive instrumental magnum opus. In astrophysics, a tidal lock is also known as "synchronous rotation," representing perfect, mathematical alignment. 

Lyrically sparse, the track relies entirely on Cassidy's virtuosic classical piano training layered over massive, sweeping analog synthesizers. It is an intricate, building instrumental suite that maps out the orbital mechanics of the band's independent empire. It pushes the physical limits of the CD format and the emotional limits of The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound®.
