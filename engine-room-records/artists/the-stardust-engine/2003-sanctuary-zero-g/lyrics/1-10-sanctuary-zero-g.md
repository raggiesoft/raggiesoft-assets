**LORE NOTE:**
The epic 74-minute journey reaches its cinematic conclusion. *Sanctuary (Zero-G)* pushes the physical limitations of the CD format and the emotional boundaries of The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound®. 

Serving as the climax to Cassidy's solo orbit, this massive, symphonic track introduces a male vocal into the mix. Representing the intercepted transmission from Ryan's parallel *Mile Marker 98* orbit, the sibling voices blend and trade lines organically. To simulate the impact of the crash, Ryan's initial transmission is heavily distorted and unintelligible, breaking through the absolute silence of Cassidy's orbit. The album then beautifully closes with the 1990s planetarium narrator bringing the documentary exhibition to an end.
