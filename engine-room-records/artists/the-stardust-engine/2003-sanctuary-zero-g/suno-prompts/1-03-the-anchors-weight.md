## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

Following the high-speed slingshot of *Periapsis*, the album settles into its first true atmospheric ballad. *The Anchor's Weight* explores the psychological concept of "phantom gravity." Even while floating in the absolute safety of her zero-g sanctuary, Cassidy still feels the residual, ghostly weight of the burdens she carried on Earth. 

Lyrically, it explicitly reflects on the 1992 *Friction* trial, viewing the trauma from a million miles away. It isn't a song of anger; it is a melancholic but ultimately peaceful acceptance that while the heavy chains have been cut, the metal still remembers the weight. Musically, it is a flawless execution of the "Studio Master" prompt, leaning heavily into Cassidy's classical piano training layered beneath thick, shimmering analog synths.
