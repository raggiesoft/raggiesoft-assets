## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

If *Silent Ocean* was about the terrifying emptiness of space, *Deep Pressure* is about the overwhelming comfort of a manufactured atmosphere. Operating as a direct cosmic sequel to the B-Side *No Hidden Gravity*, this track internalizes the concept of the "autistic safe person." 

Musically, it is the sonic equivalent of a weighted blanket. The mix is incredibly dense, pushing the bass and mid-tones to the front while dialing back the sharp, piercing highs. Cassidy's vocals are layered and harmonized to sound like they are wrapping around the listener. It transforms the concept of heavy gravity from a destructive force into a protective, sensory safe space.
