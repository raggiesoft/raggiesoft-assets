## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

Exiting the mathematical perfection of *Synchronous*, the orbit inevitably carries the satellite into the shadow of the parent planet. *The Penumbra* is undeniably the coldest, most brooding track on *Sanctuary (Zero-G)*. 

Bringing back the 1990s planetarium narrator from *Silent Ocean*, the track opens with an educational but ominous warning about the nature of a celestial shadow. It then drops into a brooding, industrial-tinged piece of darkwave. Driven by an unrelenting, mechanical drum beat and icy, piercing analog leads, it explores the sudden fear of the unknown when the light source is cut off and the solar batteries begin to drain in the freezing vacuum of space.
