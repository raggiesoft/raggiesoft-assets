## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

Safely established in her habitable orbit, Cassidy finally turns her attention outward. *Telemetry* is a tight, kinetic, experimental 80s pop track that represents her communicating back to Ryan on his parallel, destructive orbit (*Mile Marker 98*). 

Relying heavily on rhythmic, pulsing analog synthesizers engineered to sound like radar pings and data transmissions, the track feels like a high-speed data stream shooting across the void. It serves as the narrative bridge between the two albums, acknowledging the "Engine" without letting his gravity pull her out of the "Anchor's" sanctuary.
