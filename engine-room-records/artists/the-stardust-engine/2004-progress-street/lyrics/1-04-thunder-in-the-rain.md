**LORE NOTE:**
Opening with a deceptive ten-second Vangelis-style synthesizer swell before exploding into a 150 BPM C-minor stadium rocker. Placed directly after "Seconds Into Forever" as Track 4, this song serves as Part II of The Stardust Engine's published narrative story of fraternal twins John and Erin. At a campus speed-dating event where the organizer was supposed to play "Seconds Into Forever" (Track 3) but accidentally skipped to "Thunder in the Rain" (Track 4), John sits across from a magnetic woman while the lyrics roar ("The neon signs are flashing 'Danger' in red / But I'd rather be living than safe and dead"). Crucially, John mistakes the high-voltage adrenaline for romance and does not realize she is going to be his abuser—a published story detail that later makes Freddie and Paige wonder why they have heard "Thunder in the Rain" before when the song surfaces in their own lives.
