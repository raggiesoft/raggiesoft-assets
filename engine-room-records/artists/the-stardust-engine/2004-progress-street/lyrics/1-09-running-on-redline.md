**LORE NOTE:**
A fiery 140 BPM 1980s arena rocker that captures the band pouring every last ounce of energy into their third studio album in under two years (*Sanctuary: Zero-G* and *Mile Marker 98* in 2003, followed immediately by *Progress Street* in 2004), directly foreshadowing the physical and emotional exhaustion that triggered their Burnout Hiatus.
