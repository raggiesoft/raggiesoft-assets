**LORE NOTE:**
Originally tracked as a stripped-down, heartbroken acoustic sketch in late 1990 right after The Crash (*"Empty Arenas (Early Demo)"*, later released on 2007's *Lost Sounds*), the band resurrected the song in 2004 for *Progress Street* and transformed it into a massive, full-band 1980s Album-Oriented Rock (AOR) stadium anthem. Driven by Tyler's thunderous gated-reverb acoustic drum kit, Evan's locked-in bass, Cassidy's 1984 analog synth swells and backing harmonies, and Ryan's crying overdriven electric guitar, the polished 2004 lyrics reframe the empty arena: what began in 1990 as a young man's terror that the lights had gone out forever becomes a triumphant post-encore reflection after filling stadiums across North America, bridging the high-speed exhaustion of *"Running on Redline"* directly into the farewell of *"Last Bus Home (Lights Out on Progress Street)"*.
