## I. Canonical Lore & Setlist Rules for *Live in Norfolk* (2005)

1. **The Tour That Triggered the 2005–2014 Burnout Hiatus:**
   - Between 2001 and 2004, The Stardust Engine wrote, recorded, and released four full studio albums in rapid succession:
     1. *Midnight Drivers* (2001)
     2. *Moon 1: Sanctuary (Zero-G)* (2003 — Cassidy's solo vocal *The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™* album)
     3. *Moon 2: Mile Marker 98* (2003 — Ryan's solo vocal hard-rock confrontation with the 1990 bus crash)
     4. *Progress Street* (2004 — the Blacksburg homecoming AOR album)
   - Immediately taking both halves of the *Twin Moons* project and *Progress Street* onto a grueling 2003–2004 tour left all five family members physically and sensorily exhausted. This final tour stop under the concrete geodesic dome of the **Norfolk Scope Arena** is the exact reason the band needed a decade-long breather (the **2005–2014 Burnout Hiatus**), before returning rested and recharged with 2015's ***Reignition***.
2. **Mandatory Opener & Closer Rule:**
   - **Track 01 (Opener):** **MUST open with *"The Stardust Engine"***.
   - **Final Track (Closer):** **MUST close with *"Electric Color"***.
3. **Unbreakable Setlist Boundaries:**
   - ***"If You Walked Away"* is STRICTLY OFF LIMITS:** Never played live in the independent era to respect Cassidy's autistic needs.
   - ***"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"* is NOT Played Here:** By band vow, *"Saturday Night Lights: The Crucible"* will **only ever be performed live inside The Crucible** in Blacksburg (where it makes its live concert debut on 2016's *Live at the Crucible*).
   - **No 5:09 Radio Edit of *"Escape Velocity (Ad Astra)"*.**
