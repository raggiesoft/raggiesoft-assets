# Lost Sounds (2007)

A compilation of B-sides, outtakes, and rare vault tracks recorded between 1986 and 1992, during the band's tumultuous era under Apex Records.

## Track Notes
**1. The Stardust Engine (The Rejected Mix)**

**The Lore:** 
During the recording of their canonical cinematic masterpiece, Apex Records (obsessed with chasing radio singles and profit) brought in a heavy-handed "hit-maker" producer to forcefully re-engineer the band's signature track. 

Instead of the atmospheric, emotional journey the band had envisioned, this producer completely stripped the song down and built it back up as a massive, driving 1980s stadium rock anthem. He sped up the tempo, cranked up the electric guitars, and anchored it with a heavy pop-rock drumbeat. 

The band fought tooth and nail against this "Apex Mix," viewing it as a soulless corporate sellout of their artistic vision. After a massive internal battle, the band won, and their atmospheric cut made it onto the album. Apex, in retaliation, spitefully locked this driving radio mix deep in the vault. 

It became a "holy grail" myth among hardcore fans for two decades. Finally, years after breaking free of their contract in 1992, the band officially released this fascinating "what-if" alternate reality track as the centerpiece of their 2007 *Lost Sounds* compilation.
