### **1-01-the-stardust-engine-alternate.md**
***

**LORE NOTE:**
This alternate version of "The Stardust Engine" was one of the original demos shopped around to record companies when the band formed in 1986. Apex Records signed them but rejected this version, burying the final studio version as track 13 on 1987's *Electric Color*. The band famously despises Apex for what happened to them during the 1992 label dispute. For this vault release, they officially retitled this early cut "(The Rejected Mix)" instead of naming Apex Records directly, serving as a quiet nod to their lingering resentment.

Yeah, we're taking flight
Turning our future on, shining so bright!

(Verse 3)
They tried to ground us, keep us small
Tell us that we were gonna fall
But they don't know the things we know
They haven't seen the places we'll go

(Chorus)
We are the Stardust Engine!
Racing through the night
We're burning on the fuel of a brand new light

Yeah, we're taking flight
Turning our future on, shining so bright!

(Guitar Solo)

**LORE NOTE:**

It was ultimately deemed too depressing for the *Friction* era and was kept locked away. Its inclusion on *Lost Sounds* is a poignant reminder of the darkest period in the band's history.

Recorded entirely in secret during their bitter legal dispute with Apex Records, the band absolutely shreds their former label for their suffocating creative control. The sheer anger in Ryan's guitar work makes this a standout deep cut. It was never intended for public release, serving strictly as a therapeutic middle finger to the studio executives.

Burn it to the ground!
We're taking back the music!
We're taking back the sound!
You can keep the money, you can keep the fame
But you're never gonna own our name!
Tear up the contract!

[Verse 2]
Read the fine print, the devil's in the ink
They tell you what to wear, they tell you what to think
A manufactured dream on an assembly line
Well you can take your plastic crown, the kingdom is mine!

[Chorus]
Tear up the contract!
Burn it to the ground!
We're taking back the music!
We're taking back the sound!
You can keep the money, you can keep the fame
But you're never gonna own our name!
Tear up the contract!

[Bridge]
(Driving, angry bassline with feedback swells)
We are not your property!
We are not your toys!
We're bringing back the static!
We're bringing back the noise!
(Scream) NOISE!

[Guitar Solo]
(A frantic, blistering punk guitar solo, heavily distorted and fast)

[Chorus]
Tear up the contract!
Burn it to the ground!
We're taking back the music!
We're taking back the sound!
You can keep the money, you can keep the fame
But you're never gonna own our name!
Tear up the contract!

[Outro]
Tear it up!
Burn it down!

### **1-08-steel-and-synthetics.md**
***

**LORE NOTE:**
The final track of the compilation, originally written during the 1997 *Hard Reset* sessions. It serves as a love letter to the band's dual identities—the "Steel" of rock and the "Synthetics" of electronic music. It was cut simply due to runtime constraints on the double album, but serves as the perfect closer for *Lost Sounds*.

**LYRICS:**

[Intro]
(A driving, heavy drumbeat accompanied by a thick, analog synth bass and a distorted rhythm guitar)

[Verse 1]
They said we had to choose a side
The organic truth or the electric ride
They wanted us to draw the line
Between the circuit board and the grand design

[Chorus]
But we are made of steel and synthetics!
Flesh and bone and pure kinetics!
A heavy riff and a digital beat
The cold machine and the human heat!
Steel and synthetics!

[Verse 2]
The strings are bleeding, the motherboard's fried
There's no place left for us to hide
We take the rust and we make it shine
We take the code and we make it divine

[Chorus]
'Cause we are made of steel and synthetics!
Flesh and bone and pure kinetics!
A heavy riff and a digital beat
The cold machine and the human heat!
Steel and synthetics!

[Bridge]
(A staccato, syncopated rhythm section)
Upload the soul!
Plug in the heart!
This is where the science and the magic start!

[Guitar and Synth Duel]
(An epic, blazing solo section where a heavy metal guitar solo trades frantic licks with a rapid-fire synthesizer arpeggio)

[Chorus]
We are made of steel and synthetics!
Flesh and bone and pure kinetics!
A heavy riff and a digital beat
The cold machine and the human heat!
Steel and synthetics!

[Outro]
Steel!
Synthetics!
Human heat!
(The track ends on a massive, combined guitar and synth power chord that rings out for ten seconds before a digital power-down sound cuts it off)
