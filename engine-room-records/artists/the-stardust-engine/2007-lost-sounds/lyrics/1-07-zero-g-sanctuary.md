**LORE NOTE:**
An outtake from the 2003 *Sanctuary: Zero-G* sessions. While the band loved the atmospheric, floating vibe of the track, they felt it disrupted the pacing of the final album, which they wanted to keep more grounded in its rock elements. It remained a beloved studio jam in the vault until this compilation.
