## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

An early demo outtake of the massive closer from the 2003 *Sanctuary: Zero-G* album. Before it was expanded into a 74-minute symphonic journey with a spoken-word planetarium outro, it existed as this raw, atmospheric space-rock demo. It showcases the core duet between Ryan and Cassidy and the foundation of The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound® before the studio gloss and orchestral layers were applied.
