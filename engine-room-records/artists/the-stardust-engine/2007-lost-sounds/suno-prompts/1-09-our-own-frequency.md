## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

A brand new track recorded specifically for the 2007 *Lost Sounds* compilation, intentionally designed to simulate the stripped-down, acoustic music Ryan and Cassidy were making together back in 1985 (before their cousins Evan and Tyler joined to form The Stardust Engine). After digging through years of painful, heavily-manufactured Apex Records history, they wanted to close the album by returning to their roots. Written and recorded in a single afternoon with just an acoustic guitar and a piano, "Our Own Frequency" is a triumphant, intimate declaration of their bond as brother and sister. It is the exact kind of acoustic scratch track they always wanted to make, completely free of label interference.
