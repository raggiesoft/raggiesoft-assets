**LORE NOTE:**
Written as a direct response to the overwhelming press cycle and industry expectations that caused their 2008 burnout hiatus. "The Noise Outside" explores Ryan and Cassidy physically and metaphorically locking the studio doors to shut out the world. Driven by a warm acoustic guitar and an atmospheric synth pad, the track emphasizes their need to find a quiet space away from the friction of the industry.
