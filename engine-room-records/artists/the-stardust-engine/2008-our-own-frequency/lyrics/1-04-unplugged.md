**LORE NOTE:**
A literal and metaphorical deconstruction of the band's massive arena-rock sound. "Unplugged" is about cutting the power lines to the big machines and walking away from the "crazy circus" of the music industry. It leans heavily into a mellow acoustic guitar paired with a soft, atmospheric synth pad, creating a nocturnal, reflective mood as the siblings contemplate their hiatus.
