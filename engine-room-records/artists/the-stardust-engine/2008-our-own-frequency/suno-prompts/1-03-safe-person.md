## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

The emotional core of the album. "Safe Person" strips away the protective rock-star armor to reveal the vulnerability underneath. Ryan, seated at his acoustic guitar, sings to Cassidy about being her anchor when the stadium lights and screaming crowds become too much to bear. Returning to the pure acoustic guitar and piano arrangement, this track cements their unbreakable sibling bond.
