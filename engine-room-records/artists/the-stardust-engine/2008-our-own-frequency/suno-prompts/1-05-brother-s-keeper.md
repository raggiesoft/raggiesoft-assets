## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

"Brother's Keeper" explores the profound, life-or-death mutual protection Ryan and Cassidy offer one another. Due to his T-10 paralysis, Ryan literally would not survive without Cassidy. She fiercely guards him and performs his daily ADLs (Activities of Daily Living)—including toileting requirements and anti-atrophy leg stretches. At night, they share a bed pushed against the wall, with Ryan on the inside and Cassidy sleeping on the edge to act as a human bed-rail so he doesn't roll off. In return for this intense physical devotion, Ryan acts as Cassidy's emotional anchor, protecting her from the brutal psychological toll of the music industry. The acoustic guitar and piano arrangement creates a delicate, vulnerable atmosphere for this raw confession of absolute co-dependence. (The lyrics are intentionally written from a unified, perspective-neutral standpoint to reflect their shared reality).
