## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

"Through the Static" explores Ryan and Cassidy's almost telepathic connection, born from years of shared trauma, recovery, and absolute reliance on one another. The title refers to the chaos of the music industry, the physical realities of Ryan's paralysis, and the overwhelming noise of the world. In the midst of all that static, they can always find each other's signal. A glance across a crowded room, a shift in breathing in the dark, a subtle change in the rhythm of the wheels—they understand it all. It is a song about tuning out the interference and focusing on the one frequency that matters: their bond. The acoustic guitar and piano arrangement leaves plenty of space for their vocal harmonies to interlock. (The lyrics are written to be perspective-neutral and fluid so that whichever vocal Suno randomly assigns won't break the canon).
