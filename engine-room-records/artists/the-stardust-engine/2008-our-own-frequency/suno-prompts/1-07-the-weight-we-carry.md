## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

"The Weight We Carry" acknowledges that their profound codependence, while beautiful and necessary, is incredibly heavy. It’s an honest, acoustic confession that sometimes the pressure of being everything to someone else is overwhelming. Ryan carries the physical weight of his condition and the guilt of needing Cassidy so much. Cassidy carries the emotional weight of his physical survival and the constant vigilance required. It’s not resentment, but rather a somber realization of their reality. The song is a quiet promise that even when the weight is crushing, they will never drop each other. (The lyrics are written to be perspective-neutral and fluid so that whichever vocal Suno randomly assigns won't break the canon).
