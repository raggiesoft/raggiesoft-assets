## IV. Narrative & Studio Lore

"Just Us" is the closing track of the album. After exploring their profound trauma, intense codependence, and the noise of the industry, the album ends on a note of quiet peace. It’s a stripped-down, acoustic lullaby. No matter what happens—whether the band fails or succeeds, whether the pain worsens or fades—at the end of the day, it is just Ryan and Cassidy. They are enough for each other. It's a gentle, reassuring end to a deeply emotional album.
