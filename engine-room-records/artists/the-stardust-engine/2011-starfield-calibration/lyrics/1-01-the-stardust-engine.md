**LORE NOTE:**
Like the rest of *Electric Color*, *"The Stardust Engine"* was officially released in 1987 (buried by Apex Records at the very end of the album as Track 13). In truth, it was the very first song Cassidy and Ryan O'Connell came up with in 1986 when their twin cousins Evan and Tyler Wright joined them at CPI in Blacksburg. Too scared at the time to shop the song around, the four of them kept it to themselves and decided to use that first song to name their band: **The Stardust Engine**. Twenty-five years later, the band's eponymous theme is reborn as the opening calibration sequence of 2011's *Starfield Calibration*—stripped of Apex Records' 1987 neon gloss and rebuilt from the ground up inside Cassidy O'Connell's proprietary **The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™** (an in-universe trademark of Engine Room Records, LLC; not registered in real life), suspending Ryan and Cassidy's sibling duet inside a 360-degree zero-gravity planetarium dome.
