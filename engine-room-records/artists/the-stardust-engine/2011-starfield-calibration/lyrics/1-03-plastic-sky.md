**LORE NOTE:**
Originally written in 1987 as a sharp critique of Los Angeles artifice (*"Look up at the ceiling, painted blue and white / There are no stars in this fake night"*), *"Plastic Sky"* undergoes a breathtaking thematic inversion on *Starfield Calibration*. Performed inside real-world planetarium domes where the ceiling literally opens into a simulated universe, Cassidy's solo vocal and soaring analog keytar solo shatter the painted fiberglass ceiling and launch the listener into open orbit.
