**LORE NOTE:**
On 1987's *Electric Color*, *"Worlds Apart"* was marketed by Apex Records as a conventional separation ballad, though Ironheads quickly decoded its true meaning: the agonizing distance between Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell when industry handlers tried to divide them in the studio. Re-engineered in **The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™**, the song's "silent satellite" metaphor becomes literal—two voices separated across the vacuum of space, locking onto the same frequency across a million miles of static.
