**LORE NOTE:**
Originally the emotional centerpiece of 1987's *Electric Color*—chronicling how Cassidy's grounding presence kept Ryan tethered through sensory overload and industry hostility—*"My Anchor Holds"* becomes the gravitational thesis of *Starfield Calibration*. In orbital mechanics, tidal lock occurs when two bodies are permanently bound by mutual gravity; Cassidy's solo vocal performance transforms the 1987 acoustic-rock anthem into a weightless cathedral of analog warmth.
