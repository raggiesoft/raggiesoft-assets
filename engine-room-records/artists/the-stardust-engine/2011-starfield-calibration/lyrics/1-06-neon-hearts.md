**LORE NOTE:**
The title track of the band's 1989 sophomore album was originally an icy, high-BPM synth-pop single masking the band's growing exhaustion on the Apex promotional circuit (*"Pumping electricity instead of blood / Caught up in a technicolor flood"*). Recalibrated at 104 BPM in **The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™**, Cassidy strips away the frantic 1989 commercial anxiety, letting the "broken glass and argon gas" float in a serene, midnight-blue planetarium field.
