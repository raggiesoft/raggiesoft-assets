**LORE NOTE:**
On 1987's *Electric Color*, *"Rearview Mirror"* was Cassidy's quiet lament about feeling like a passenger in her own career (*"I'm just a passenger in this ride / With all this 'Stardust' left inside"*), as Apex Records reduced her and Ryan's love of the cosmos and rockets into a cheap corporate naming gimmick. Looking back twenty-four years later on 2011's *Starfield Calibration* from the helm of her own zero-gravity sanctuary, Cassidy flips the 1987 lyric on its head: *"I used to be a passenger on their ride / With all this Stardust locked inside / Now I'm at the helm of the frequency / And the whole horizon belongs to me!"*
