**LORE NOTE:**
Originally recorded in September 1992 for the canceled *Friction* album (where rogue Apex executive Julian Vance infamously misinterpreted its literal space-shuttle re-entry physics—*"Here comes the friction, feel the fire"*—as sexual innuendo), *"Atmosphere"* existed for nineteen years only as an early rough cut (*"Atmosphere (1992 Demo)"* on the 1996 *Ad Astra* single and *"Atmosphere (Warehouse Demo)"* on 2007's *Lost Sounds*). Because that 1992 rough demo sounded like an early-1990s track tracked under label pressure, the band was never happy with it. On 2011's *Starfield Calibration*, Cassidy and Ryan finally deliver the **definitive, finalized version** of *"Atmosphere"*—using **The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™** to bring the song back to the lush, shimmering 1980s sound they love so much.
