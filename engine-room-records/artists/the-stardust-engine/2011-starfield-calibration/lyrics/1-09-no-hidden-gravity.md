**LORE NOTE:**
First recorded in the raw concrete echo of "The Fortress" for 1995's *The Warehouse Tapes*, *"No Hidden Gravity"* celebrated the band's liberation from predatory record contracts (*"Just a pure and weightless trajectory / In the quiet of the space we made"*). On *Starfield Calibration*, Cassidy replaces the 1995 warehouse grunge with the crystalline, zero-gravity shimmer the lyrics always envisioned—a pure, unencumbered orbit where nothing pulls the family down.
