**LORE NOTE:**
Originally a heavy, foot-stomping industrial-rock declaration of survival on 1997's *Hard Reset* (*"A body in motion stays in motion / Across the desert, across the ocean"*), *"Inertia: Unstoppable"* is reimagined through the lens of Newtonian physics in deep space. In a frictionless vacuum, momentum requires no brute force to sustain itself—Tyler Wright's precision 4/4 gated snare and Evan's gliding fretless bass propel Ryan and Cassidy forward into the stars.
