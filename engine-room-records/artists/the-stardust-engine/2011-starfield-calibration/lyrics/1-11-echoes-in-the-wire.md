**LORE NOTE:**
Originally introduced on 1997's *Hard Reset* to permanently replace 1989's *"If You Walked Away"*—a forced Apex Records breakup ballad whose abandonment scenario caused Cassidy (who is autistic) severe backstage distress after every concert, requiring her brother and safe person Ryan to hold her tightly in twenty-minute deep-pressure grounding hugs. *"Echoes in the Wire"* is the story of Cassidy finding comfort, quiet, and safety when Ryan is hugging her tightly as the stage noise fades away (*"All the terrestrial static faded out / Leaving nothing of the doubt... We traded time for something real / A tidal lock that you can feel"*). In this 2011 **Cosmic Tidal Lock Mix**, Cassidy wraps that grounding embrace in warm, zero-gravity analog synthesizer pads and a soothing 96 BPM heartbeat.
