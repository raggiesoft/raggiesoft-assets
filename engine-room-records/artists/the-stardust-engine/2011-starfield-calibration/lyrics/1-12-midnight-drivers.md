**LORE NOTE:**
On 2001's *Midnight Drivers*, the title track captured the claustrophobic, nocturnal solitude of interstate touring aboard the family's bus at 3:00 AM (*"Passing through the towns that never wake / Everything we love is at stake"*). Recalibrated in **The Cosmic Tidal Lock Sound™**, the interstate highway lifts off the asphalt and becomes an orbital trajectory through the Milky Way, with the green dashboard glow morphing into starlight on the cockpit glass.
