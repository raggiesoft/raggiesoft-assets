**LORE NOTE:**
Originally the closing beacon of hope on 2001's darkwave-tinged *Midnight Drivers*, *"The Horizon Line"* serves as a glowing beacon across the Burnout Hiatus, promising Forger Nation that the longest night is nearly over. Ryan and Cassidy's duet gazes out past the curvature of the Earth as the first golden band of sunrise crests over the atmosphere, framed by sweeping spatial reverb and soaring analog lead lines.
