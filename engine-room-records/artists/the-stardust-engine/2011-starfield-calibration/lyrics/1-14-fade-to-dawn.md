**LORE NOTE:**
First recorded in 1991 for the ill-fated *Friction* sessions and later rescued from the Apex vault for *Lost Sounds*, *"Fade to Dawn"* was the quiet morning-after truce written by Cassidy after the band's most brutal studio battles (*"We survived the midnight, we survived the storm"*). Serving as the grand finale of 2011's *Starfield Calibration*, Cassidy's solo vocal brings the planetarium dome lights slowly back up to a warm golden sunrise—reassuring Forger Nation from their Blacksburg sanctuary that The Stardust Engine is still alive and in orbit.
