**LORE NOTE:**
Written and recorded in a burst of autumn excitement in October 2015 immediately after **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)** traveled down to Norfolk for **Virginia Dominion University (VDU)**'s Homecoming game. After VDU students printed thousands of mocking *"38–0"* t-shirts commemorating their 2014 upset, the Forgers walked into Norfolk and delivered a triumphant **52–0 shutout victory**. Featuring Tyler's thunderous marching snare cadence and a roaring stadium chorus, it became an instant autumn anthem across Blacksburg.
