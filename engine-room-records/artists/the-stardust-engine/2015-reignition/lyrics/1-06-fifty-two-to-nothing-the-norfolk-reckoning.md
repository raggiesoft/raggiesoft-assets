**LORE NOTE:**
Written and recorded in a white-hot burst of inspiration in October 2015 immediately after **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)** traveled down to Norfolk for **Virginia Dominion University (VDU)**'s Homecoming game. Intoxicated by their 2014 upset in Blacksburg, VDU students had printed thousands of mocking *"38–0"* t-shirts—only for the Forgers to walk into Norfolk and deliver a merciless, retaliatory **52–0 shutout blowout** on VDU's own turf. Featuring Tyler's thunderous marching snare cadence and a roaring stadium chorus, it became an instant autumn anthem across Blacksburg.
