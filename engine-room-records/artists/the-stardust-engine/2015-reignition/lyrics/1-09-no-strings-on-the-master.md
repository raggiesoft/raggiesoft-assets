**LORE NOTE:**
A scorching 146 BPM 1980s hard-AOR guitar anthem celebrating thirty years of fiercely guarded creative independence. Ryan calls out the 1980s corporate label executives at Apex Records who once tried to reduce Cassidy to their "Stardust" and Ryan to their "Engine" as a cheap marketing gimmick, raising a glass to Holly's 1986 `$1.00` Cross-License and Cassidy's 1996 buyout that ensured the family owns every master tape in the vault.
