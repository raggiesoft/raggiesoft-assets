**LORE NOTE:**
The emotional heart of *Reignition*. Opening with just Cassidy's acoustic grand piano and Ryan's weeping clean electric guitar before building into a monumental 78 BPM 1980s arena power ballad, the brother and sister sing to each other—and to every neurodivergent, overwhelmed, or healing listener in Forger Nation: *"When the static hits a hundred and the room begins to spin / I am still your safe person, let the quiet in."*
