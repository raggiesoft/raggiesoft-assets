**LORE NOTE:**
The triumphant closing anthem of *Reignition*, which served as The Stardust Engine's official October 2015 announcement that they would return home to Blacksburg on Saturday, May 21, 2016 to play a 30th-Anniversary homecoming concert inside **The Crucible** with all 300 members of the **CPI Marching Forgers**. Bridging thirty years from their 1986 student contest tape (*"Ignition: The Forger's Call"*) to the 2016 stadium stage, the track ends with the roar of 65,000 fans chanting *"LET'S GO FORGERS!"*—leading directly into 2016's *Live at the Crucible*.
