**LORE NOTE:**
The sweeping, horizon-bound 134 BPM 1980s AOR and cosmic stadium-rock finale of *Reignition*. Looking up at the autumn constellations above the Blue Ridge after a ten-year hiatus, Ryan and Cassidy celebrate the enduring bond of their five-person family—knowing that time has not dimmed the spark they lit in 1986 and that the open highway ahead is still waiting where the stars still burn.
