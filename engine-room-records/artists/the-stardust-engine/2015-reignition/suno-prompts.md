## I. Canonical Lore & Narrative Arc for *Reignition* (2015)

1. **Ending the 2005–2014 Burnout Hiatus:**
   - Between 2001 and 2004, the band recorded four full studio albums (*Midnight Drivers*, *Moon 1: Sanctuary [Zero-G]*, *Moon 2: Mile Marker 98*, and *Progress Street*) and toured relentlessly through 2004 (*Live in Norfolk*), leaving all five family members exhausted.
   - At the end of 2004's *Progress Street*, Track 11 ([*"Last Bus Home: Lights Out on Progress Street"*](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2004-progress-street?track=1-11-last-bus-home-lights-out-on-progress-street)) ended with Cassidy whispering *"Lights out on Progress Street..."* and Ryan saying *"Goodnight, Forger Nation,"* promising fans that once they had rested and recharged, they would turn the switches back on.
   - After a decade of rest (punctuated only by 2007's archival *Lost Sounds*, 2008's quiet acoustic *Our Own Frequency*, and Cassidy's 2011 silver-anniversary studio project *Starfield Calibration*), the family is completely recharged and returns to full-throttle 1980s studio and stadium performance.
2. **The August–October 2015 Context (The Delaware Migration & CPI's 52–0 Norfolk Reckoning):**
   - In August 2015, younger sister and CEO **Holly O'Connell** passed the Delaware Bar and executed **The Delaware Migration**—folding *Engine Room Records, LLC*, *Artists First Management, LLC*, *Stardust Properties, LLC*, and *Pacific Rim Properties, LLC* into anonymous Delaware entities beneath **The O'Connell Family Revocable Trust** ($2.4B+ AUM).
   - That same autumn (October 2015), **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)** Football traveled to Norfolk and delivered a merciless **52–0 retaliatory shutout** against **Virginia Dominion University (VDU)** on VDU's own Homecoming, avenging the 2014 `38–0` loss and igniting fever-pitch excitement across Blacksburg as The Stardust Engine dropped *Reignition* and announced their 30th-Anniversary concert inside **The Crucible** for May 21, 2016.
