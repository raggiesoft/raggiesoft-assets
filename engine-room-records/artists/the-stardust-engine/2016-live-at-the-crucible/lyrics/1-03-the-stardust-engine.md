**LORE NOTE:**
While standard Stardust Engine concerts open directly with **"The Stardust Engine"**, here at their alma mater it serves as the culminating third movement of the opening sequence—performed with all 300 members of the **CPI Marching Forgers** still on the stage! Ever since December 12, 1985 in the Progress Street NE basement, the heart of the song has been Cassidy's chorus vocal call answered immediately by her synthesizer's staccato brass leitmotif response (*Ba-da-DA-da-DAAA!*). Every student in the **CPI Marching Forgers** knows what that leitmotif means to Cassidy—they know it is her pride and joy, and they know how it broke her heart when Apex Records muted her synthesizer response out of the chorus on 1990's *Live in Chicago*. Here inside **The Crucible** (`01:44–01:47`, `02:02–02:06`, `02:42–02:45`, `04:00–04:03`), when Cassidy belts *"We're burning on the fuel of a brand new light!"* and *"Turning our future on, shining so bright!"* and sends up her soaring vocal call, **the entire CPI Marching Forgers trumpet and horn section blasts that leitmotif response in thunderous unison with Cassidy's Vektron Z-1 synthesizer**—CPI promising Cassidy that they have her back for this song, and that her vocal call will never go unanswered again.
