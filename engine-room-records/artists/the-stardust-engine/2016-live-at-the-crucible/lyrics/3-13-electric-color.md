**LORE NOTE:**
The secret 5-minute-26-second Grand Finale Encore of the May 21, 2016 homecoming concert inside **The Crucible**—and the very first time in the band's 30-year history that **"Electric Color"** was ever performed as the blistering **1980s Album-Oriented Rock (Stadium Rock)** anthem Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler originally heard in their heads before Apex Records forced them to release it as a glossy 1987 Dance Synth-Pop single.

Protected prior to concert night by Holly O'Connell's **$300,000 collective student escrow ("The Forger Pot")** under Addendum No. 4 to her 1986 Cross-License—and disguised in the **CPI Marching Forgers**' rehearsal folders as *"Exercise 87-B: The Anvil Finale"* with the vocal melody stripped out—the band first staged **"Last Bus Home: Lights Out on Progress Street"** as a false ending, plunging the stadium into darkness so the audience believed the concert was over. Then, in the pitch black, all 300 members of the Marching Forgers marched back onto the stage to join the band (just as they had in the four-song opening act) for the actual ending. For the first **1 minute and 40 seconds**—through Cassidy's spoken greeting over a lone piano chord (*"Blacksburg! We've been all over the world..."*), the Kevlar snare roll-off, and a massive 77-second AOR stadium guitar and 300-horn brass overture (`0:23–1:40`)—not a single fan in the 65,000-seat stadium knew what song the band was playing, until **`1:40`** on the dot when the instruments dropped to a lone heavy kick drum and Cassidy sang the opening words of Verse 1: *"Woke up this morning, the world was gray..."*
