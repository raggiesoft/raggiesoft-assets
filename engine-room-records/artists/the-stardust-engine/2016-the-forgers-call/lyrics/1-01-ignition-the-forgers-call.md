**LORE NOTE:**
The official **October 1986 Crucible Stadium Master** played over the PA system at **The Crucible** during every CPI Forgers home football game as the football team runs out of the tunnel onto the field—released on an album for the very first time on October 15, 2016 alongside *Live at the Crucible* (both inside the *Live at the Crucible* 30th-Anniversary Box Set at The Foundry and as a standalone companion release). After `Demo Take 3` (Track 2) won the October 1986 CPI Student Government Association anthem contest—and after 16-year-old dual-enrolled **Holly O'Connell** executed the **`$1.00` Perpetual Mutual Cross-License** ("The Progress Street Redline") with CPI's Office of University Counsel—Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler tracked this finished **`82 BPM`** stadium master specifically for the press-box audio booth at The Crucible. Replacing the cassette demo's vocalized `(TING... BOOM!)` placeholders with an actual steel foundry anvil strike and Tyler Wright's chest-rattling kick-drum impact, this is the exact recording to which the student section in **The Anvil** organically crafted **The Stomp** on the heavy kick-drum hits over several successive plays.
