**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **September 1988** in Blacksburg between *Electric Color* (1987) and *Neon Hearts* (1989), ***"This Side of Heaven"*** (`1988 Blacksburg Campus Cut`) was written by Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell during autumn move-in week at **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)**. Watching hundreds of eighteen-year-old freshmen carrying cardboard boxes up the steps of **Bennett Hall** and **Kincaid Hall** into the crisp Blue Ridge sunlight—many of them escaping difficult or suffocating childhood homes for the very first time—Ryan and Cassidy crafted an unapologetically joyful, sunlit **`126 BPM`** late-1980s synth-rock and stadium-guitar anthem about stepping out of the shadows, leaving the dark behind forever, and waking up safe in a brand-new world surrounded by chosen family.

Because the band refused to let **Apex Records** twist a deeply personal song about Blacksburg sanctuary into a disposable corporate single, **Holly O'Connell** kept the master tape out of the label's hands under her 1986 CPI cross-license and distributed it on campus cassettes through the **CPI Campus Bookstore** and student radio. For nearly three decades, *"This Side of Heaven"* has served as the unofficial move-in anthem of CPI—and in **January 2015** (*The Eleventh Child: The Story of Jordan*, Episode 3, Chapter 1), hearing this song while unpacking his maroon-and-orange CPI shirts in **Bennett Hall Suite 214/216** sends **Jordan Fuller** into an overwhelming meltdown of pure joy (`Emotional Overflow: 100`) as it hits him all at once that his mother's house is in the past, he is **SAFE**, and being back at CPI with the people who genuinely love him truly is *This Side of Heaven*.
