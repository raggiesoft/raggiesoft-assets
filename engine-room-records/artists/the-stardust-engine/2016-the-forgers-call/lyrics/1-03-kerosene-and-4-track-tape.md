**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **late October 1986** inside the unheated two-car garage on **Progress Street** in Blacksburg, Virginia, ***"Kerosene & 4-Track Tape"*** (`1986 Garage Outtake`) was tracked on the B-side of the exact same Maxell XLII-S 90 cassette tape that held `Demo Take 3` of *"Ignition: The Forger's Call"*. With October mountain temperatures dropping into the thirties outside and only a portable cylindrical kerosene heater glowing orange in the corner of the concrete garage, 22-year-old **Ryan O'Connell**, 20-year-old **Cassidy O'Connell**, and 19-year-old identical twins **Evan and Tyler Wright** tore through this raw, high-energy **`138 BPM`** garage rocker to warm up their fingers while 16-year-old **Holly O'Connell** rode the faders on their four-track Tascam Portastudio.

Never submitted to **Apex Records**—who would have balked at the unvarnished tube-amp grit and tape saturation—this 1986 time capsule captures the unfiltered joy of five broke college kids building a life and a sound together on Progress Street before the industry ever knew their names.
