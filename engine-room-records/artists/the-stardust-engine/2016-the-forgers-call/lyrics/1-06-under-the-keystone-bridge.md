**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **November 1991** at the Progress Street house in Blacksburg, ***"Under the Keystone Bridge"*** (`1991 Blacksburg B-Side`) was the very first song **Ryan O'Connell** wrote and recorded after returning home to Blacksburg as a T10 paraplegic manual wheelchair user following the December 10, 1990 I-81 tour bus crash and his long months of inpatient spinal cord rehabilitation.

For weeks after coming home, Ryan was hesitant to roll across the hilly CPI campus where he had once walked and run as an undergraduate. Then, on a crisp November afternoon, **Cassidy**, **Holly**, **Evan**, and **Tyler** walked beside his custom manual wheelchair up **Commonwealth Mall** and paused beneath the soaring gothic **Ironstone** arches of **The Keystone Bridge**—the iconic campus skybridge spanning the mall between **Commonwealth Green** and the main gate. Looking out through the stone arches at the campus that still welcomed him home without pity or hesitation, Ryan went back to Progress Street that night and tracked this driving **`124 BPM`** heartland synth-rock anthem of resilience, dignity, and belonging.
