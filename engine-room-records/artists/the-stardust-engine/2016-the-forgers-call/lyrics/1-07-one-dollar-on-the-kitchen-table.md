**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **July 1992** in the living room on **Progress Street** on the exact evening 22-year-old **Holly O'Connell** filed the articles of incorporation for ***Engine Room Records, LLC*** in preparation for the band's impending war of independence against Apex Records, ***"One Dollar on the Kitchen Table"*** (`1992 Founding Jam`) is Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler's joyous **`132 BPM`** melodic arena-rock tribute to their youngest sister.

Six years earlier, in October 1986, sixteen-year-old dual-enrolled CPI freshman Holly O'Connell had sat at the Progress Street kitchen table with a red ballpoint pen, crossed out the university's boilerplate *"Work Made for Hire"* clause on the student stadium anthem contest entry form, and negotiated the historic **`$1.00` Perpetual Mutual Cross-License** ("The Progress Street Redline") with CPI's Office of University Counsel. That crisp one-dollar bill—still framed beside the kitchen table—kept 100% of *"Ignition: The Forger's Call"* out of Apex Records' catalog and became the legal cornerstone upon which Engine Room Records was built.
