**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **September 1998** in Blacksburg during a brief rest between legs of the *Hard Reset* World Tour, ***"The Old Lady on the Hill"*** (`1998 Bennett Hall Acoustic Cut`) is a warm **`112 BPM`** heartland acoustic-rock tribute to **Bennett Hall**—Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute's oldest surviving red-brick academic building (constructed in **1882** on the highest knoll overlooking the upper quad) and affectionately known to every generation of CPI students and alumni as ***"The Old Lady on the Hill"***.

Before the campus expanded into the gothic **Ironstone** quadrangles of **Commonwealth Green**, Bennett Hall housed CPI's original classrooms, laboratories, and dormitories. It was inside Bennett Hall's creaking heart-pine lecture rooms that Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell took their freshman humanities seminars in 1985—and twenty-nine years later, in 2014–2015, its renovated lower residential wing (`Bennett Hall Suite 214/216`) would become the beloved freshman sanctuary of **Jordan Fuller**, **Aubrie Jean Sinclair**, **Mark**, **Madison**, **Zoë**, **Lauren**, **Hannah**, **Sarah**, **Jessica**, and **RA Isabel**.
