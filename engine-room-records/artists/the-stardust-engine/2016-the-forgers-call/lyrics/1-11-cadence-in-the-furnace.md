**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **February 2000** for CPI Athletics to blast inside **The Furnace**—Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute's 10,000-seat parabolic-arch basketball and convocation coliseum located right across the plaza from **The Crucible**—***"Cadence in The Furnace"*** (`2000 Drumline & Guitar Studio Cut`) is a high-voltage **`120 BPM`** percussion and electric-guitar instrumental showcase led by **Tyler Wright** and **Evan Wright**.

Because **The Furnace** was engineered with a steep, windowless poured-concrete parabolic ceiling that reflects crowd and percussion transients directly back onto the hardwood court, Tyler invited eight student snare and tenors players from **The Engine Room** (the championship drumline of the **CPI Marching Forgers**) into the studio to trade blistering Kevlar marching-snare cadences with his acoustic drum kit, Evan's syncopated slap-and-fingerstyle bass, Ryan's razor-sharp rhythm/lead guitar, and Cassidy's clavinet-and-brass synth stabs.
