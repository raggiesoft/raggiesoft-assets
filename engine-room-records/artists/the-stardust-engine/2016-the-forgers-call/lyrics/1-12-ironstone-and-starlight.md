**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **August 2004** at the **Progress Street** house studio in Blacksburg during the sessions for *Progress Street* (2004), ***"Ironstone & Starlight"*** (`2004 Progress Street Outtake`) was mixed and mastered alongside the rest of the album, but held back in the vault only because the band felt *Progress Street* needed to end directly at Track 13 with *"Last Bus Home: Lights Out on Progress Street"*.

Written at a majestic **`118 BPM`** in the band's classic 1980s AOR and melodic synth-rock style, the duet brings together the two halves of The Stardust Engine's identity: the terrestrial permanence of **Ironstone**—the rough-hewn gray-and-pink dolomitic limestone quarried from the campus quarry to build **Preston Hall**, **Pendleton Library**, **The Keystone Bridge**, and **The Crucible**—and the weightless **Starlight** of the cosmos above the New River Valley.
