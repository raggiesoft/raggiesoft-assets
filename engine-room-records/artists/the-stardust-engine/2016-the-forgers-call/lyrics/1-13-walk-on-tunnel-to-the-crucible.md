**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in **August 2006** in the Progress Street studio—two years into the band's ten-year Burnout Hiatus (2004–2014)—***"Walk-On: Tunnel to the Crucible"*** (`2006 Athletics Vault Cut`) was recorded quietly by Ryan, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler as a 20th-Anniversary gift to the **CPI Athletic Department** and the football coaching staff.

Never released commercially during the hiatus—and played exclusively inside the CPI team locker room and over the tunnel speakers thirty minutes before kickoff—this blistering **`142 BPM`** hard-rock charger honors the long tradition of unrecruited, zero-star CPI walk-on players who came to Blacksburg with no scholarship and no hype, earned their jersey through sheer polytechnic grit, and charged down the concrete tunnel into the roar of 65,000 fans inside **The Crucible**.
