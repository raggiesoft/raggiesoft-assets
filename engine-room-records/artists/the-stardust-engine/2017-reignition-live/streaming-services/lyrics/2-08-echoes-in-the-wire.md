**LORE NOTE:**
Permanently holding the emotional power-ballad slot in place of the retired 1989 ballad *'If You Walked Away'*, *'Echoes in the Wire'* fills the dark indoor arena with 20,000 swaying lights and Ryan's weeping, delay-soaked guitar solo.
