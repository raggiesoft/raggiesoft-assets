**LORE NOTE:**
Recorded in an out-of-state arena midway through the 2015–2016 Reignition World Tour, this performance captures the exact inverse of the legendary May 21, 2016 homecoming version on Live at the Crucible. Thousands of miles away from the New River Valley, 99% of the 20,000 concertgoers in the building have no idea what a "Huckleberry Train" or "Price Mountain" is. Knowing this, Ryan and Cassidy politely ask the arena to indulge them for five minutes so the five of them can play a quiet slice of home. While the main floor listens in warm, respectful stillness, the moment Ryan strikes the opening 12-string arpeggio and Cassidy mentions the six miles of asphalt from the library lawn, you can hear isolated pockets of homesick CPI alumni and Montgomery County expats up in the nosebleed sections losing their minds.
