After an A-Side full of studio-mandated synth-pop, Apex allowed this "one-two punch" of pure rock (paired with the following track, "Not Your Doll") as a concession. It became an immediate fan favorite, proving that the "Engine" sound was what the audience actually wanted.

## Light Up the Night
### Lore
Inserted right after their breakout hit *Electric Color*, this track is a monument to corporate miscalculation. *Light Up the Night* was the song Apex Records poured *all* of their money into. The executives were convinced that this highly polished, aggressively safe synth-pop track would be "The Big One" that cemented The Stardust Engine as global pop idols. 

It wasn't. Despite a massive marketing campaign, the sterile, manufactured sound failed to connect with audiences. ## Escape Velocity (Ad Astra)
### Lore
The "Magnum Opus" and the grand finale of the *Hard Reset* double album. This monumental 15-minute and 33-second progressive rock epic was originally written and recorded in 1995, during the financial struggle of the band's "Wilderness Years" (a full year before the 1996 lottery windfall).
