# Engine Room Records — Narrative & Broadcast Soundtrack Vault

This archival vault houses standalone character themes, narrative singles, and in-universe broadcast television bumpers rescued from the Suno commercial production sessions.

---

## 1. Narrative Character Singles

### `rue-church.wav` (`3:50`)
* **Title:** Rue Church
* **Narrative Tie-In:** *Beyond Words (Alex & Chloe)* — [Book 3, Chapter 3, Scene 7](../../../raggiesoft-narratives/books/alex-chloe/e003/c003/s007.md) & [Book 10, Chapter 3, Scene 7](../../../raggiesoft-narratives/books/alex-chloe/e010/c003/s007.md)
* **Genre:** Heartland Roots Rock / Americana
* **Lore:** Sung from Chloe's perspective about arriving early in the April rain in Newport, Vermont on April 8th—Alex's birthday—and growing up on `Rue Church` straddling the Stanstead, Québec / Derby Line, Vermont international border beside the Stanstead-Derby Line Free Library and Opera House (*"A foot in Québec, a foot in Vermont state / Living my life on the edge of a garden gate"*).

### `marcus-sterling-in-control.wav` (`2:26`)
* **Title:** Marcus Sterling — In Control
* **Narrative Tie-In:** *The Rachel Narrative* — [Book 16, Chapter 3](../../../raggiesoft-narratives/books/rachel/e016/c003/s003.md) & *The Caretakers (Casey)*
* **Genre:** Heavy Arena Metal / Pro-Wrestling Heel Authority Entrance Theme
* **Lore:** The official entrance theme for AWF/AWE General Manager **Marcus Sterling** (in-universe counterpart to Eric Bischoff), blaring over the arena PA as he steps onto the entrance ramp to enforce corporate synergy and the bottom line (*"I'm in control! I own the board, I own the ring! Welcome to the bottom line where every single cent is mine!"*).

## 2. In-Universe Broadcast Bumpers (APEX Network)

### `apex-world-premier-presentation-bumper.wav` (`3:33`) & `apex-world-premier-presentation-bumper-extended-8m.wav` (`7:59`)
* **Title:** APEX World Premier Presentation Bumper
* **Narrative Tie-In:** *Pebble & Borrow* — [Book 2, Chapter 2, Scene 2: The APEX World Premier Presentation](../../../raggiesoft-narratives/books/pebble-borrow/e002/c002/s002.md)
* **Genre:** 1980s Synth-Orchestral Broadcast Fanfare
* **Lore:** The iconic 1980s feature presentation intro for the **APEX** premium cable television network (in-universe counterpart to HBO's *Feature Presentation* bumper), featuring a booming analog synth pulse, rising neon cityscape, and brass fanfare as lasers etch `"WORLD PREMIER PRESENTATION"` across the screen. Includes both the standard `3:33` broadcast suite and the `7:59` extended symphonic suite.

### `apex-fantasy-adventure-bumper.wav` (`3:05`)
* **Title:** APEX Fantasy Adventure Bumper
* **Genre:** 1980s Synth-Brass & Orchestral Adventure Fanfare
* **Lore:** Network feature bumper used by APEX for family fantasy-adventure broadcasts.

### `apex-high-fantasy-bumper.wav` (`3:12`)
* **Title:** APEX High Fantasy Bumper
* **Genre:** Grand Choral & Symphonic High-Fantasy Overture
* **Lore:** Epic choral and orchestral feature presentation bumper used by APEX for sword-and-sorcery and high-fantasy premieres (such as *The Silver Gauntlet of Aethel*).
