**LORE NOTE:**
Sung from Chloe's perspective in *Beyond Words (Alex & Chloe)* about arriving early in the April rain in Newport, Vermont on April 8th—Alex's birthday—and growing up on `Rue Church` straddling the Stanstead, Québec / Derby Line, Vermont international border beside the Stanstead-Derby Line Free Library and Opera House.

**LYRICS:**

[Intro]
(Warm heartland electric guitar lead over steady acoustic strumming and drums)

But I heard a different song to sing
