# Cover Letter: NuAxis Innovations - Mid-Level QA/508 Tester

**To the Hiring Team at NuAxis Innovations,**

As an Accessibility Engineer with a deep background in full-stack web development and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, I was thrilled to see the Mid-Level QA/508 Tester opening at NuAxis. Your mission to put people at the center of federal IT solutions resonates deeply with me, as I have spent my career ensuring that digital platforms are genuinely usable for everyone, not just "technically compliant" on paper.

During my time at Old Dominion University (ODU Online), I served as the primary technical gatekeeper for Section 508 compliance during a massive university-wide migration to the Canvas LMS. While we utilized tools like Deque axe for baseline scanning, I quickly learned what I consider the most important rule of accessibility engineering: automated scanners only catch about 30% of actual violations. A tool like axe will catch a missing alt attribute, but it takes rigorous manual testing—navigating with NVDA, verifying focus traps in custom JavaScript modals, and auditing logical reading orders—to actually guarantee a usable experience for assistive technology users. 

My background as a software architect (building custom PHP/Vanilla JS MVC frameworks) gives me a unique advantage in QA. When I identify a defect, I don't just report that it's broken; I understand *why* the underlying DOM, JavaScript event, or ARIA state failed, which allows me to provide highly actionable remediation guidance to the development team. 

Furthermore, I am highly motivated to formalize my manual testing expertise by obtaining the DHS Trusted Tester Certification within the first 90 days of employment. Having previously held a Public Trust clearance (for a federal contractor role with HHS), I am well-prepared for the federal onboarding process and background investigation.

I am excited about the opportunity to bring my passion for rigorous, human-centered accessibility testing to NuAxis. Thank you for your time and consideration, and I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills align with your team's goals.
