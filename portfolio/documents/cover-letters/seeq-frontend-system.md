I thrive in the space between design and engineering—zooming in to perfect the interaction state of a complex data table, and zooming out to ensure that the broader dashboard template supports the user's ultimate goal of analyzing and triaging information. 

I would love to bring my deep experience in frontend foundations, Tailwind CSS, and accessibility to Seeq to help evolve a design system that both your users and your engineers love. Thank you for your time and consideration.
