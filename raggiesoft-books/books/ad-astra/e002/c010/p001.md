At **`HC 00:45`** on the morning of their fourth forty-eight-hour orbit on Caelum-IV—after a deep recovery sleep following their all-night [***Starfield Calibration***](https://music.raggiesoft.com/the-stardust-engine/2011-starfield-calibration) recording session in the Mother-Grove—**Ryan O'Connell** woke up in the center of **King Bed 1** inside the **Family Stateroom** and immediately noticed something missing.

On his right side, **Cassidy** was still curled warmly against his ribs under the down comforter. Across the three-foot carpeted aisle in **King Bed 2**, **Tyler and Evan** were just beginning to stir as the warm amber morning light of **Solis-V** filtered through the overhead viewport.

But on Ryan's left side—where **Holly** always slept as his port-side human bed rail—the mattress was already neatly smoothed and empty, with a fresh thermal carafe of water sitting on the nightstand beside his locked manual wheelchair.

Fifteen minutes later, after Cassidy helped Ryan through his morning **Protocol T-10** routine and the four of them got dressed in their slate-blue and charcoal survey tunics, Ryan propelled his wheelchair down the central corridor toward the aroma of fresh Kona coffee—while Cassidy walked beside his left wheel with her right hand resting on his rubberized rear **push-handle** like a rolling walker to steady her morning cerebral palsy gait.

When they rounded the bulkhead into the **Mission Operations & Telemetry Alcove**, they found their twenty-seven-year-old youngest sister already two hours into her workday.

Holly had her brown hair tied back in her signature no-nonsense ponytail, her glasses perched on her nose, and three holographic diagnostic displays glowing above the starboard console while she cross-checked a multi-spectral topographical map on her **MagnaPad**.

Ryan braked his wheels beside the console, looked at Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler, and couldn't help laughing out loud.

"Look at her," Ryan said to the other three. "Back on Earth at **The Fortress** and on **Progress Street NE**, she gets up two hours before the band to triage the lawyers, advance the stadium ramps, audit the master tapes, and guard our routines. Now we're twelve hundred light-years from Blacksburg on an alien exoplanet—and the four of us are *still* sleeping in while our little sister advances the entire planet by herself."

Holly looked up from her MagnaPad, her cheeks flushing slightly behind her glasses. "I just wanted to get the **Mother-Grove** master stems backed up and run the morning atmospheric and seismic sweep before you guys woke up!"

"Put the stylus down for a second, Counselor," Ryan grinned, locking both wheelchair brakes. "Today, the guitars, basses, drums, and synthesizers are staying in their racks. All four of us are job-shadowing **Mission Operations** for the entire sixteen-hour shift."

## Phase 1 (`HC 01:00 – HC 04:00`): Shipboard Triage & The First-Humans Realization

Pulling three swivel stools up beside Ryan's wheelchair around the Mission Operations station, Cassidy, Evan, and Tyler leaned in with their coffee mugs as Holly walked them through the morning checklist she ran every single orbit before anyone was allowed to unseal the aft cargo hatch.

"All right," Holly said, tapping the primary status board. "If you four want to see what keeps the *U.S.S. Aethelgard* running as our home away from home, every morning starts with **Four-Pillar Shipboard Triage**."

> **U.S.S. AETHELGARD (`ERR-NCC-1996`) // DAILY MISSION OPS & BAND MANAGER LOG**<br>
> **Duty Officer:** **Holly O'Connell, J.D.** (`Chief of Mission Operations / General Counsel & Manager`)<br>
> **1. Master Vault & Archival Redundancy (`Engine Room Protocol`):** Verify bit-for-bit checksums on all 32-channel `96kHz / 24-bit` digital stems from **`STARFIELD-CALIBRATION-TIDAL-LOCK`** (`Tracks 1-01` through `1-14`) across three optically isolated crystal arrays (`Bridge Core`, `Studio Vault`, and `Portable Black-Box Beacon`).<br>
> **2. Life Support, Power Bus & `0.88G` Deck Harmonizers:** Verify solar-inductive charge from **Solis-V** (`100.0%`), atmospheric scrubber equilibrium, and `72.0°F` thermal stability in the Family Stateroom and Locker Bay.<br>
> **3. Pre-Exit Environmental & Weather Advance:** Run mass-spectrometer air check (`21.4% O2`, `Zero Spores/Toxins`), monitor Vesperan ring-shadow barometric fronts (`Next 82.0°F Warm Rain Window: HC 19:30`), and calculate shepherd-moon **Lumen/Umbra** tidal tables.<br>
> **4. All-Band Hailing & Orbital Lidar Audit (`120.0 BPM` Beacon):** Audit 48-hour automated transceiver logs across VHF, UHF, S-Band, and Subspace Continuum channels.

Ryan pointed to Item 4 on the console screen, where the automated hailing counter had just ticked past **`192.0 Consecutive Earth Hours`** (`Four 48-Hour Vesperan Orbits`) with **`0.000 External Human Responses`**.

"Show us the orbital synthetic-aperture radar composite you've been compiling since touchdown, Holls," Ryan said quietly.

Holly tapped the center monitor, bringing up a high-resolution, three-dimensional globe of **Caelum-IV** compiled by the *Aethelgard*'s passive orbital sensors prior to atmospheric entry and cross-referenced against seven days of full-spectrum radio listening.

Across every amethyst ocean basin, every black-basalt archipelago, and every lavender bio-silica continent on the planet, the artificial thermal, metallurgical, and electromagnetic emission counter read a flat **`0.000`**.

"When we dropped out of the Continuum on `Stardate 49601.0`," Holly explained softly, looking around at her brother, sister, and cousins, "I kept the automated hailing beacon running on every standard human frequency because standard aerospace protocol assumes a habitable world might already have an automated survey buoy or a deep-space listening post. It took four full 48-hour orbits for the passive global spectrum sweep to finish eliminating every blind spot on the far hemisphere."

She zoomed out on the holographic globe until the five green crew biometric dots inside the *U.S.S. Aethelgard* on the Sanctuary Peninsula were the only human markers on the entire world.

"There are no survey buoys, no mining outposts, and no colonial transmitters anywhere on Caelum-IV," Holly said softly. "That's why the hailing frequencies only ever echoed back the planet's `120.0 BPM` mycelial pulse. Guys... **we are the very first human beings ever to set foot on this world.**"

For a long moment, the alcove was completely silent except for the gentle hum of the ship's life-support fans.

Cassidy reached out and rested her hand on Ryan's shoulder, her eyes wide with pure, unguarded wonder behind her glasses.

"No Apex Records," Cassidy whispered. "No fences, no parking meters, and no conquerors. Just the five of us and the singing forest."

"And because we're the first humans here," Holly added firmly, tapping the environmental protocol binder on her knee, "we don't leave a single footprint without advancing the ground first, and we don't take a single thing for granted. Which brings us to Phase 2 of my morning: **Advancing the Venue**."

## Phase 2 (`HC 04:00 – HC 08:00`): Advancing an Alien Ridge & "Scan First, Trust Second"

Back on Earth, whenever **The Stardust Engine** was booked to play a new stadium or amphitheater, Holly never let Ryan's wheelchair or Cassidy's sneakers touch the venue floor until she had personally walked the ramp with a spirit level, a tape measure, and her 14-page accessibility rider.

Watching her work outside on Caelum-IV at **`HC 04:00`**, Ryan realized his little sister was applying the **exact same 14-page Band Manager Rider** to an alien planet.

Their destination that morning was an unexplored inland plateau half a mile north of the ship that Holly had flagged on orbital lidar: **The Prism-Moss Terrace**, a sunlit basalt overlook hanging two hundred feet above the purple bay.

Normally, Evan and Tyler would have trotted ahead up the trail while Ryan and Cassidy rolled behind them talking about chord progressions. Today, because they were job-shadowing Holly, all four musicians walked and rolled strictly at Holly's elbow, watching every step of her field procedure:

1. **Subsurface Crust & Grade Verification (`The 1:20 Basalt Rider`):**
   - Before letting Ryan propel his wheelchair onto a new branch of the black pahoehoe lava causeway, Holly walked twenty paces ahead carrying a handheld **ultrasonic ground-penetrating radar wand**.
   - "Volcanic pahoehoe basalt looks solid on top, but sometimes cooling gas bubbles leave a thin, hollow 'lava blister' two inches beneath the crust," Holly explained, showing Ryan and Cassidy the density waveform on her MagnaPad (holding the tablet angled toward her own eyes as they looked over her shoulder). "If a 180-pound guy in a titanium wheelchair rolls over a hollow half-inch basalt blister, the crust cracks and drops your front caster into a trench. Look here—solid three-meter basalt slab, zero voids, and a steady `3.6%` (`1:28`) incline. Path is cleared for rolling."
2. **Full Biochemical & Chirality Assay (`"Scan First, Trust Second"`):**
   - At the top of the **Prism-Moss Terrace**, where a cool freshwater spring bubbled out of the volcanic rock beside a grove of low, shimmering trees bearing translucent amber fruit that looked like glowing honeyed figs (**Vesperan Star-Figs**), Tyler reached out as if to pluck one.
   - "Ah-ah!" Holly tapped Tyler's wrist with her sterile sample forceps. "Rule One of Planetary Management: nothing touches a musician's mouth until Legal and Toxicology sign the release."
   - Sitting on a flat basalt boulder beside Ryan's chair, Holly ran the full twenty-minute field assay while the four of them watched every step:
     - **Spring Water Assay:** Testing pH (`7.38`), dissolved mineral electrolytes (calcium and magnesium bicarbonate), zero heavy metals, and zero microbial pathogens before logging **`SPRING-NORTH-02: CLEARED FOR DRINKING`**.
     - **Vesperan Star-Fig Assay:** Slicing a translucent amber fruit into the microfluidic bio-scanner to verify **right-handed `D-fructose / D-glucose` carbohydrate chirality** and **left-handed `L-amino` proteins** (ensuring human digestive enzymes could actually metabolize the fruit rather than rejecting mirror-image alien molecules), screening for zero neurotoxins, zero cardiac glycosides, and zero histamine allergens, and waiting for the solid green **`99.98% SAFE FOR HUMAN CONSUMPTION`** badge before handing the first sliced fig to Ryan and Cassidy.

It tasted like warm honeycomb, ripe pear, and cinnamon.

"Okay," Evan grinned, biting into a slice of Star-Fig as he looked out over the glittering purple ocean below the terrace. "I take back every joke I ever made about Holly's fourteen-page contract riders."

## Phase 3 (`HC 08:00 – HC 13:00`): Quartermaster, Hydroponics & The Silent Wrist-Link Timer

Returning to the *U.S.S. Aethelgard* for the midday shift, the four of them shadowed Holly down into the lower **Quartermaster, Medical & Hydroponics Bay**—the quiet engine room of their daily domestic life that Holly maintained while the rest of them were writing songs or swimming in the cove.

Ryan rolled down the wide, zero-threshold corridor beside her as Holly:
- Inventoried their climate-controlled **Protocol T-10 Medical Locker**, checking the sterile intermittent catheterization kits, skin-integrity barriers, and electrolyte packets;
- Checked the shipboard **Closed-Loop Hydroponic Garden** (where fresh terrestrial basil, cherry tomatoes, strawberries, and mint grew under full-spectrum LEDs alongside jars of dried **Vesperan Velvet-Pod** cocoa powder and **Myco-Root Nectar**);
- And balanced the ship's **48-Hour Circadian Lighting Schedule**, programming the interior cabin LEDs to shift gradually from `5600K` daylight during the twenty-four-hour **Solis-V** day to warm `2700K` amber during **Golden-Violet Ring-Night** so Ryan and Cassidy's autistic nervous systems never experienced abrupt sensory shock.

Just as Holly reached up to log the hydroponic moisture reading at **`HC 08:15`**, Ryan noticed the slim silver telemetry band on her left wrist give two tiny, completely silent haptic pulses—accompanied by a tiny amber icon that flashed:

> **`T-10 WEIGHT SHIFT (00:15:00)`**  
> **`NEXT T-10 ADL WINDOW: 01:45:00`**  
> **`SYZYGY TIDE TRACKER: +18h 12m`**

Ryan stopped his wheels in the center of the bay and looked at his sister's wrist.

"Holly," Ryan said softly, remembering the little black timer box Velcroed to the mixing console in Studio A back at The Fortress. "You brought the fifteen-minute and four-hour timers all the way to Kepler-442c."

Holly looked down at her wrist-link, a shy, crooked smile tugging at the corner of her mouth.

"Of course I did, Ry," she said quietly. "Out here on a forty-eight-hour orbit where the sun stands still for twenty-four hours and a glowing gas giant hangs in the sky all night, time feels completely different than it does in Virginia. When you and Cass were out on the **Glass Reef** during Syzygy or playing for twenty-two straight hours inside the **Mother-Grove Amphitheater**, the last thing in the universe I wanted was for either of you to have to stare at a stopwatch and feel like your body was a ticking clock. So I linked `Protocol T-10`, Cass's cerebral palsy rest intervals, and the moon's tide tables right into my wrist-link."

Beside Ryan's wheelchair, Cassidy didn't say a word—she simply stepped forward, wrapped both arms around her younger sister's neck, and hugged her tight.

***

## Phase 4 (`HC 14:30 – HC 16:00`): Putting the Mission Commander Off the Clock

At **`HC 14:30`**—ninety minutes before their first eight-hour sleep window of Orbit Four—Holly sat down at the galley table with her MagnaPad to draft the next day's geological survey log.

Just like on **Progress Street NE**, she never got to finish the sentence.

Evan gently slipped the MagnaPad out of her hands and placed it on the charging dock. Tyler set a steaming ceramic mug of **Sanctuary Hot Chocolate** on the table in front of her. Cassidy draped a soft maroon **CPI** fleece blanket around Holly's shoulders, and Ryan wheeled up beside her with his unplugged acoustic guitar resting across his lap.

"Hey—I still have to log the Prism-Moss soil core!" Holly protested, laughing as she sank back against the cushioned observation bench.

"Section 1A of the Big Brother Interstellar Rider," Ryan said firmly, striking a warm G-major chord on his acoustic guitar as Cassidy sat down at the portable electric piano beside him and Evan and Tyler picked up an acoustic bass and shaker. "Even the Mission Commander of Caelum-IV gets clocked out at `HC 14:30`."

As the golden afternoon light of Solis-V shimmered across the purple waves outside the viewport, the four of them played a gentle, four-part acoustic arrangement of [**"The Horizon Line"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/the-stardust-engine/2011-starfield-calibration?track=1-13-the-horizon-line) just for Holly—singing softly to the little sister who had carried them across courtrooms, highways, stadiums, and twelve hundred light-years of deep space without ever dropping a single stitch.

At **`HC 16:00`**, after Cassidy helped Ryan through his pre-sleep `Protocol T-10` routine and the cabin lights dimmed to their warm sleep setting, all five of them headed into the **Family Stateroom**.

While Tyler and Evan stretched out on **King Bed 2**, Ryan transferred into the center of **King Bed 1**, with Cassidy curling up on his right side and **Holly**—officially off the clock at last—curling up in her soft cotton pajamas against his left shoulder as his port-side human bed rail.

"Goodnight, Boss," Ryan whispered, kissing the top of Holly's head as he rested his hand over hers and Cassidy's.

"Goodnight, Ry. Goodnight, Cass," Holly murmured sleepily against his shoulder—and within sixty seconds, the fiercest band manager in two star systems was fast asleep between the people she loved most.
