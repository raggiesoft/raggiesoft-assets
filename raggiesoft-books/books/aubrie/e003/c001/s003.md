At two-fifteen, **Jordan** pressed the exterior handicap paddle beside the **first-floor inner quad entrance** of **Bennett Hall** and rolled across the threshold into the warm lower-level main lobby (`Room 100`), with **Aubrie Jean**, **Madison**, **Zoë**, **Lauren**, **Hannah**, **Mark**, **Sarah**, and **Jessica** stepping in right behind him.

Because the first floor of Bennett Hall was 100% administrative and business space—housing the 60-inch TV lounge, the **Bennett Hall Mail Room (`Room 101`)**, Residence Director **Kelsey Brennan**’s office and apartment (`Suite 102 / Room 104`), and the curved oak **1st-Floor Front Desk (`Ext. 4-2100`)**—Jordan had originally planned to just roll past the front desk, take the elevator up one floor to the second-floor West Wing, and knock on **`Room 215`** to see if **Isabel** was back from training.

Then he glanced toward the curved oak front desk—and stopped his wheelchair in sheer delight.

*Wait—who was that at the desk?!*

**ISABEL!!**

Standing right behind the reception counter in her maroon **CPI Residence Life** polo with her duty lanyard around her neck and a Spring Semester check-in clipboard in her hands—flanked by Residence Director **Kelsey Brennan** and a tall, broad-shouldered senior RA wearing a **`Room 201`** name badge—was **Isabel Claire Dawson**.

At the sound of the lobby doors opening, Isabel looked up from her clipboard.

The instant her hazel eyes landed on Jordan sitting upright and smiling in his wheelchair at the center of the lobby, her breath visibly caught in her throat.

She didn't wait for him to roll up to the counter window. Setting her clipboard down on the oak desk so fast the pen rolled across the blotter, Isabel walked briskly around the end of the reception counter and crossed the lobby floor straight to him.

"Jordan!!" Isabel breathed, her voice cracking with sudden, overwhelming emotion.

Jordan set his wheel locks and opened both arms wide, grinning from ear to ear at his favorite CPI staff member.

Dropping smoothly to her knees beside his wheelchair just as she always did, Isabel wrapped both arms around his shoulders.

Jordan had no idea what was going through Isabel’s head in that moment. All he knew was what he could feel and see for himself: that **Isabel’s hug was a lot closer, tighter, and more lingering than usual**.

Instead of her customary two-second big-sister squeeze and two brisk pats on the shoulder, Isabel pulled him tightly against her chest and buried her face against the shoulder of his maroon CPI jacket, holding onto him for several long beats as a quiet, trembling breath escaped her lips.

"I'm so glad you're back, Jordan," Isabel whispered against his shoulder, her voice thick and wavering. "I'm so, so glad you made it back."

When she finally pulled back an inch and gave the back of his shoulder two quick pats, Jordan blinked in surprise behind his glasses—because **Isabel was crying**.

Real tears were spilling over her lower lashes, and as she hastily swiped the back of her wrist across her eyes and stood up beside his chair, **a vivid crimson blush bloomed all the way up her neck to the tips of her ears**.

"Sorry—I'm sorry," Isabel let out a shaky, self-conscious laugh, fanning her flushed cheek with one hand as she wiped another tear from the corner of her eye. "Even knowing you were with Daniel and Laura, I just... part of me was so afraid all break that your mother was going to find some way to drag you back to Virginia Beach and undo *every* single bit of progress you've made since August. I'm just so grateful you made it safely back to Bennett Hall."

Sitting in his wheelchair, Jordan looked up at her wet eyelashes with pure, earnest warmth—touched to the core that his favorite CPI staff member had worried about him and cared about his progress that deeply—and looked straight down at the keys of his `Precisionist 5000` to type his reply:

<aac>I missed you too, Isabel! You were the very first person I came looking for as soon as we got off the Maroon Express!</aac>

Because Jordan was looking down at his keyboard as he typed, **he completely missed the silent exchange that happened ten feet away behind the curved oak front desk**.

Standing just behind Jordan’s right shoulder, however, **Mark Anderson** saw the whole thing.

Mark had always been the quiet observer of the group when it came to reading body language across a room. He had noticed how closely and how long Isabel had held onto Jordan, he had seen the tears glistening on her lashes and the scarlet blush rushing into her cheeks—and when Isabel glanced nervously back toward the front desk to retrieve her clipboard, Mark’s sharp eyes caught **Residence Director Kelsey Brennan** looking right at her.

Kelsey didn't say a word aloud. Instead, Mark watched Kelsey give Isabel a brief, unmistakable **knowing look**—causing Isabel’s breath to hitch slightly—followed immediately by a slow, steady, measured nod from Kelsey that communicated an entire conversation without a single syllable: *as long as you stay professional, I understand.*

Mark watched a wave of visible relief wash across Isabel’s flushed face as she gave Kelsey a tiny, grateful nod in return.

Mark didn't say a word to anyone, and his expression didn't change by a millimeter—he simply filed that look from Kelsey away in the back of his mind, quietly noting it to himself as Kelsey and the new second-floor male RA stepped out from behind the reception counter to greet the group.
