## March 2, 2015 • 2:45 PM — The Investigator at the Front Desk

The first Monday of March arrived with crisp mountain sunshine across **Commonwealth Green**, five days before the start of CPI's ten-day **Spring Break**.

At two-forty-five that afternoon, as **Jordan**, **Aubrie Jean**, **Madison**, **Zoë**, **Lauren**, **Hannah**, **Mark**, **Sarah**, and **Jessica** walked and rolled across the lower residential quad from **Kepler Hall** toward the **first-floor inner quad entrance** of **Bennett Hall**, Jordan's phone chimed with an urgent campus email alert from Hall Director **Kelsey Brennan**:

> **From:** Kelsey Brennan, Residence Director (Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall)<br>
> **To:** Jordan Fuller, Mark Cooper, Zoë Brooks<br>
> **Date:** Monday, March 2, 2015 — 14:45 EST<br>
> **Subject:** URGENT — Come to 1st-Floor Office via Inner Quad Doors (Room 104)
>
> *Hey guys — come straight in through the first-floor inner quad doors and step directly into my private office (Room 104). Do **not** walk up the hill toward the second-floor Washington Street SW entrance right now. Our fourth-floor RAs just intercepted a man claiming to be a private investigator from Virginia Beach who tried to tailgate behind a resident at the entrance door, and CPI Campus Police are escorting him up to his rental car on Washington Street SW.*

Around Jordan's wheelchair, the eight members of his family immediately locked into Dr. Gable's Roman *testudo* formation—**Mark** stepping to his right flank, **Lauren** and **Madison** to his left, **Aubrie Jean** resting her hand on his shoulder, and **Zoë** already activating **`[Jordan Comms]`** on her phone to alert **Daniel and Laura Brooks** as Jordan tapped his **Poly Pass** against the exterior reader to unlock the **first-floor inner quad doors** and rolled straight across the lobby into Kelsey's office.

Inside the private Hall Director's office (Room 104) behind the front desk, **Kelsey Brennan** had closed the horizontal Venetian blinds to the main lobby. Standing beside her desk were fourth-floor Female RA **Brianna Foster** (`Room 415`) and fourth-floor Male RA **Aaron Vance** (`Room 401`)—along with second-floor RAs **Isabel Dawson** and **Garrett Reynolds**—all wearing their two-tone Chicago Maroon CPI Residence Life athletic polo shirts tucked into belted khaki pants and visibly bristling with protective adrenaline.

"Everybody's safe, Jordan—he never laid eyes on you, and he never set a single foot inside Bennett Hall," Kelsey said immediately as Jordan wheeled inside.

"What happened, Kelsey?" Mark asked, closing the solid oak office door behind the group.

"Twenty minutes ago, one of our fourth-floor female residents was walking up the pathway toward the Bennett Hall entrance carrying two bags of groceries," **Brianna Foster**, the fourth-floor female RA, explained clearly to Jordan and his found family. "She noticed a stranger in a tan trench coat with a DSLR camera bag slung over his shoulder walking uncomfortably close behind her heels—trying to position himself to tailgate right through the door the moment she unlocked it. Instead of tapping her Poly Pass and letting him force his way inside behind her, she slowed down on the approach and looked deliberately through the lobby window toward the front desk, trying to catch the eye of whoever was on duty to wordlessly communicate that someone behind her was trying to force entry."

"Aaron was on duty at the front desk," **Kelsey** continued, gesturing toward the fourth-floor male RA, "and he immediately recognized her as one of his and Brianna's fourth-floor female residents—and read the warning look in her eyes through the glass right away."

"Before I even stepped out from behind the counter, I discreetly hit the silent under-desk panic button—which instantly summoned Kelsey from Room 104 and sent a priority dispatch alert to **CPI Campus Police**," **Aaron** said, folding his arms across his chest. "Brianna and I went straight to the entrance door, got our fourth-floor resident and her groceries safely inside, and I stepped into the threshold to block the door and asked the guy outside on the walkway if I could be of any assistance."

Jordan felt a cold jolt in the pit of his stomach, even as his hands on his wheel rims stayed steady. Beside him, Aubrie Jean squeezed his shoulder firmly.

"He tried to bluff his way past the door," **Brianna** said fiercely. "Standing outside the entrance, he flashed a Tidewater private investigator's license, claimed he had been retained by **Aubrie Sinclair** down in Virginia Beach, and demanded to know whether *'Aubrey Jordan Fuller'* lived in Bennett Hall and what time he left the building for classes. Aaron and Kelsey didn't budge an inch—they cited CPI's **FERPA Directory Hold Policy** and our **No-Tailgating Residence Hall Security Policy**, and told him flatly that Housing & Residence Life will **neither confirm nor deny** that there is a student named Aubrey Jordan Fuller enrolled at the university or residing in this building."

"Because Aaron hit the silent panic button the second he saw our resident being tailed, **CPI Campus Police** were at the door outside of Bennett Hall in ninety seconds," **Kelsey** added, holding up a yellow campus police incident copy. "Officer Jennings intercepted him right there outside the door before he ever crossed the threshold, served him with a permanent criminal trespass bar from all Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute property, and escorted him back up to his tan rental sedan on **Washington Street SW**. However, before Officer Jennings walked him away from the door, the man muttered that he's booked at a motel down on Route 460 for the rest of the week and plans to stake out the town intersections and bus stops off-campus before Spring Break starts on Friday."

Listening to Kelsey, Brianna, and Aaron's report, the pieces clicked together in Jordan's mind with crystal clarity.

Opening his **Pinnacle Vanguard Pro** laptop on his lap tray, Jordan typed steadily:

<aac>My mother's tax return bounced. When she tried to e-file her 2014 taxes at the end of February and claim my Social Security number as a dependent, the IRS computer rejected her return because of Operation File First. And when she called the insurance company and found out I wasn't on her policy anymore, she realized she had lost control—so she hired a private investigator to stalk me and find out where I'm going for Spring Break.</aac>

"And if she paid a PI to drive seven hours from Virginia Beach to Blacksburg, she's hoping he can track you down off-campus or follow us home to Christiansburg," **Zoë** said, looking up from the encrypted **`[Jordan Comms]`** thread on her phone with a calm, focused nod. "I just activated **Jordan Comms** and briefed Dad and Mom on the priority channel. They're convening a War Council at our house on Geneva Street in Christiansburg at five o'clock, and as soon as Campus Police confirm Washington Street SW is clear, Dad and Caleb are pulling right up to the second-floor Washington Street SW entrance to pick us up."

Looking up at **Kelsey**, **Brianna**, **Aaron**, **Isabel**, and **Garrett**, Jordan tapped his AAC screen with deep gratitude:

<aac>Thank you, all of you—and please thank your fourth-floor resident for us too. Because she caught Aaron's eye through the window and you stopped him at the door, he never even made it into the lobby.</aac>

"Not in our building," Aaron said firmly. "Not ever."

And beside the door, Brianna, Garrett, and Isabel gave Jordan a fierce, unified nod—their hands resting near the embroidered **CPI HOUSING & RESIDENCE LIFE** crests on the left chest of their maroon polo shirts, guarding his sanctuary with everything they had.

## March 2, 2015 • 5:15 PM — The War Council and The Hotel at Poly

By five-fifteen that Monday evening—after **Daniel** and **Caleb** had picked up the nine students outside the second-floor **Washington Street SW** entrance of Bennett Hall in Blacksburg and driven them fifteen minutes down Route 460 to the Brooks family residence at **414 Geneva Street** in **Christiansburg**—the dining room had been transformed into a tactical command center.

Seated at the head of the oak table were **Daniel** and **Laura Brooks**, surrounded by all nine members of Suite 214/216 along with **Caleb** and nineteen-year-old **Owen Brooks**.

"First," Daniel said calmly, looking directly at **Jordan** with steady, unshakeable reassurance, "take a deep breath, son. Because of the FERPA Directory Hold that you and Ms. Higgins put in place back in the fall, and because that sharp-eyed fourth-floor resident caught Aaron Vance's eye through the lobby window so Aaron, Brianna, and Kelsey could intercept the man outside the door in less than ninety seconds, Aubrie Sinclair's private investigator never even set foot inside Bennett Hall—and walked away from campus today with zero photographs, zero room numbers, and a permanent criminal trespass bar from CPI Campus Police."

Jordan let out a slow breath, his shoulders easing slightly as **Aubrie Jean** and **Madison** held his hands on either side of his wheelchair.

"What we know right now," **Laura** continued gently, "is that your mother is furious because her 2014 tax return was rejected by the IRS after you filed your own taxes in January, and because she can no longer track you through her health insurance or cell-phone bill. She hired this Tidewater private investigator to figure out where you're living, who you're with, and where you go when CPI lets out for breaks—probably hoping she can either confront you off-campus or find an angle to harass us."

"Wait—what about that investigator lurking around Blacksburg and Route 460 *this* week?" **Aubrie Jean** asked protectively, her hand tightening around Jordan's. "We still have four days of classes before Spring Break starts on Friday afternoon, and we don't want that creep tailing the Blue Ridge Transit buses or staking out the edges of campus waiting for Jordan!"

"And he won't get the chance," **Daniel** said, his calm London accent turning steel-hard. "Because until Spring Break starts on Friday afternoon, Jordan is not going to be at Bennett Hall—and he isn't going to be commuting up and down Route 460 where a private investigator can follow our license plates. Neither is Aubrie Jean or Madison."

Daniel slid three white electronic hotel keycards across the oak table.

"Twenty minutes ago," Daniel said, "we booked an accessible, ground-floor executive suite at **The Hotel at Poly**—the university conference hotel right across the street from the upper quad. And so that no private investigator running a credit-card header or hotel registry search for *Fuller*, *Kelly*, *Scott*, or *Daniel Brooks* can ever trace the room, the reservation is booked and checked in strictly under **nineteen-year-old Owen Brooks's** name."

From the kitchen doorway, **Owen** grinned and held up the hotel check-in folio, while **Caleb** stood beside him with two empty suitcases.

"Caleb and I are driving back up to Bennett Hall tonight with Mark and Lauren to pack five days' worth of clothes, toiletries, and textbooks for Jordan, Aubrie Jean, and Madison," Owen said proudly. "We'll bring them straight through the side courtyard entrance of The Hotel at Poly so the three of you don't even have to step foot near the public lobby."

Jordan looked from Owen and Caleb to Daniel, and then typed on his tablet:

<aac>Wait—what about our classes this week? PHYS 102, ASTR 204, HIST 115, GEOL 101, and MATH 112?</aac>

"Already handled, Jordan," Laura smiled, gesturing toward her phone. "With your permission, we called **Ms. Patricia Higgins** at the **Office of Educational Accessibility (Callahan Hall)**, who immediately notified **Dr. Alistair**, **Dr. Marcus Gable**, **Mr. Evans**, and **Dr. Petrova** that a stalker hired by your estranged mother was trespassed from Bennett Hall today."

Just then, Jordan's laptop chimed with two incoming campus emails—one from **Dr. Alistair** and one from **Dr. Marcus Gable**:

> **From:** Dr. Alistair (Department of Physics & Astronomy, CPI)<br>
> **To:** Jordan Fuller, Aubrie Jean Kelly<br>
> **Subject:** Pre-Spring Break Remote Lab & Lecture Clearance
>
> *Mr. Fuller and Ms. Kelly — Ms. Higgins at OEA informed me that you require a secure off-site study location prior to Friday's Spring Break departure. Consider yourselves 100% excused from in-person attendance for the remainder of the week. Ms. Brooks is authorized to stream the lectures to your terminal, and my door in Kepler Hall is always on your side.*

> **From:** Dr. Marcus Gable (Department of History, CPI)<br>
> **To:** Jordan Fuller, Aubrie Jean Kelly, Madison Scott<br>
> **Subject:** HIST 115 — Holding the Testudo
>
> *Keep your shields locked and stay safe this week. Zoë and Mark will record Tuesday and Thursday's lectures on the Roman Republic for you. See you all after Spring Break.*

## March 2, 2015 • 6:30 PM — Checked In at The Hotel at Poly

An hour later, inside the quiet, plushly carpeted ground-floor accessible suite of **The Hotel at Poly**—with the curtains drawn, room-service dinner trays set out on the table by **Owen** and **Caleb**, and **Aubrie Jean** and **Madison** curled warmly on either side of him on the king-sized hotel bed—Jordan looked around their temporary fortress.

His mother had sent a stalker across the state to hunt him down. Instead, his professors, his RAs, his brothers, and his Constellation had moved like a single unbreakable shield wall before the investigator could even get past the front desk.

***

## March 7, 2015 • 9:15 AM — Sanctuary Compromised (The Rubber Duck Convoy to Bluewater)

At nine-fifteen on Saturday morning, March seventh—the official start of Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute's ten-day **Spring Break**—**Jordan**, **Aubrie Jean**, and **Madison** were packing their overnight bags inside the ground-floor suite of **The Hotel at Poly** while **Mark**, **Lauren**, **Zoë**, **Hannah**, **Sarah**, and **Jessica** loaded the vehicles in the sheltered side courtyard.

Suddenly, **Mark’s** phone buzzed with a text from sixteen-year-old **Owen Brooks**, who was running a perimeter check down in Christiansburg with **Daniel** and **Caleb**:

> **From:** Owen Brooks<br>
> **To:** Mark Cooper, Zoë Brooks<br>
> **Time:** 9:18 AM
>
> *Heads up — Dad, Caleb, and I just spotted that tan rental sedan with Tidewater plates parked across North Franklin Street near Strickland Farms, watching the turnoff toward Geneva Street. The PI knows CPI just let out for Spring Break and he's waiting to see if you guys bring Jordan back to the Christiansburg house today.*

Inside the hotel suite, **Madison** didn't hesitate for a single second. Back in January, on the night her mother had called in tears to tell her that fourteen-year-old **Bob**—Madison's beloved **"squirt"**—had been assaulted and stuffed into his locker at Bluewater High, permanently damaging his leg, Madison had promised her little brother and her fifteen-year-old cousin **Emily** that she would come home for Spring Break in March. Now, she held up her phone, where she had just finished a call with her parents.

"He can sit in that parking lot all week staring at an empty driveway," Madison said firmly, her bright blue eyes flashing with protective resolve. "I just got off the phone with my Mom and Dad—**David and Diane Scott**—down in **Bluewater**. I already promised **Bob** and **Emily** back in January that I was coming home for Spring Break, and Mom says the **Bluewater Bunker** is open and waiting for all of us—including Daniel, Laura, Caleb, and Owen."

Jordan looked up at Madison from his wheelchair and tapped his **Pinnacle Vanguard Pro** screen:

<aac>Are your parents sure they don't mind all of us descending on their house for Spring Break on ten minutes' notice? Especially with everything Bob has been recovering from since January?</aac>

"Mind?!" Madison smiled warmly, blinking back a sudden shimmer of emotion as she leaned down to kiss the top of Jordan's head. "Jordan, ever since that phone call in January, I've been counting the days until I could get home to hug my squirt and Em in person. Since Bob has been in a wheelchair since the assault, Mom and Dad already built a ramp up the front porch steps. And Mom says having you there—someone who already knows how to live and thrive on wheels—is the best medicine my little brother could possibly ask for."

"And because the Scott residence is ninety minutes southwest in rural **Bluewater** under David and Diane Scott," **Zoë** added with a crisp, approving nod as she closed her laptop, "there is zero public-record link between Aubrie Sinclair's investigator and Madison's family. Dad, Mum, Caleb, and Owen are already pulling out via the back streets of Christiansburg to rendezvous with us at the Interstate 81 ramp. If we depart directly from the west courtyard exit of The Hotel at Poly, we vanish completely off his grid."

Within ten minutes, the **Rubber Duck Convoy** rolled quietly out into the bright March morning and linked up on the highway.

Following **Mark’s** lead, the four vehicles—Daniel and Laura's van, Caleb and Owen's car, Mark's SUV with Hannah, Sarah, and Jessica, and Lauren's car with Zoë riding shotgun and **Jordan** buckled securely in the back seat between **Aubrie Jean** and **Madison**—moved as a single unit down Interstate 81 South and Interstate 77 into Carroll County, maintaining a tight, defensive formation that never let a single unknown vehicle slip between them.

As the convoy climbed into the deep, pine-covered ridges of Southwest Virginia toward Bluewater—leaving the Virginia Beach investigator sitting empty-handed in a Christiansburg parking lot—Jordan leaned his head back against Aubrie Jean’s shoulder, held Madison’s hand across his lap, and got ready to keep the promise he had made to her in January: to show her little squirt that life on wheels wasn't the end of the road.
