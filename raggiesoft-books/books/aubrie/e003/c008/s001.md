The first Monday of March arrived with crisp mountain sunshine across **Commonwealth Green**, five days before the start of CPI's ten-day **Spring Break**.

At two-forty-five that afternoon, as **Jordan**, **Aubrie Jean**, **Madison**, **Zoë**, **Lauren**, **Hannah**, **Mark**, **Sarah**, and **Jessica** walked and rolled across the lower residential quad from **Kepler Hall** toward the **first-floor inner quad entrance** of **Bennett Hall**, Jordan's phone chimed with an urgent campus email alert from Hall Director **Kelsey Brennan**:

> **From:** Kelsey Brennan, Residence Director (Eleanor Bennett Commonwealth Hall)<br>
> **To:** Jordan Fuller, Mark Anderson, Zoë Brooks<br>
> **Date:** Monday, March 2, 2015 — 14:45 EST<br>
> **Subject:** URGENT — Come to 1st-Floor Office via Inner Quad Doors (Room 104)
>
> *Hey guys — come straight in through the first-floor inner quad doors and step directly into my private office (Room 104). Do **not** walk up the hill toward the second-floor Washington Street SW entrance right now. Our fourth-floor RAs just intercepted a man claiming to be a private investigator from Virginia Beach who tried to tailgate behind a resident at the entrance door, and CPI Campus Police are escorting him up to his rental car on Washington Street SW.*

Around Jordan's wheelchair, the eight members of his family immediately locked into Dr. Gable's Roman *testudo* formation—**Mark** stepping to his right flank, **Lauren** and **Madison** to his left, **Aubrie Jean** resting her hand on his shoulder, and **Zoë** already activating **`[Jordan Comms]`** on her phone to alert **Daniel and Laura Brooks** as Jordan tapped his **Poly Pass** against the exterior reader to unlock the **first-floor inner quad doors** and rolled straight across the lobby into Kelsey's office.

Inside the private Hall Director's office (Room 104) behind the front desk, **Kelsey Brennan** had closed the horizontal Venetian blinds to the main lobby. Standing beside her desk were fourth-floor Female RA **Brianna Foster** (`Room 415`) and fourth-floor Male RA **Aaron Vance** (`Room 401`)—along with second-floor RAs **Isabel Dawson** and **Garrett Reynolds**—all wearing their two-tone Chicago Maroon CPI Residence Life athletic polo shirts tucked into belted khaki pants and visibly bristling with protective adrenaline.

"Everybody's safe, Jordan—he never laid eyes on you, and he never set a single foot inside Bennett Hall," Kelsey said immediately as Jordan wheeled inside.

"What happened, Kelsey?" Mark asked, closing the solid oak office door behind the group.

"Twenty minutes ago, one of our fourth-floor female residents was walking up the pathway toward the Bennett Hall entrance carrying two bags of groceries," **Brianna Foster**, the fourth-floor female RA, explained clearly to Jordan and his found family. "She noticed a stranger in a tan trench coat with a DSLR camera bag slung over his shoulder walking uncomfortably close behind her heels—trying to position himself to tailgate right through the door the moment she unlocked it. Instead of tapping her Poly Pass and letting him force his way inside behind her, she slowed down on the approach and looked deliberately through the lobby window toward the front desk, trying to catch the eye of whoever was on duty to wordlessly communicate that someone behind her was trying to force entry."

"Aaron was on duty at the front desk," **Kelsey** continued, gesturing toward the fourth-floor male RA, "and he immediately recognized her as one of his and Brianna's fourth-floor female residents—and read the warning look in her eyes through the glass right away."

"Before I even stepped out from behind the counter, I discreetly hit the silent under-desk panic button—which instantly summoned Kelsey from Room 104 and sent a priority dispatch alert to **CPI Campus Police**," **Aaron** said, folding his arms across his chest. "Brianna and I went straight to the entrance door, got our fourth-floor resident and her groceries safely inside, and I stepped into the threshold to block the door and asked the guy outside on the walkway if I could be of any assistance."

Jordan felt a cold jolt in the pit of his stomach, even as his hands on his wheel rims stayed steady. Beside him, Aubrie Jean squeezed his shoulder firmly.

"He tried to bluff his way past the door," **Brianna** said fiercely. "Standing outside the entrance, he flashed a Tidewater private investigator's license, claimed he had been retained by **Aubrie Sinclair** down in Virginia Beach, and demanded to know whether *'Aubrey Jordan Fuller'* lived in Bennett Hall and what time he left the building for classes. Aaron and Kelsey didn't budge an inch—they cited CPI's **FERPA Directory Hold Policy** and our **No-Tailgating Residence Hall Security Policy**, and told him flatly that Housing & Residence Life will **neither confirm nor deny** that there is a student named Aubrey Jordan Fuller enrolled at the university or residing in this building."

"Because Aaron hit the silent panic button the second he saw our resident being tailed, **CPI Campus Police** were at the door outside of Bennett Hall in ninety seconds," **Kelsey** added, holding up a yellow campus police incident copy. "Officer Jennings intercepted him right there outside the door before he ever crossed the threshold, served him with a permanent criminal trespass bar from all Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute property, and escorted him back up to his tan rental sedan on **Washington Street SW**. However, before Officer Jennings walked him away from the door, the man muttered that he's booked at a motel down on Route 460 for the rest of the week and plans to stake out the town intersections and bus stops off-campus before Spring Break starts on Friday."

Listening to Kelsey, Brianna, and Aaron's report, the pieces clicked together in Jordan's mind with crystal clarity.

Opening his **Pinnacle Vanguard Pro** laptop on his lap tray, Jordan typed steadily:

<aac>My mother's tax return bounced. When she tried to e-file her 2014 taxes at the end of February and claim my Social Security number as a dependent, the IRS computer rejected her return because of Operation File First. And when she called the insurance company and found out I wasn't on her policy anymore, she realized she had lost control—so she hired a private investigator to stalk me and find out where I'm going for Spring Break.</aac>

"And if she paid a PI to drive seven hours from Virginia Beach to Blacksburg, she's hoping he can track you down off-campus or follow us home to Christiansburg," **Zoë** said, looking up from the encrypted **`[Jordan Comms]`** thread on her phone with a calm, focused nod. "I just activated **Jordan Comms** and briefed Dad and Mom on the priority channel. They're convening a War Council at our house on Geneva Street in Christiansburg at five o'clock, and as soon as Campus Police confirm Washington Street SW is clear, Dad and Caleb are pulling right up to the second-floor Washington Street SW entrance to pick us up."

Looking up at **Kelsey**, **Brianna**, **Aaron**, **Isabel**, and **Garrett**, Jordan tapped his AAC screen with deep gratitude:

<aac>Thank you, all of you—and please thank your fourth-floor resident for us too. Because she caught Aaron's eye through the window and you stopped him at the door, he never even made it into the lobby.</aac>

"Not in our building," Aaron said firmly. "Not ever."

And beside the door, Brianna, Garrett, and Isabel gave Jordan a fierce, unified nod—their hands resting near the embroidered **CPI HOUSING & RESIDENCE LIFE** crests on the left chest of their maroon polo shirts, guarding his sanctuary with everything they had.
