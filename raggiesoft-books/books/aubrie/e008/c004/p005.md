## 9:50 PM — *Our Own Frequency* & *Brother's Keeper*

When the stage lights dimmed after *"Twin Moons: Touchdown"*, **Jordan** watched something unexpected happen out on the stage deck.

**Evan Wright** unstrapped his bass, **Tyler Wright** stepped down from the drum riser, and the twin cousins—along with all five members of the touring section (**Marcus Vance**, **Darius "Doc" Holloway**, **Claire Lindqvist**, **Julian Vance**, and **Elena Rostova**)—walked over to center stage, gave **Ryan** and **Cassidy** a long, warm hug, and walked off into the wings.

Across the massive stadium stage, every towering amplifier stack and orbital truss light went dark—leaving only a single, warm golden spotlight shining down at the front lip of center stage.

**Cassidy O'Connell** slid over to the right half of her gleaming black grand-piano bench, and **Ryan O'Connell** rolled his manual wheelchair flush against the left end of the bench and locked his wheel brakes—sitting hip-to-hip and shoulder-to-shoulder with his sister, a sunburst acoustic guitar resting across his lap angled slightly outward to clear the keys while their shoulders touched.

"Evan and Tyler are gonna take five backstage for a little bit," Cassidy said softly into her piano microphone across the hushed stadium. "Back in two thousand and eight, right in the middle of our ten-year hiatus, Ryan and I couldn't face the cameras or the stadiums anymore. So we locked the front door of our house right down the road on Progress Street, sat down in the living room with just an acoustic guitar, a piano, and two microphones, and recorded an album we never toured."

"We just uploaded it on a Tuesday morning with one sentence: *'We're still here, and we're okay,'*" Ryan added into his mic. "We never thought we'd play these songs in a stadium... but tonight, sixty-five thousand of you feel just like our living room."

In `Section 104, Row A`, **Daniel Brooks**’s breath caught as Ryan fingerpicked the first gentle acoustic chord and Cassidy joined him on the grand piano.

"No way..." Daniel whispered to Laura and Jordan. "That's [**'Our Own Frequency'**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible?track=3-01-our-own-frequency). They've never played a single note of that album in front of a crowd!"

In the hushed stillness of the sixty-five-thousand-seat stadium, brother and sister sang *"Our Own Frequency"* with just an acoustic guitar and a grand piano, turning the loudest football stadium in the Commonwealth into a quiet Blacksburg living room.

When the final chord faded into pin-drop silence, Ryan rested his hands on the body of his acoustic guitar and leaned closer to his microphone.

"Earlier in *Star City: Orbital Ward*, we sang about the four-hour clock and the maintenance protocols in sci-fi metaphors," Ryan said softly, his voice echoing clearly off the quiet limestone stands. "This next song is the centerpiece of [**Our Own Frequency**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2008-our-own-frequency), and it's the most private song Cassidy and I have ever written—without any metaphors at all. People look up at a stage and see two hours of lights and guitars. They don't see what T-ten paralysis actually takes behind closed doors. They don't see Cassidy and Evan and Tyler helping me through my daily care and toileting routines, or stretching my legs every morning so they don't atrophy. They don't see how we push the bed against the wall at night so I can sleep on the inside, and my sister sleeps on the outside edge as a human bed rail so I don't roll off in the dark."

Beside him at the grand piano, Cassidy smiled tenderly through her glasses and spoke into her mic:

"And they don't see how you keep me anchored when the world gets too loud for my head to handle. We take care of each other. That's what family does."

Down in `Section 104, Row A`, nobody moved a muscle.

**Jordan** felt a lump rise in his throat so thick he could barely swallow. On his left, **Aubrie Jean**’s fingers tightened warmly around his hand; on his right, **Madison** and **Megan** leaned against his shoulder and armrest; and down the row, **Mark**, **Hannah**, **Zoë**, **Lauren**, **Sarah**, **Jessica**, and **Isabel** were all looking at Jordan with shining, misty eyes. Every single thing Ryan and Cassidy were describing on that stage—stretching legs in the morning, helping with daily care without a shred of shame, building a human bed-rail in the dark so someone you love never falls, and anchoring each other when sensory overload hits `100`—was the exact life they had built together in `Suite 214/216` and on `Geneva Street`.

Up on stage, Ryan took a slow breath.

"Doing all of those daily routines on tour buses and in hotel rooms across the world was one thing when I was twenty-six in nineteen-ninety," Ryan continued, his voice honest and steady. "Twenty-six years later... I'm gonna be honest with you, Forger Nation. Accommodating this chair on the road has gotten too hard on my body, and on my family. Tonight is the final stop of the *Reignition* tour... and tonight is the last concert a touring Stardust Engine will ever play."

An audible gasp rippled across sixty-five thousand people inside **The Crucible**. Down in `Row A`, **Madison** pressed both hands over her mouth, and **Mark** whispered, *"No..."* Around the stadium, thousands of fans stood in stunned heartbreak, thinking they were witnessing the band's farewell announcement—before a warm, deeply respectful wave of supportive applause rose from the field to thank Ryan for everything he had given them.

And then Cassidy leaned forward into her piano microphone, her eyes sparkling behind her glasses:

"Wait—don't be sad! We aren't retiring!" Cassidy called out over the murmur. "If touring the world is too hard on Ryan, then the world can come to Blacksburg! To give the **Town of Blacksburg** and **CPI** time to build this right, starting in **August 2017**, we're launching the annual **Forger Nation Summer Festival**! A massive CPI Open House for incoming and prospective freshmen, the biggest downtown Blacksburg block party of the summer with stages for signed and unsigned bands, and every single year we'll close the festival right here inside **The Crucible** with fireworks—and sleep in our own beds on Progress Street every night!"

For ten full seconds, the roar that erupted inside **The Crucible** was deafening—heartbreak turning instantaneously into pure, roof-shaking euphoria as the 120-foot curved LED screen behind the piano lit up in warm gold lettering:

```
THE FORGER NATION SUMMER FESTIVAL
CPI OPEN HOUSE • DOWNTOWN BLACKSBURG BLOCK PARTY • THE CRUCIBLE RESIDENCY
INAUGURAL WEEKEND: AUGUST 2017
```

"They're staying home in Blacksburg forever!" **Mark** cheered, wiping his eyes and grinning at Jordan. "Every August, right here in our backyard!"

As the cheers gently hushed back to pin-drop silence, Ryan whispered, *"This is 'Brother's Keeper,'"* and Cassidy’s fingers began the delicate, melancholy grand-piano intro of [**"Brother's Keeper"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible?track=3-02-brothers-keeper), joined seconds later by Ryan’s steady, comforting fingerpicked acoustic guitar:

> *"They look at the chair and they think it's weak*<br>
> *They whisper their pity whenever we speak*<br>
> *But they don't see the mornings, the stretches and pain*<br>
> *The quiet routines keeping us sane*<br>
> *Sleeping on the edge, a human bed rail*<br>
> *Catching the other whenever we fail..."*

When Ryan and Cassidy harmonized on the chorus—*"We trade all the armor, we trade all the scars / We built our own world underneath all the stars / When the body won't work, we become our own feet / When the pressure is heavy, we make it retreat"*—Jordan tapped his `mPad` softly so only Aubrie Jean, Madison, and Megan beside him could hear:

<aac>"That song is us."</aac>

"Every single word of it, J," Aubrie Jean whispered, kissing his temple as sixty-five thousand fans held up quiet white phone lights across the dark stadium bowl and brother and sister flowed directly into the gentle acoustic assurances of 2008's [**"Safe Person"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible?track=3-03-safe-person)—and then, after Cassidy told the crowd, *"On Reignition, we recorded the sequel to 'Safe Person' with the full stadium band... but since we're still sitting here in our living room with you guys, Ryan and I wanted to strip this one all the way down to just the piano and the six-string,"* stripped 2015's [**"Still Your Safe Person"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible?track=3-04-still-your-safe-person) all the way down to Cassidy’s grand piano and Ryan’s fingerpicked acoustic guitar (*"When the static hits a hundred and the room begins to spin / I am still your safe person—let the quiet in! / Put your headphones on or lean against my shoulder tight / I will hold the perimeter throughout the longest night!"*), bringing the entire stadium to its feet in a deeply moved ovation.
