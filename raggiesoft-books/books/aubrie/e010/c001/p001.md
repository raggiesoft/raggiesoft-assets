## 8:50 AM — Outside Room 104, Carter Hall

Monday, August 22, 2016—the first morning of Junior Year at Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute—began in the sunlit ground-floor corridor of **Carter Hall**.

Parked near the open doorway of `Room 104` for their shared intermediate foreign language block, **`JAPN 201: Intermediate Japanese I`**, **Jordan Shawn Kelly** sat comfortably in his `Vanguard Pro` manual wheelchair, his prescription glasses resting cleanly on the bridge of his nose and his behind-the-ear hearing aids calibrated to the gentle morning hum of the hallway. Because Jordan was never alone in a single class at CPI—and because **Zoë Wren Brooks**, who had lived in Osaka, Japan for four years as a child and spoke Japanese at a near-native level, had coached and tutored the entire family through `JAPN 101` and `JAPN 102` during their sophomore year—the whole circle was right there with him. Five weeks after his wedding and legal name change in Christiansburg, Jordan's left hand—wearing his gold wedding band matching the gold wedding band on his wife's left ring finger—was laced effortlessly with the right hand of his wife, **Aubrie Jean Kelly**, while **Zoë**, **Madison**, **Lauren**, **Sarah**, **Jessica Gallagher**, **Hannah**, and **Mark** stood in a relaxed semicircle around his chair reviewing their first-week *kanji* flashcards and trading predictions about the upcoming Forgers football opener.

Then a new transfer student walked around the corner from the main stairwell, glancing anxiously up at the brass room numbers above the doors.

She was twenty years old, about five-foot-four with a softly rounded, slightly heavier build, wearing a cozy dusty-teal knit pullover sweater layered over a white crew-neck tee, medium-wash blue jeans, canvas sneakers, and a dark backpack slung over her right shoulder. Neat bangs framed her forehead beneath soft chestnut-brown hair held back on the sides with tortoiseshell barrette clips.

As she checked the placard beside `Room 104`, her gaze shifted toward the circle of students gathered by the doorway—and locked directly onto Jordan.

She stopped dead in her tracks.

Across twelve feet of polished terrazzo floor, Jordan looked up into those warm, welcoming **hazel eyes**—flecked with the exact same green and amber gold he had last seen across an oak library table in Virginia Beach four years ago. Her face was a little more mature now, but the quiet kindness in her expression was unmistakable.

For two full seconds, Jordan’s breath caught in his chest, his fingers tightening around Aubrie Jean’s hand.

"Aubrey...?" the young woman whispered, the name slipping past her lips in stunned, hopeful disbelief before she could stop herself.

Then, in the blink of an eye, the color drained from her cheeks.

The memory of June 4, 2012—the memory of **Aubrie Renee Sinclair**’s shrieking, venomous voice coming through the Mercer family's kitchen telephone, calling her a manipulative predator and threatening to call the police and press harassment charges against her parents if she ever came within twenty feet of her son again—slammed into **Jessamine Mercer** like a physical blow.

Her hands clamped with white-knuckled terror onto the straps of her backpack. She took two rapid, stumbling steps backward against the brick hallway wall, her wide hazel eyes darting frantically toward both ends of the Carter Hall corridor as if she half-expected Aubrie Sinclair to come storming around the corner at any second.

"I—I'm sorry!" Jessamine stammered, her voice trembling so badly it barely carried over the hallway chatter as she pressed her back flat against the wall, terrified to come an inch closer and terrified even to speak to him. "I didn't know you were here—I'll drop the class—I won't come near you, I promise, I don't want to get you in trouble or make your mother call the police—"

She spun half-around, ready to bolt back toward the stairwell.

Around Jordan’s wheelchair, the entire circle went dead silent, instantly recognizing the sheer, conditioned terror in the stranger's voice.

Jordan didn't let her run. Releasing Aubrie Jean’s hand for a second, he drove both push-rims forward two paces—stopping six feet away so he wouldn't crowd her against the wall—and typed at lightning speed into his `mPad` so that the warm Australian voice of `Sydney` spoke clearly into the quiet corridor:

<aac>Jess! Please stop—don't run away! It's really you! And you're not in trouble—my mother isn't here!</aac>

At the sound of the nickname that **only he** had ever been allowed to use—*Jess*—Jessamine froze mid-step. Slowly, she turned her head back toward him, her hazel eyes glistening with unshed tears and utter confusion as she looked from the sleek tablet mounted on his chair to his glasses, his hearing aids, and the eight protective faces standing behind him.

"You... you're allowed to talk to me?" Jessamine whispered, her voice shaking as she hugged her elbows tightly against her dusty-teal sweater, careful to keep ten feet of empty floor between them. "Where is she? If she finds out I'm even in the same building as you, she'll—"

**Aubrie Jean Kelly** stepped gently forward beside Jordan’s left wheel—keeping her posture open, calm, and completely non-threatening as she spoke in a warm, steady voice using the only name she had just heard Jordan's `mPad` say.

"Jess," Aubrie Jean said softly, "she isn't anywhere near this campus. And she can never hurt him—or you—ever again."

Hearing a stranger call her *Jess*—the sacred nickname that only the boy in the library had ever been given permission to use—gave Jessamine a tiny, automatic internal jolt. Under any normal circumstance, she would have politely corrected anyone else to *Jessamine* on the spot. Yet she didn't say a word; she didn't want to be rude to this kind woman who was trying to calm her down, and her mind was already completely overwhelmed just trying to process the words she had just heard.

Jessamine blinked rapidly, looking at the young woman with the long brown side braid standing beside his wheelchair. "Who... who are you?"

"I'm Aubrie Jean Kelly," she said with a gentle smile, resting her hand lightly on Jordan’s shoulder. "I'm his wife. And I need you to hear me very clearly: **he has cut his mother completely out of his life.**"

Inside the two minutes before the nine o'clock bell, right there in the quiet alcove outside `Room 104`, Aubrie Jean and Zoë gave Jessamine the clear, factual truth that lifted four years of terror off her shoulders:
* How he had stood up to his mother during Freshman Fall and served her with a permanent university and police No-Contact Directive;
* How his mother had filed a fraudulent adult guardianship petition against him the following summer, only for the Montgomery County Circuit Court to **dismiss her case with prejudice** and remand her into custody for civil contempt and felony perjury;
* And how, five weeks ago on July 15, 2016, in that exact same courthouse across Main Street from their wedding ceremony, he had legally terminated the birth name his mother had used to own him—becoming **Jordan Shawn Kelly**.

Jessamine stared from Aubrie Jean to Zoë, and then back down at the young man in the wheelchair, her hand rising slowly to cover her mouth as four years of fear began to crack apart—while her reeling brain tried simultaneously to process that **Aubrie Sinclair** was truly gone and to rewire the name she had carried in her heart since she was sixteen.

"She's really out of Aubrey's—I mean, *Jordan's*—life?" Jessamine breathed, catching herself mid-word as she tried to process that he went by **Jordan** now instead of **Aubrey**. "You go by Jordan now? Your name is Jordan Shawn Kelly... and she really can't touch you anymore?"

Jordan nodded, his eyes shining brightly behind his glasses as a huge, unguarded smile broke across his face:

<aac>I am Jordan Shawn Kelly now, Jess. She has zero power over me. And this is my family—my wife Aubrie Jean, and Madison, Zoë, Lauren, Sarah, Jessica, Hannah, and Mark.</aac>

"And since our sensei just walked in," **Zoë** added with a warm, welcoming smile in her crisp London cadence, gesturing toward the open doorway of `Room 104` and naturally using the same name they had just heard twice from Jordan's tablet, "it appears you are in our Intermediate Japanese section, Jess. I lived in Osaka for four years as a child and serve as Jordan's official note-taker and our family's Japanese tutor—come sit at our table."

For the second time in sixty seconds, Jordan saw the tiny, almost imperceptible hitch in Jessamine’s shoulders at hearing someone else call her *Jess*—followed immediately by her swallowing the correction with a polite, self-effacing nod because she didn't want to seem rude to his family while her head was still spinning.

Jordan knew that reflex by heart from May 2012. Before anyone could take a step toward the classroom door, his fingers flew across the keyboard of his `mPad`:

<aac>Wait—everyone, I need to tell you something important about her name! Her real name is Jessamine, with an M-I-N-E. Back in our high school library in 2012, she told me that everyone else has to call her Jessamine, and only I am allowed to call her Jess! She's just being way too polite to correct you while she's trying to process everything!</aac>

Jessamine’s cheeks flushed a warm, startled rose all the way to the tips of her ears. Her hazel eyes went wide as she looked down at Jordan—touched to her absolute core that after four years and everything he had survived, he had remembered and protected her rule from their corner table in Virginia Beach.

"You... you still remembered that?" Jessamine whispered, a shy, misty smile breaking through her shock as she glanced apologetically at Aubrie Jean and Zoë. "I'm so sorry—I really didn't want to be rude when you were both being so kind to me! My head was just spinning trying to process that his mother is actually out of Aubrey's—no, *Jordan's*—life, and trying to teach my brain that he's Jordan now."

"Oh, Jessamine, I am so sorry for calling you Jess!" Aubrie Jean said warmly, pressing a hand to her heart with a bright, affectionate laugh. "Of course we only heard Jordan's `mPad` say *Jess*! From this second on, you are strictly *Jessamine* to all of us—and *Jess* belongs exclusively to Jordan."

"Actually, that solves a major namespace collision for our group," **Jessica Gallagher** grinned from her six-foot-three vantage point beside Mark, offering Jessamine a friendly wave. "Because I'm Jessica—never Jess—so having you as *Jessamine* to the rest of us and *Jess* only to Jordan keeps our roster completely crystal clear."

"Namespace updated and permanently locked: **Jessamine**," Zoë smiled, giving a respectful nod toward `Room 104`. "After you, Jessamine."

Still dazed—and still carefully keeping a respectful two-foot buffer around Jordan’s chair so she wouldn't accidentally brush against him—Jessamine nodded gratefully, wiping a happy tear from her cheek with the sleeve of her sweater.

"I'd like that," she whispered. "Thank you... Jordan."

## 9:55 AM — The Cosmic Coincidence

For the next fifty minutes, as the instructor walked the class through the `JAPN 201` syllabus and intermediate *kanji* compounds—the exact language Jessamine had begun teaching herself from a workbook at that corner library table in 2012 before completing her elementary Japanese sequence at community college—Jordan could barely keep from smiling at his keyboard. Using the Japanese Input Method Editor (`IME`) that Zoë had configured inside `NeoEdit`, Jordan typed the two characters Jessamine had once drawn for him in high school—`星空` (*hoshizora*, "starry sky")—and angled his screen slightly so she could see them. Sitting at the accessible table at the front of the room surrounded by Aubrie Jean, Zoë, and the rest of his family on one side and Jessamine one seat over on his right, his past and his present were sitting in the same row.

When class let out at `9:50 AM` and the group filed back into the Carter Hall corridor, **Mark**, **Hannah**, **Zoë**, **Madison**, **Lauren**, **Sarah**, and **Jessica** paused by the main doors before heading toward their ten o'clock classes across the quad.

"All right, J, we'll see you and Aubrie at Kincaid for lunch at noon," Mark said, giving Jordan’s shoulder a warm squeeze and offering Jessamine a friendly nod. "Really great meeting you, Jessamine. And Zoë's evening Japanese study sessions are legendary—you're officially invited."

Because Jordan and Aubrie Jean were both Astrophysics majors who registered for every single lecture and lab section together so Jordan was never alone in class, the two of them remained in the Carter Hall foyer with Jessamine. Already bracing himself for her to walk away toward another part of campus, Jordan turned his chair toward her and typed:

<aac>I still can't believe you're at CPI, Jess. Did you transfer in as a junior from Community College of Hampton Roads? Which way is your next class?</aac>

"Yeah, I finished my associate's degree and my first two semesters of Japanese at CCHR in Virginia Beach first so my parents wouldn't worry," Jessamine explained softly, shifting her backpack strap on her shoulder and pointing down the main quad walkway toward the science complex. "My next lecture is at ten-thirty in Copernicus Hall. Stellar Astrophysics."

Jordan and Aubrie Jean both froze, staring at her, and then looked at each other in stunned delight.

<aac>No way! ASTR 305 with Dr. Alistair at 10:30?!</aac> Jordan typed so fast his fingers nearly tripped over the keys. <aac>And the Tuesday afternoon observatory lab section?!</aac>

"Yes!" Jessamine gasped, her hazel eyes going wide as a real smile broke through her shyness. "You're both Astrophysics majors?!"

"We sure are—Jordan and I take every single Astrophysics class and lab together," Aubrie Jean laughed warmly, stepping beside Jessamine—though noticing how Jessamine still instinctively kept her hands tucked close to her own sides, Aubrie Jean didn't grab her arm without asking. "Come on, walk with us to Copernicus Hall! Between Intermediate Japanese with our whole family and Stellar Astrophysics with the two of us, you're sitting right with us all semester!"
