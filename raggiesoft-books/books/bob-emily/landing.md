---
title: "Bob Emily"
---
After her father's tragic passing, fifteen-year-old Emily—a towering and athletic teenager—finds sanctuary with her aunt and uncle, legally escaping her estranged, conspiracy-obsessed mother. In this new home, she solidifies her bond with her cousin, Bob, a young wheelchair user who shares her profound need for security. 

Communicating through their own private, multi-modal language of sounds and gestures they call "The Heart-Hum," the two become inseparable anchors for each other. From battling sovereign-citizen legal terror in the courtroom to riding the Blue Line bus to Bluewater High, Bob and Emily prove that the strongest fortresses aren't built with walls, but with unconditional understanding, shared resilience, and an unbreakable family bond.
