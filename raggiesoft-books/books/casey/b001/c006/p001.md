# Part 1: The Morning Knock – 7:30 AM

The pale morning light filtering through the blinds of my childhood bedroom was the first thing to pull me out of sleep. Liz was a warm, heavy weight across my chest, her breathing perfectly steady in the quiet room. For the first time in three weeks, my internal processor wasn't running a frantic, defensive loop. I was completely, deeply anchored.

Then, a soft, polite tapping echoed from the bedroom door.

"David?" Casey’s slightly raspy morning voice filtered through the wood. "Are you guys awake?"

"Yeah, we're awake," I called back, my voice still thick with sleep. "Just give us a second, Case!"

Beside me, Liz’s eyes flew open. The haze of sleep vanished instantly as we both became acutely aware that we were completely bare beneath the heavy winter blankets.

From the hallway, a sharp, unmistakable giggle vibrated through the drywall.

"Take your time!" Casey chirped happily, entirely lacking any social filter. "I heard the muffled noises through the wall last night. I know exactly what you guys were doing!"

Liz’s face instantly flushed a brilliant, furious shade of red, and she buried her face into the nearest pillow with a muffled groan. I felt a massive wave of heat crawl up the back of my own neck, my face burning as I realized my childhood bedroom shared a very thin wall with Casey’s.

"We'll be right out, Casey!" I managed to shout back, my voice cracking slightly.

We scrambled out from under the covers, frantically digging through our duffel bags for fresh clothes. Once we were both fully dressed and sufficiently decent, I walked over and pulled the bedroom door open.

Casey didn't hesitate for a microsecond. She launched herself forward, wrapping her arms around my torso in a crushing, deep-pressure squeeze. I hugged her back just as fiercely, resting my chin on top of her head.

"Good morning," Casey beamed, stepping back to pull Liz into a massive hug next. Her dark eyes were practically vibrating with excitement, the awkwardness of her hallway commentary completely overridden by her hyper-focus on the day's itinerary. "I am so ready to go to the airport. You guys are going to absolutely lose your minds when you see the corporate jet. It is the ultimate perimeter."

"I can't wait," Liz laughed, her cheeks still slightly pink as she hugged my little sister back. "Let's go get some breakfast."
