# Part 3: The Evening Routine – 8:30 PM

By the time the dinner dishes were cleared from the kitchen island, a heavy, comfortable exhaustion had settled over the apartment. The adrenaline of the cross-country flight and the sheer relief of our consolidation had finally run its course.

"Alright," Cassandra announced, wheeling herself away from the kitchen counter. "Let's initiate the evening routine."

The Federal Way apartment was efficiently laid out, but space was absolutely at a premium. There were exactly two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Cassandra’s master suite sat at the end of the hall, boasting a massive king-sized bed and a heavily modified ensuite bathroom. Casey’s room, with the large queen-sized bed she was so eager to share, sat adjacent to the standard hallway bathroom, which featured a standard sink, toilet, and a bath-and-shower combo unit.

Casey lingered in the hallway, looking longingly from her bedroom door to the living room where my duffel bag sat on the pull-out sofa.

"I know it feels arbitrary, Case," I said gently, stepping over to give her a quick hug. "But the rules are absolute. Just until you turn eighteen."

"May 25th," Casey recited, giving a small, resilient nod. "I know. No loopholes for Tom."

"Exactly," Cassandra agreed, smoothly establishing the logistical boundaries for the night. "To ensure the perimeter remains legally and visually flawless, Casey will use her bedroom and the hallway bathroom to change. David and Liz, grab your bags and come into the master suite with me. We keep the sightlines entirely separated."

Casey grabbed her pajamas and disappeared into her room, clicking the door shut.

Liz and I hoisted our duffel bags and followed Cassandra down the hall into the master bedroom. The space was dominated by the sprawling king-sized mattress.

"You two are more than welcome to share this bed with me if the living room sofa gets too uncomfortable," Cass offered, parking her chair near the doorway of her ensuite bathroom. "There is plenty of square footage."

"We'll be fine in the living room for tonight," Liz smiled warmly, setting our bags on the edge of the mattress. "But we might take you up on that tomorrow. We're just happy to be here."

Cassandra offered a grateful nod before looking over at me. Her executive mask dropped completely, leaving only the practical, unfiltered reality of our sibling dynamic.

"David, I could use your help with the bathing and toileting tonight, if you don't mind," Cass asked quietly.

"Of course," I nodded immediately, stepping into the ensuite bathroom behind her.

Liz remained in the bedroom to unpack our sleepwear, entirely unfazed. She was a fully integrated member of the pack, and she understood that my physical presence was a functional asset to my sisters, not just an emotional one.

Cassandra’s bathroom was a masterpiece of accessibility, retrofitted entirely to accommodate the permanent reality of her 1999 axial load injury. The space featured a custom roll-under sink, heavy-duty grab bars bolted directly into the wall studs next to the toilet, and an oversized shower stall equipped with a sturdy transfer bench.

We had executed this routine dozens of times over the years. Because the hollow, pinned-together remnants of her heels and ankles simply could not support the weight of her body without catastrophic failure, transferring from the chair to the shower bench required immense upper-body strength and careful leverage. I acted as her physical anchor. I braced her chair, offered my arms for stability, and provided the secure, unyielding support she needed to safely navigate the transfers without exhausting her own system.

It was a quiet, highly coordinated process built on absolute trust. I handed her the towels, secured her chair, and made sure she was completely safely situated before stepping out to give her privacy.

Twenty minutes later, the entire apartment had successfully transitioned.

The four of us emerged back into the living room, all dressed in comfortable, oversized pajamas. The heavy winter rain was still drumming a steady rhythm against the apartment windows, but inside, the thermal envelope was perfect.

Liz and I climbed onto the sprawling pull-out sofa bed, pulling the heavy blankets over our laps. Casey immediately abandoned her bedroom, practically diving onto the mattress next to me. She curled her legs up, pressing her side firmly against my ribs, instantly locking into her deep-pressure baseline. Cassandra wheeled her chair right up to the edge of the mattress, resting her hand lightly on my knee to complete the circuit.

I grabbed the television remote and flipped the screen on, the ambient glow washing over the dark living room. We didn't really care what was playing. For the first time in months, my processor was completely silent. The perimeter was secure, the legal boundaries were flawless, and the entire pack was finally anchored exactly where we belonged.
