# Future Plot Point: The Kristine Timebomb

**Timeline Context:** Sometime after David and Liz break up, when David is dating Kristine. 

**The Catalyst:** Kristine is looking through David's laptop (or a physical photo album) and discovers the massive "puppy pile" photos from the 2004 Aquidneck Christmas. Specifically, she finds the photo where David is in the massive pile, Liz is happily smiling next to him, and Erin Delaney is physically leaning over and kissing David on the cheek. 

**The Confrontation:** Because Kristine is incredibly toxic, insecure, and possessive, she completely freaks out. She cannot mentally comprehend how David could let another woman (Erin) kiss him on the cheek while his girlfriend (Liz) is sitting right there. 

**The Conflict:** She views the Delaney sisters as a massive threat and attacks David over the photo. David has to try (and likely fail) to explain the "pack mentality" to her—that the Delaneys are his fiercely protective older sisters in every way that matters, and that Liz was so deeply secure in their relationship and integrated into the pack that she didn't possess an ounce of jealousy. 

**Narrative Purpose:** It serves as a massive, glaring red flag highlighting how completely incompatible Kristine is with David and his neurodivergent, fiercely loyal "pack" support system, acting as a direct contrast to how safe and unconditional Liz was.
