The gymnasium was transformed into a glittering sea of streamers and fairy lights, but to Courtney, it felt like a prison. She spent the entire night anchored near the punch bowl in the far corner, strictly adhering to the mandated fifty-foot distance from Matt's wheelchair across the floor. 

At 10:45 PM, the music faded out. Principal Bernard stepped up to the microphone on the small, carpeted stage, holding two envelopes. 

"Alright, seniors, the votes are in. Your 1999 Prom King and Queen are..." He opened the first envelope. "Matt Vance!"

A massive cheer erupted from the student body. Matt smiled warmly, pushing his manual wheelchair through the parting crowd until he reached the base of the stage. 

Bernard opened the second envelope. He glanced at the card, and for a split second, he physically froze. He looked up, his eyes sweeping across the gymnasium until they locked onto the far corner. 

"And... Courtney Evans."

The gym went dead silent. You could hear a pin drop. 

Courtney's heart stopped. The votes from her relentless campaigning weeks prior had held, locking in before the Omni-Q incident had destroyed everything. 

All eyes darted between her, Matt, and Principal Bernard. The tension was suffocating. Bernard tensed up, gripping the edges of the podium, fully prepared to stop Courtney from approaching the stage to enforce the mandate. 

Courtney remained frozen by the bleachers, terrified. She desperately missed him. But she was a Straight A student; she knew that if she moved forward and initiated contact, it was an automatic recommendation for expulsion. She was paralyzed, trapped between the profound desire to be near him and the absolute terror of ruining her future. 

But Matt didn't look at Bernard. He turned his wheelchair around to face her. 

He gripped the handrims of his chair and pushed forward, rolling away from the stage and directly across the empty dance floor. When Bernard stepped down to intervene, Matt looked up at him, firmly shook his head, and held up a single hand to signal *stop*. 

Because Matt was the protected party, actively crossing the room himself, Bernard made the agonizing, split-second administrative choice to stand down.

Matt wheeled himself straight to Courtney, intentionally breaking the physical boundary mandated by the school. Sensing her terror, he took the burden of the rule-breaking onto his own shoulders. He leaned forward in his chair and reached his arms out to her first.

Overwhelmed by a tidal wave of guilt and relief, Courtney finally moved. She leaned down and wrapped her arms tightly around him, burying her face against his shoulder. He couldn't speak to tell her that she was forgiven, but as he hugged her back, he didn't need to. It was a silent, profound moment of grace in front of the entire school.
