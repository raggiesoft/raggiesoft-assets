The bass from the final song of the night was still echoing through the gymnasium walls when Sarah pulled the accessible van into the school's circular drive. It was 11:30 PM, and the crisp May air offered a brief reprieve from the humid, crowded atmosphere inside.

Sarah stood by the entrance, wrapping her light jacket tighter around herself as students began filtering out. It didn't take long to spot Matt rolling toward her, Arthur Vance trailing dutifully a few steps behind. Matt looked sharp in his suit, but his posture was tense.

Instead of heading straight for the van's deployed ramp, Matt stopped just inside the lobby doors. He looked up at Sarah, then turned his wheelchair slightly, gesturing with a nod toward the corner of the lobby where Principal Bernard was monitoring the departing students.

Sarah, who held Joint and Several guardianship of Matt alongside their parents, immediately understood. She stepped inside the lobby, the noise of the departing crowd buzzing around them, and walked over to the principal, Matt rolling alongside her.

"Principal Bernard," Sarah said, keeping her tone polite but firm. "Matt told me about his meeting with you earlier this week. And from what I saw when I walked in, he clearly initiated contact with Courtney tonight. As his legal guardian, I'm formally asking what it will take to lift this No Contact Order. He wants his friend back."

Bernard sighed, running a hand over his face. He looked exhausted, the weight of the night taking its toll. "Sarah, please understand. I admire Matt's heart. But I told him the same thing I have to tell you—my hands are tied."

He lowered his voice, stepping slightly closer so they couldn't be overheard by the passing students. "The district is operating under absolute zero-tolerance right now. After the tragedy in Colorado last month, the superintendent's office is terrified of a negligence claim. Their only objective is keeping these two separated until graduation day when our jurisdiction ends."

"So that's it?" Sarah asked, her protective instincts flaring. "You're just going to run the clock out?"

Bernard glanced around the lobby, ensuring no other administrators were within earshot. He leaned in closer. "Officially? Yes. The order stands through the end of the year."

He paused, his eyes darting briefly to Matt before looking back at Sarah. 

"But *off the record*... if you want the district to back down before graduation, you have to remove their fear of a lawsuit. If your parents and Courtney's parents sit down together, draft a Joint Liability Release explicitly releasing the school from any negligence claims, and walk it directly into the superintendent's office... they might just grant a waiver. But it has to come from both families, united. That's the only way around the bureaucracy."

Sarah absorbed the information, nodding slowly. "A Joint Liability Release."

"Off the record," Bernard repeated firmly. He stepped back, reassuming his administrative posture. "Have a good night, Sarah. Good night, Matt."
