---
title: "Before the Distance: The Story of John & Erin"
---
An official five-song narrative novella written and published by **Ryan and Cassidy O'Connell** of [**The Stardust Engine**](https://stardust.raggiesoft.com) across their 2004 hometown concept album [**Progress Street**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2004-progress-street) and their 2007 archival compilation [**Lost Sounds**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2007-lost-sounds).

At **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)** in Blacksburg, Virginia, fraternal twins **John and Erin** navigate the steep **Ironstone** hills of campus in their power wheelchairs—living in a ground-floor accessible apartment on **Progress Street NE**, just two blocks down the sidewalk from the chaotic **Blue Ridge Transit** bus lineup beneath **The Keystone Bridge** on **Commonwealth Mall**.

When a manipulative relationship isolates John after a Friday-night campus speed-dating mixer in **Kincaid Hall** ([**"Seconds Into Forever"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2004-progress-street?track=1-03-seconds-into-forever) and [**"Thunder in the Rain"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2004-progress-street?track=1-04-thunder-in-the-rain)), Erin drives her power wheelchair directly onto the rain-slicked sidewalk of Commonwealth Mall to block the front wheelchair ramp of a departing bus right before the steel doors close ([**"Before the Distance"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2004-progress-street?track=1-08-before-the-distance)), locks a physical perimeter to bring her brother the two blocks home to Progress Street NE ([**"Sanctuary in the Storm"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2007-lost-sounds?track=1-13-sanctuary-in-the-storm)), and stands shoulder-to-shoulder with John when he returns the favor the following spring ([**"Not Her"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2007-lost-sounds?track=1-14-not-her)).
