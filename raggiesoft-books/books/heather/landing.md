At seventeen years old in Tidewater Virginia, Heather Collins thought being a good person came effortlessly. Having grown up right alongside her younger, non-verbal developmentally disabled cousin Wesley, she never flinched at wheelchairs or quiet routines—which was why sixteen-year-old fraternal twins Nathaniel and Katie Prairie trusted her inside their fiercely guarded world at Northwood High School, and why fifteen-year-old Wesley treasured Saturday pretzel outings at Emerald Creek Mall with Nate, the very first peer-age guy friend Wesley had ever had.

Then sixty seconds of teenage humiliation and a classmate's malicious lie shattered everything. Panicking in a crowded high-school hallway without letting Nate or Katie finish a single sentence, Heather threw away eighteen months of trust—never pausing to realize that while she had speech, a driver's license, and hallways full of friends, non-verbal Wesley had zero phone bridge of his own to the friend she had just severed from his life.

*The Story of Heather Lindstrom: Scales, Tails, and Safe People* follows Heather from the gut-wrenching Saturday morning Wesley blew up at her in Chesapeake through their family's return home to Minnesota, stepping up at nineteen as Wesley's legal guardian, falling in love with wildlife biologist Dan Lindstrom as an inseparable package deal, giving birth to severely disabled non-verbal twins Jonas and Nora and their fearless younger sister Molly, and spending ten humble years strictly behind the camera lens building *North Lion Scales & Tails*—proving that who you become after your worst mistake is the truest measure of a safe person.
