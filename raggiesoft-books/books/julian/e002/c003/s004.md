The Colonial Parkway was a twenty-three-mile stretch of historical, National Park Service roadway that connected the Historic Triangle. It was completely devoid of commercial trucks, billboards, and stoplights.

More importantly, it wasn't paved with smooth asphalt. The Parkway was constructed of exposed aggregate concrete.

The moment Gabriel merged the heavy wheelchair van onto the road near Jamestown, the tires hit the textured surface. A deep, rhythmic, continuous thump-thump-thump instantly vibrated through the floorboards and up into the frame of Julian’s manual wheelchair.

It was a heavy, sensory vibration—a massive, full-body tactile input that acted exactly like a deep-pressure cuddle.

Julian’s rigid posture instantly melted. He let his head fall back against his headrest, closing his eyes as the deep, mechanical hum of the road traveled through his bones.

"How's that, Julep?" Gwendolyn asked quietly from the seat across from him, watching the tension bleed out of his face.

Julian didn't type. He just offered a wide, relaxed smile, opening his eyes to look out the window.

The van cruised at a steady, peaceful forty-five miles an hour. Outside, the world was a blur of towering, skeletal winter trees and the cold, gray waters of the James River cutting through the shoreline.

There were no flashing police lights. There were no national news vans. There was just the quiet, insulated warmth of the cabin, the grounding vibration of the road, and the absolute certainty that every single person in the vehicle was willing to tear the world apart to keep him safe.

"<lang="fr-CA" trans="It's beautiful, isn't it?">C'est beau, n'est-ce pas?</lang>" Maria whispered in flawless French, resting her chin on his shoulder as they looked out at the water together.

Julian reached over, lacing his fingers through hers.

He didn't have his Blue Line bus ride, and he didn't have his normal routine back yet. The trauma of the abduction was still a heavy, dark shadow that they would have to fight off for a long time.

But as the family van followed the winding, textured road toward Yorktown, carrying the newly minted Rowlands family away from the wreckage of their past, Julian knew he had exactly what he needed.

The perimeter was moving with him.
