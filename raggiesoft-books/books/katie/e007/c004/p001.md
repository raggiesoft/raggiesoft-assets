## Monday, July 30, 2007, 2:00 PM — Dr. Evelyn Carter's Exam Room & Peeling Off the Bandage

"You're safe right here with me and Dad, Katie," I whispered at two o'clock on Monday afternoon inside Exam Room Two at Dr. Evelyn Carter's family medicine office on Kempsville Road, keeping both of my arms wrapped tightly around my twin sister's shoulders across the gap between our parked SwiftChairs while Dad stood right behind us before driving back to Northern Virginia.

![Inside a warm ground-floor examination room at Dr. Evelyn Carter's family medicine practice in Virginia Beach in late July 2007, twenty-two-year-old fraternal twins Katie and Nathaniel Prairie sit side by side in their black motorized SwiftChair power wheelchairs holding hands tightly across the gap between their chairs with their father David standing supportively right behind them while Dr. Evelyn Carter, seated on a rolling stool in a white lab coat and blue nitrile gloves, gently peels the white MedStrip and gauze bandage off the healed bridge of Katie's nose]({{CDN}}/raggiesoft-books/images/scenes/katie/b006/mar_2007_dr_carter_office_medstrip_removal_annual_physical_katie_nate_david.jpg)

Listening with visible indignation as Katie and I recounted every detail of our twelve days in Fishersville—from staffers grabbing our wheelchair push-handles and pitching ValueMart door-greeter tracks to the pool assault, the eleven-twenty-five Rec Hall closet ambush, and the administration whisking Chris into a van without calling police after hurting Natalie—Dr. Carter uncapped her fountain pen and documented permanently in both of our medical charts that any institutional program separating the two of us or grabbing our wheelchairs without consent was medically contraindicated.

Then, snapping on blue nitrile gloves and moistening the protective tape with warm sterile saline, Dr. Carter carefully peeled away the outer gauze and sterile MedStrip wound-closure strips across Katie's healed nasal bridge.

"That knitted together into a clean, flat seam that will fade completely over the next couple of weeks, Katie," Dr. Carter smiled, handing her a small hand mirror.

## Wednesday, August 1 – Friday, August 17, 2007 — Morning PHP With Tina, Sheila, and Jim & Our First Paid Client Checks

![Inside their shared bedroom on Arvin Road in Kempsville in summer 2007, while the wooden queen bed beneath the framed blue butterfly print sits completely untouched behind their two parked black SwiftChair power wheelchairs, twins Katie and Nathaniel Prairie cuddle closely in a soothing two-arm embrace inside their shared sleeping bag on the floor of the open walk-in closet.]({{CDN}}/raggiesoft-books/images/scenes/katie/b006/mar_2007_arvin_road_bedroom_closet_full_cuddle_sanctuary_katie_nate.jpg)

Waking up safe inside our own carpeted closet sleeping bag on Arvin Road—while down the hallway Heather drank her morning tea getting ready for new-teacher orientation in Virginia Beach City Public Schools' High School Adapted Curriculum program and Wesley munched warm toast right beside her—melted the last shadow of Barton Hall out of our house.

Every weekday morning at nine o'clock sharp in our twelve-by-twelve dual-desk office, Tina, Sheila, Jim, and Sarah chimed online from Fishersville while Katie and I mastered PHP 5 session arrays, SQL foreign keys, and relational normalization directly on our own Linux staging server—rebuilding *Ideas for Coastal Virginia Transit* from top to bottom as a dynamic database application and opening *Prairie Twin Interactive, LLC* for commercial contracts.

We didn't need a corporate HR director to tell us we were "the right skill match." When small business owners across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, and Chesapeake hired *Prairie Twin Interactive* over email and phone to build their PHP catalogs and normalize their inventory databases, the code didn't care that the two developers writing it sat side by side in power wheelchairs. The Friday afternoon in mid-August when Katie and I rolled into The Parcel Center, unlocked Box 214, and pulled out our very first three client checks payable to *Prairie Twin Interactive, LLC*, Katie held the envelopes up in the sunlight with tears of pride shining in her blue eyes.
