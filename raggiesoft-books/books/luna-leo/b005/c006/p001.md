They woke refreshed the next morning, grabbed a quick motel breakfast, and headed straight to the Coastal Wonders Marine Center just down the road. Navigating the parking lot and entrance with Leo's wheelchair was straightforward, and the accessible design of the facility made getting around easy. The smell of saltwater hit them the moment they stepped through the gates, carrying the distant, barking sounds of sea lions.

The twins were immediately captivated. Leo parked his chair right up against the thick acrylic glass of the main indoor reef tanks, watching mesmerized as colorful schools of fish darted past intricately constructed coral habitats. Luna was fascinated by the playful African penguins in the outdoor exhibit, laughing at their waddling walks and their shockingly sleek, torpedo-like dives into the water.

The highlight for everyone, however, was the sprawling Arctic Coast exhibit featuring the beluga whales. The massive outdoor pool was designed to mimic a rocky northern shoreline, complete with underwater viewing areas. They watched the large, graceful white whales glide through the cool, clear water, their haunting, melodic calls echoing slightly in the subterranean viewing chamber. Leo typed "SO BIG" on his AAC device, his eyes wide with wonder as one of the belugas swam right up to the glass, seemingly just as curious about Leo as Leo was about him. 

Stella found herself just as enthralled, leaning against the railing next to Luna and enjoying the simple pleasure of watching the twins experience something new and exciting together. Even Arthur and Eleanor seemed relaxed, pointing out different sea creatures and sharing the moment without any of the heavy, looming tension of the impending visit to Middletown. It was a perfect, lighthearted break, a buffer of pure joy before the more emotionally charged part of their trip back to Rhode Island. 

After a couple of enjoyable hours exploring the exhibits, grabbing a quick souvenir, and watching the sea lion demonstration, they headed back to the cars, the Sentinel easily accommodating Leo's chair in the trunk once more as they prepared for the final leg of their journey.
