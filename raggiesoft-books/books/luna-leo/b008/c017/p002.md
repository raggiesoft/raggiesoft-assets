Just as the adrenaline from Leo's nightmare began to fully dissipate, a sharp knock echoed from the hotel room door. 

Stella practically scrambled off the bed, smoothing down her pajamas. "That's breakfast," she announced, checking her watch. "Thank god I pre-ordered it last night. I don't think any of us have the energy to hunt for food this morning."

She opened the door to reveal a hotel attendant pushing a stainless steel cart laden with silver-domed plates, a carafe of hot coffee, and a pitcher of orange juice. The smell of bacon, fresh pancakes, and roasted potatoes instantly filled the room, sending a collective, much-needed wave of comfort over the pack.

As Stella tipped the attendant and wheeled the cart toward the small dining table by the window, Olivia grabbed her phone from the nightstand. She tapped out a quick text message to Eleanor and Isabel.

<sms>We're awake! Leo had a pretty rough night, so we're taking it slow. Just got our room service.</sms>

Her phone buzzed almost immediately with a reply from Isabel. 

<sms>Oh no! Poor Leo! Tell him we're sending hugs. The conference room is officially ours for the day. We've got our own food down here, so whenever you guys are done with breakfast, come on down to B2. We're building a fort.</sms>

Olivia read the text aloud. A tiny, genuine smile finally broke through the lingering anxiety on Leo's face. 

"A fort," Luna said, perking up as she finally rolled off Leo and stretched her back. "Well, we can't let Oliver and the twins build a fort without our structural engineering expertise. That's a collapse waiting to happen."

"Exactly," Stella agreed, lifting the silver domes off the plates. "Eat up, everyone. We have a bunker to fortify."

They gathered around the small table. After the Mexican food incident the day before, Stella had intentionally ordered a very gentle, simple breakfast for Leo: plain scrambled eggs, a side of toast, and some sliced bananas. Nothing greasy, nothing heavy, just easily digestible fuel.

He ate quietly, his hands still a little shaky, but the presence of his pack and the promise of a safe, enclosed day in the sub-basement was clearly working its magic. The terrifying isolation of his dream was fading, replaced by the warm reality of his family.

By 9:30 AM, the plates were cleared, they had all brushed their teeth, and they were dressed in their most comfortable, casual clothes. 

Stella grabbed the room key, and Olivia took her familiar place pushing Leo's wheelchair. They walked out into the carpeted hallway and headed straight for the elevators.

"Going down," Luna announced, hitting the call button. "Destination: B2."
