Emerging from *The Ghostly Estate* back into the bright California sunlight, Leo took a deep, refreshing breath. The sensory-friendly darkness of the ride had completely reset his nervous system, washing away the lingering anxiety from earlier that morning. 

Olivia pushed his wheelchair onto the shaded, brick-paved path of the French Quarter, the sounds of a distant jazz band wafting through the warm air. 

"Well, that was delightfully spooky," Stella said, adjusting her sunglasses. "What's next on the agenda, captain?"

Leo looked down at his NovaVox, his fingers flying across the keys. 

<aac>Buccaneer's Bounty,</aac> the synthesized voice announced. <aac>I want to see the pirates.</aac>

"Pirates it is," Olivia agreed with a warm smile, angling his wheelchair down the path toward the massive, Spanish-style fortress at the edge of the square. 

They had barely made it past a wrought-iron balcony when it happened. 

There was a low, guttural rumble—like a heavy freight train passing directly beneath their feet. Then, the brick pavement suddenly shifted with a sharp, sickening jolt. 

Leo gasped, instinctively grabbing the armrests of his wheelchair. Beside him, Luna stumbled slightly, her hands shooting out to catch herself on Olivia's arm. The wrought-iron street lamps above them rattled violently for a few seconds, the glass panes clinking together, and then it was over just as quickly as it had begun. 

"What in the world was that?" Stella asked, her voice tight with confusion. She looked around, expecting to see a massive parade float or some elaborate ride mechanism that had malfunctioned. 

"Did someone drop something?" Luna asked, her eyes wide behind her glasses. 

Being from Virginia, neither the twins, their older sister Stella, nor Olivia had ever experienced anything quite like it. But all around them, the California locals were already murmuring the answer. 

"Earthquake," a passing guest said casually, not even breaking their stride. "Felt like a three-point-something. Just a baby one."

"An *earthquake*?" Olivia breathed out, her grip tightening on the handles of Leo's chair. "That was an earthquake?"

Before they could even fully process the concept of the ground moving beneath them, the park's ambient background music abruptly cut out. A calm, pre-recorded voice echoed over the PA system. 

*"May I have your attention, please. The Enchanted Kingdom has just experienced a minor earthquake. For your safety, all attractions have been temporarily halted while our maintenance crews conduct mandatory safety inspections. Please remain calm and listen to the instructions of cast members in your area."*

True to the announcement, cast members were already emerging from the ride entrances. Two cast members wearing pirate costumes stepped out in front of the entrance to *Buccaneer's Bounty*, unfurling a thick rope across the queue. 

"Sorry, folks," one of the pirates announced to the gathering crowd. "The captain's ordered a full inspection of the ship. Buccaneer's Bounty is temporarily closed until further notice." 

Leo stared at the closed rope, his shoulders slumping. He looked down at his NovaVox. 

<aac>Ride is closed?</aac> he typed, looking back up at Olivia for confirmation. 

"It sounds like all the rides are closed right now, buddy," Olivia said gently, kneeling beside his chair. "Disneyland protocol. They have to inspect every single ride track to make sure the earthquake didn't break anything. It might take a few hours before anything reopens." 

Leo let out a heavy sigh, but he didn't tense up. The morning's meltdown was still fresh in his memory, but this was different. He wasn't being tricked; the Earth itself had just decided to shake. 

"Well, on the bright side, the restaurants don't have ride tracks," Stella pointed out, trying to find the silver lining. "And it's just about lunchtime. Who's hungry for some gumbo?"
