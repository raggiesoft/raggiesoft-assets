At exactly 11:32 PM, the quiet darkness of the hotel room was broken by a sudden, frantic whimper. 

The pack had fallen into their usual, highly coordinated sleeping arrangement. Stella and Olivia were asleep on either side of the massive hotel bed, serving as physical boundaries. In the center, Leo was on his back, while Luna lay directly stomach-to-stomach on top of him, providing the heavy, grounding deep-pressure therapy his spastic muscles required to sleep. 

Luna was jolted awake when Leo's chest began heaving underneath her. 

He was hyperventilating, his arms twitching wildly as he let out a series of terrified, suffocating gasps. He was trapped in a nightmare. 

Because she was already pinned directly on top of him, Luna didn't even have to move to intervene. She immediately adjusted her weight, pressing down more firmly against his chest to anchor him, and slipped her hands up to cup his face. 

"Leo. Leo, hey," Luna whispered fiercely, keeping her voice low but authoritative as she pulled him out of the sleep terror. "Wake up. I've got you. You're right here."

Leo gasped, his eyes flying open in the dark. He stared up at Luna's face hovering inches above his own, his chest rising and falling violently against hers. 

Stella and Olivia were awake instantly. Without a word, Stella threw the covers back and hurried over to the hotel mini-fridge. 

Leo was shaking violently. He couldn't reach his tablet, but his hands managed to weakly sign a few frantic, disjointed words in the dark. 

<sgn>Car. Away. Trapped.</sgn>

"A car?" Luna asked softly, keeping her weight firmly on his chest. "You were trapped in a car?"

Leo nodded frantically, tears spilling out of the corners of his eyes and soaking into the hotel pillow. He signed again, his movements clumsy from the sheer terror. <sgn>Faceless girl. Unfamiliar SUV. Driving across the country. Couldn't escape. Pack was gone.</sgn>

"Oh, buddy," Olivia whispered, sitting up and reaching across the bed to gently stroke his hair. "It was just a dream. Nobody is taking you anywhere. We're right here."

Stella returned to the bed holding a plastic hotel cup filled with ice water and a straw. "Alright, let him sit up," she murmured. 

Luna shifted her weight and rolled off of her twin, sitting cross-legged next to him. Olivia slipped her arms under Leo's shoulders, gently hoisting him into a seated position against the headboard. 

Stella sat on the edge of the mattress and guided the straw to his lips. "Slow sips. Breathe."

Leo closed his eyes, taking a shaky sip of the freezing water. The terrifying vision of the SUV, the endless highway, and the complete, suffocating isolation felt so real that it made his chest ache. He pulled his mouth away from the straw and let out a quiet, heartbroken sob. 

Instantly, the three women closed ranks around him. Stella kept her hand on his cheek, Olivia kept her arm tightly around his shoulders, and Luna leaned against his side, wrapping both of her arms around his waist. They didn't say anything else. They just held him in the dark, letting him cry out the residual terror of the nightmare while Stella periodically offered him another sip of water. 

For twenty minutes, they sat in the quiet solidarity of the fortress. 

By 11:56 PM, Leo's breathing had finally slowed. He took one last, long drink from the straw and gave Stella a small, exhausted nod. 

"Better?" Stella asked softly, setting the empty cup on the nightstand. 

Leo nodded again. He was completely drained. 

Olivia gently lowered him back down against the pillows. Stella climbed back under the covers on his left side. Luna didn't hesitate. She crawled right back into the center of the bed and lowered her body onto his, settling stomach-to-stomach on top of him. She rested her chin on his shoulder and draped her arms heavily over his sides. 

Leo let out a long, shuddering breath, the immense weight of his sister pressing him securely into the mattress. Within minutes, grounded by the physical reality of his pack surrounding him, he drifted back into a deep, dreamless sleep.
