After taking one look at the vast, sun-baked, empty concrete plazas of *Golden State Adventure*, the pack unanimously decided to head into the classic, tree-lined gates of **The Enchanted Kingdom**. 

They walked down the nostalgic, bustling storefronts of *Main Avenue* and immediately took a right into the retro-futuristic landscape of **Future City**. 

As soon as they crossed the threshold, Leo saw it. Towering over the back of the land was a massive, sleek white architectural dome. The sign out front read **Cosmic Peak**. 

Leo excitedly tapped his NovaVox, holding it up for Stella to read. 

<aac>I want to go on the spaceship simulator. It looks massive.</aac>

Stella stopped pushing his wheelchair. She looked up at the massive white dome, then looked down at Leo's tablet, her expression instantly softening into deep sympathy. 

"Oh, Leo," Stella said softly, crouching down next to his wheel. "It's not a simulator, buddy. It's a roller coaster."

Leo frowned, typing quickly. <aac>But it's inside. And it's a spaceship.</aac>

"It's an indoor roller coaster in the dark," Olivia added gently, kneeling on his other side. "It's incredibly fast, Leo. The tracks have sharp, violent drops and intense lateral turns. Your core strength isn't stable enough to keep you upright. The G-forces would literally throw you out of the seat."

Leo stared at the massive white dome. He had been so excited. He had mentally prepared himself for a slow, immersive journey through the stars. To be told that he physically couldn't ride the most iconic attraction in the park—that his body was the limiting factor—was a devastating blow. 

The disappointment hit him like a tidal wave. 

A sharp, high-pitched whine escaped his throat. He dropped his tablet onto his lap and clamped his hands fiercely over his ears. He began to rock forward and backward in his wheelchair, his breathing accelerating into ragged gasps. 

"Hey, it's okay," Stella said, reaching out to touch his knee. 

Leo let out a loud, heartbroken sob, overwhelmed by the crowds, the noise of *Future City*, and the crushing disappointment. 

Standing just a few feet away, Luna watched her twin brother break down. Being highly attuned to him, the sheer intensity of his grief immediately bypassed all of her own emotional defenses. Within seconds, Luna spiraled into a sympathetic meltdown. She dropped to her knees on the hot concrete right next to his wheelchair, wrapping her arms around her head and bursting into heavy, breathless tears.

The twin feedback loop was instant and terrifying. 

Leo heard Luna crying, which escalated his panic, causing him to scream louder. Luna heard Leo screaming, which terrified her, causing her to sob harder. They were trapped in a rapidly deteriorating emotional spiral right in the middle of a heavily trafficked theme park corridor. 

"We can't stay here," Olivia said urgently, her eyes darting around the crowded, noisy plaza as tourists began to point and stare. "We have to get them somewhere quiet. Now."

"Excuse me, do you need help?" a calm voice interrupted. 

A cast member in a crisp *Future City* uniform stepped through the growing crowd. He took one look at Leo rocking in his wheelchair and Luna sobbing on the concrete, immediately recognizing the severity of the sensory crisis. 

"Follow me," he said firmly, stepping in front of them to part the crowd. "We're going to the Medical Pavilion. It's just around the corner on Main Avenue."

With the cast member running interference to clear the pathway, Olivia pushed Leo's wheelchair at a dead sprint, while Stella physically wrapped an arm around Luna and practically carried the sobbing girl. 

They rushed out of *Future City* and ducked into the quiet, heavily air-conditioned lobby of the Medical Pavilion. The cast member gave a quick nod to the nurse at the front desk, who didn't ask any questions. 

"Room four," the nurse said gently, pointing down the hall. 

They rushed the twins into the small, private, clinical room. It was spotlessly clean and incredibly quiet. The nurse stepped inside just long enough to turn off the harsh overhead fluorescent lights, leaving only a dim lamp glowing in the corner, before quietly pulling the heavy door shut behind them. 

Instantly, the noise, the music, and the overwhelming chaos of *The Enchanted Kingdom* vanished. In the dim, safe quiet of the private room, Olivia and Stella dropped to the floor, ready to start breaking the feedback loop.
