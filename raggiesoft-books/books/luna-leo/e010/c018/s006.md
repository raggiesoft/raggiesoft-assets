The walk from the Hollywood/Highland LATA station was short, but every step felt like a marathon. The bright, chaotic neon lights of Hollywood Boulevard blurred past them as they dragged their luggage the remaining few blocks to the Voyage Inn Express. 

While Olivia, Luna, and Leo collapsed into a cluster of armchairs in the small, brightly lit lobby, Stella marched up to the front desk. She handled the check-in with ruthless efficiency, slapping her credit card on the counter and returning a few minutes later with two plastic keycards.

"Room 412," she said, motioning toward the elevators. "Let's go."

The room was exactly what they expected: a standard, aggressively beige corporate hotel room with two queen beds separated by a small nightstand. It was incredibly generic, lacking the gold-trimmed luxury of the Sovereign Regency, but to the exhausted pack, it looked like paradise.

They quickly cycled through the small bathroom, brushing their teeth in a silent, exhausted haze and changing into soft pajamas. 

Luna emerged from the bathroom last, rubbing her eyes under her thick glasses. She looked at the two queen beds, then looked at Leo. 

"I need the pressure tonight," Luna mumbled, her voice thick with exhaustion. "The train vibration is still trapped in my bones."

"Dogpile," Olivia confirmed, already pulling the heavy duvet back on the bed closest to the window. 

Luna climbed onto the mattress and laid perfectly flat on her back in the dead center of the bed. She looked up at Leo, who was sitting on the edge of the mattress. "You're my weighted blanket tonight."

Leo gave a tired, understanding nod. With Olivia's help, he shifted his body and laid down directly on top of his twin sister, stomach-to-stomach. He let his entire body go limp, dropping his full, dead weight onto Luna's torso. 

Luna let out a long, ragged exhale as the crushing pressure hit her chest. Her rigid muscles instantly relaxed against the mattress. She wrapped her arms loosely around Leo's back to secure him in place. 

But two people weren't enough to complete the pack's ritual. 

Stella climbed onto the left side of the queen bed, sliding under the covers and pressing her entire body tightly against Luna and Leo's flanks. Olivia immediately took the right side, mirroring Stella's position and throwing her heavy arm over Leo's back. 

It was a standard queen bed, utterly failing to accommodate four full-grown adults. Legs were tangled, shoulders were crammed together, and the edges of the mattress dipped precariously under their combined weight. It was ridiculously tight, but the immense, crushing, 360-degree deep pressure was exactly what the twins needed. 

Trapped in the center of the heavy, breathing cocoon of his family, Leo felt the lingering vibrations of the twelve-hour train ride finally melt out of his nervous system. 

"Goodnight, pack," Stella murmured into the darkness. 

Leo didn't even have the energy to tap on his NovaVox. He just buried his face into Luna's shoulder and let the darkness take him.
