The alarm clocks in Room 412 went off aggressively early. It was July 7th, their final full day in Los Angeles, and Stella had packed the schedule with the biggest, most exhausting destination of the entire trip: **The Enchanted Resort** down in Anaheim. 

Because they didn't have a rental car, getting from Hollywood to Anaheim in 2003 required a massive, multi-agency transit expedition. 

They grabbed a quick, quiet breakfast in the hotel lobby before walking out into the cool morning air. They swiped their transit cards and took the LATA Red Line subway down into the massive, echoing transit hub of LA Union Station. 

"Alright," Stella said, looking up at the massive departure boards in the historic waiting room. "We need the SCR Orange County Line, heading South."

They navigated through the tunnels to the heavy rail platforms, where a massive, silver bilevel commuter train was idling. Because it was a heavy commuter rail system, the ADA boarding required a conductor to manually deploy a heavy metal bridge plate over the gap. 

Olivia pushed Leo's wheelchair onto the lower level of the train, locking his brakes into the designated tie-down area while Luna and Stella grabbed the adjacent padded seats. 

The ride south into Orange County was smooth and surprisingly fast. Leo watched out the large windows as the dense, concrete sprawl of downtown Los Angeles slowly gave way to wide, suburban freeways and industrial parks. 

Forty-five minutes later, the conductor announced their arrival at the Anaheim station. They rolled off the massive train and immediately had to hustle to the adjacent bus loop, where a bright blue, local transit shuttle was waiting. 

The bus driver lowered the hydraulic ramp, and Leo rolled on. It was a short, two-mile drive down wide, palm-tree-lined boulevards before the bus pulled into the massive, bustling transportation esplanade of the resort. 

"We made it," Luna grinned, stretching her arms as they unloaded from the bus. 

They stood in the center of the massive plaza. To their left were the classic, nostalgic floral gates of **The Enchanted Kingdom**. To their right were the towering, concrete entrance letters of the brand-new **Golden State Adventure** park. 

"So," Stella said, adjusting her backpack and looking between the two massive theme parks. "Which gate do we hit first?"
