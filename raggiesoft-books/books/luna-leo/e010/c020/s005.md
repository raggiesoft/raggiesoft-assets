Feeling significantly better after the cold pineapple soft serve and the quiet, air-conditioned sanctuary of the animatronic birds, the pack ventured back out into the bright California sun. 

As they walked past the central hub, the massive, snow-capped peak of **Glacier Mountain** came into view. The iconic bobsled roller coaster roared down the side of the artificial mountain, carrying screaming riders and splashing into a glacial pool at the bottom. 

Instantly, both Stella and Olivia tensed up. Olivia tightened her grip on the handles of Leo's wheelchair, while Stella casually shifted her weight, mentally and physically bracing to catch another massive meltdown. 

Leo stopped pushing his joystick. He sat in his wheelchair and stared up at the roaring bobsleds. 

For a long moment, nobody breathed. 

Leo let out a heavy, disappointed sigh. He looked up at Olivia, his hands coming up to his chest. 

<aac>Sad,</aac> Leo typed into his NovaVox, his facial expression matching the heavy emotion. <aac>But okay. I know it rollercoaster.</aac>

Stella let out an audible, massive breath of relief, her shoulders instantly dropping. Olivia leaned over and kissed the top of Leo's messy brown hair. 

"I'm proud of you, buddy," Olivia whispered. 

That was the key to Leo's neurology. It wasn't the rides themselves that triggered his massive meltdowns—it was the sudden, crushing shift in expectations. He had believed *Cosmic Peak* was a simulator, and the sudden realization that it was a roller coaster had shattered his reality. But because he *knew* Glacier Mountain was a coaster, he had already processed the disappointment. He was sad, but he was completely safe. 

"Let's go find some ghosts," Stella declared, leading the pack toward the shaded, wrought-iron balconies of the park's **French Quarter**. 

They bypassed the long, winding standby queue for **The Ghostly Estate** and checked in with a cast member at the exit. Because the massive show building for the ride was actually located outside the park's perimeter berm, guests had to be transported underground to access it. 

A cast member escorted Leo's wheelchair directly into the estate's famous octagonal "Stretching Room." 

"Is this room actually stretching?" Luna whispered, her eyes wide as the ceiling seemed to rise and the portraits on the walls elongated to reveal sinister fates. 

<aac>Elevator,</aac> Leo typed excitedly, feeling the subtle rumble beneath his wheels. <aac>We going down.</aac>

He was right. Unlike its counterpart parks on the east coast, *The Enchanted Kingdom's* stretching room was a massive, high-capacity hydraulic elevator designed to lower guests beneath the train tracks. 

The ride was an instant hit. Leo loved the smooth, continuous-loading "doom buggies," the dancing holograms in the ballroom, and the dark, gothic atmosphere. He especially loved the disembodied voice of the Ghost Host echoing through the buggy's speakers as they passed the graveyard. 

*"We have nine hundred and ninety-nine restless spirits here,"* the voice practically purred. *"But there's always room for one more... any volunteers?"*

Leo beamed, tapping his chest excitedly. He was totally volunteering. 

When the ride ended, a cast member guided them right back onto the Stretching Room elevator to lift Leo's wheelchair back up to ground level. They emerged back into the bright sunlight of the French Quarter, the morning's trauma finally behind them.
