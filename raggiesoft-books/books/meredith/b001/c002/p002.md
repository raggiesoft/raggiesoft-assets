# Part 2: The Acoustic Clarification – 12:15 PM

By noon, the slow, peaceful morning had transitioned into a highly coordinated military operation. The Vanguard assembled in the driveway around Meredith’s practical sedan.

Meredith and Morgan operated like a well-oiled pit crew. Meredith took the driver’s side, expertly transferring Kate into the backseat while managing the tubing of her day bag. Morgan handled the passenger side, assisting David into the seat right next to his cousin. In a matter of seconds, both manual wheelchairs were collapsed, stacked, and secured in the spacious trunk.

Meredith climbed behind the wheel, engaged the clutch, and shifted into first gear. As they pulled out of the Delaney Street driveway and headed toward the CCHR Virginia Beach campus, Kate tapped a specific, questioning rhythm against the back of Meredith's seat.

"Don't worry," Meredith said, glancing at Kate in the rearview mirror. "I know what you're asking. We've refined the protocol."

Morgan turned around in the passenger seat, carefully keeping her silk bonnet from snagging on the headrest. "Just to be absolutely clear on the rules of engagement today, Kate: we are not dragging anyone into a bathroom. That’s kidnapping."

Kate nodded slowly, offering a deadpan blink. *Glad we established that.*

"Exactly," Morgan continued, a wicked grin spreading across her face. "Acoustic Warfare is a strictly mobile operation. If some guy tries to isolate you in the hallway or the student lounge, Meredith and I will simply execute a flanking maneuver. We will stand on either side of him and deploy maximum-volume, wet fog-horn noises in stereo."

David slapped his knee in the back seat, letting out a sharp, breathy giggle.

"We bring the Battle Shits directly to the customer," Meredith agreed, downshifting as they approached a red light. "Right out in the open. By the time we hit the second volley, he'll be questioning reality and running for the exits."

Kate buried her face in her hands, shaking her head. Through the gaps in her fingers, she was smiling. The sheer absurdity of her cousins threatening to publicly fart on an unsuspecting college student was ridiculous, but it completely eradicated her anxiety. A predator looking for a quiet, vulnerable target would never survive an encounter with the twins' chaotic energy.

Ten minutes later, Meredith pulled into the sprawling, suburban CCHR Virginia Beach campus. Unlike the vertical concrete towers of downtown Norfolk, the VB campus was a wide collection of brick buildings connected by open courtyards and massive parking lots.

Meredith parked in an accessible space near the main academic building. The twins jumped out, quickly retrieving the manual wheelchairs from the trunk. They transferred David and Kate with their usual clinical efficiency, ensuring Kate's leg bag was completely hidden beneath the hem of her jeans.

"Alright, Vanguard," Meredith said, slamming the trunk shut. "Formation."

They moved toward the glass entrance doors as a unified front. Kate rolled in the center, her hands gripping her push rims. David wheeled himself right beside her, his travel keyboard resting securely on his lap. Meredith and Morgan took the outer flanks, walking with their shoulders squared and their eyes scanning the crowds of college freshmen.

As they entered the air-conditioned hallway, Kate felt a profound wave of security wash over her. Students automatically stepped aside, giving the four of them a wide berth. No one was going to try and catch her alone today.

They navigated to the B-Wing, finding Kate's Student Development classroom with twenty minutes to spare. Unlike David's room with its tiny, restrictive bolted desks, this room had been recently updated with wide, modular tables.

Kate wheeled into a spot near the front, and David parked right next to her. Meredith and Morgan immediately claimed the two chairs directly behind them, establishing a physical barrier between their cousins and the rest of the room.

David typed quickly on his keyboard, hitting the trigger to send the text through his OmniTalk 3000.

"Perimeter secured," the robotic voice announced quietly to the group.

Morgan leaned forward, resting her elbows on the table behind Kate. "Let's see what the VB campus has to offer. My vocal cords are warmed up and ready for stereo."
