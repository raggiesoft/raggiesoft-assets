# Part 5: The Domestic Delusion (19:30)

The jarring transition from the bedroom back to the cramped kitchenette left Johnny’s analytical mind spinning in a silent, desperate void.

To an outside observer, the scene might have looked entirely mundane. Vanessa had wheeled him up to the small laminate dining table. She was standing at the stove, stirring a pot of canned tomato soup, the cheap fluorescent overhead light buzzing softly.

But for Johnny, the environment was a terrifying paradox. His physical sovereignty had just been inexplicably breached, yet here she was, humming softly to herself as if she were simply preparing a standard evening meal. She hadn't logged a single piece of medical data. She hadn't checked his O2 saturation, despite Victoria’s explicit mandate for hourly monitoring. She had completely abandoned the guise of the assessment.

Vanessa brought a bowl to the table and pulled up a chair beside him. She looked exhausted. Dark circles were beginning to form under her eyes—the physical toll of orchestrating a multi-county felony, compounded by a biological shift she didn't even realize was happening within her own body. Yet, despite her visible fatigue, her focus on Johnny was intensely, suffocatingly absolute.

She dipped the spoon into the soup and lifted it to his mouth.

At the Keep, mealtime assistance was a dignified, efficient process. Amanda or Sarah would sit beside him, engaging him in conversation about server arrays or rock opera lore, allowing him to set the pace.

Vanessa’s approach was deeply, uncomfortably intimate. She fed him slowly, her eyes tracking the movement of his jaw with a glazed, romantic fixation. Whenever a drop of soup missed his mouth, she didn't use a napkin; she reached out with her bare thumb, wiping his lower lip with a lingering, possessive touch that made his core spasticity flare against the heavy nylon of his wheelchair seat.

This is a civilian domicile. This is not a medical procedure, his brain repeated, the internal logic engine flashing a continuous system error. Why is she behaving like this?

His chest burned. The sea-level air of Friday Harbor was settling deep into his lungs, making every breath a conscious, exhausting labor. He wanted nothing more than to close his eyes, fall asleep, and wait for noon the next day when his aunt’s heavy transport van would arrive to pull him out of this heavy, toxic atmosphere.

"I'm so tired," Vanessa murmured, setting the empty bowl aside and resting her chin in her hand, staring at him. "But this is nice, isn't it? Just the two of us. A real home."

Johnny simply stared back at her, his face a mask of rigid, involuntary tension. He had no Quantum XN to type his refusal. He had no voice to demand his RespiraCore Compressor machine.

Vanessa pushed herself up from the table, stretching her aching back. "Let's go to bed early. We have a big day tomorrow. We have to figure out how to tell your aunt that you're staying with me."

Johnny’s heart rate spiked, a cold wash of adrenaline hitting his bloodstream. Staying? That was a catastrophic deviation from the timeline. The contract was for twenty-four hours. Victoria did not negotiate contracts.

But before his mind could fully process the threat, Vanessa unlocked the brakes on his chair and wheeled him back down the narrow, claustrophobic hallway.

She pushed him into the dim bedroom, the small lamp on the nightstand still casting its amber glow across the queen-sized mattress. The air in the room felt even heavier than before, suffocating and hot.

She transferred him from the chair to the center of the bed. Johnny waited for her to arrange the pillows beneath his shoulders to elevate his airway—a strict, non-negotiable requirement for his nighttime breathing.

She didn't. Instead, she laid him completely flat against the mattress, ignoring the sharp, sudden hitch in his breathing as gravity pressed against his weakened intercostal muscles.

Vanessa crawled onto the bed beside him. The exhausted, maternal facade she had worn in the kitchen melted away entirely, replaced once again by that terrifying, predatory softness. She leaned over him, her hair falling across his face, trapping him beneath her.

"You make me so happy, Johnny," she whispered, her hands reaching down to unfasten his clothing, completely bypassing his physical inability to push her away. "Let's have one more special secret before we go to sleep."

Johnny squeezed his eyes shut, his lungs burning as the rigid, mathematical boundaries of his world collapsed once again.
