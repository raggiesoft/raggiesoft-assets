# Part 4: The Revelation (21:45)

The transition into the evening had been slow, deliberate, and fiercely guarded. The core four had retreated to their corner suite, locking the heavy oak door against the frantic, echoing preparations of the Centennial Gala downstairs.

For years, Johnny’s evening routine had been a meticulously choreographed, non-verbal dance. Because his cerebral palsy made it impossible for him to bathe himself, Amanda, Ashleigh, and Sarah handled it together. It was a clinical, quiet, and deeply respectful process, completely devoid of modesty or hesitation. They were a pack, and this was simply how they cared for their own.

The accessible bathroom was warm, filled with the sharp, clean scent of eucalyptus and the rush of hot water. Amanda was managing his stability, while Ashleigh handled the soap and Sarah prepared the heavy, heated towels.

It was entirely routine. Until it wasn't.

Ashleigh reached down with a soapy washcloth to clean his lower half, moving with the same gentle, practiced efficiency she had used a thousand times before.

Johnny violently flinched.

His entire body locked up, a sharp, ragged gasp tearing from his throat. His good hand shot out, grabbing Ashleigh’s wrist with terrifying, desperate strength, stopping her cold.

The bathroom went entirely silent, save for the rush of the water.

Amanda froze, her eyes snapping to her brother’s face. Johnny’s eyes were wide, dilated with a raw, unadulterated panic that none of them had ever seen before. He was trembling so hard the water in the basin was rippling.

"Johnny?" Ashleigh whispered, her voice tight, immediately dropping the washcloth and stepping back to give him space. "Hey, it's okay. I stopped. I stopped."

He didn't let go of her wrist. His breathing was rapid, shallow, bordering on hyperventilation. He looked frantically between the three girls, his jaw working as he tried desperately to force his vocal cords to produce words he couldn't form.

Amanda didn't hesitate. She threw a heavy towel over his lap, stepped out of the splash zone, and grabbed his Quantum XN laptop off the counter, holding it out in front of him.

Johnny’s trembling hand released Ashleigh and hit the keyboard. His fingers were stiff, lacking their usual precision, hitting the large, macro keys he rarely used.

*"Vanessa,"* the synthetic voice echoed off the tile.

The temperature in the room seemed to drop ten degrees.

He hit another key, tears suddenly spilling hot and fast down his cheeks.

*"Special. Said it was special. Just for me."*

Amanda stopped breathing. Ashleigh slowly backed up until her shoulders hit the tiled wall, her hands coming up to cover her mouth.

Johnny hit one final, devastating key.

*"I didn't want the special."*

The horrific, sickening reality of what had happened in that Friday Harbor apartment slammed into the room. The three girls shared a single, horrified look, communicating a thousand words in a fraction of a second.

Sarah didn't say a word. She spun around, grabbed a heavy terrycloth bathrobe off the hook, shoved her arms through the sleeves, and sprinted out of the bathroom. The heavy suite door banged open, and she was running down the timber corridor.

In the bathroom, Amanda dropped to her knees right in the puddles of water. She wrapped her arms around Johnny’s shoulders, pulling his face into her neck, letting him sob into her collarbone. "I've got you," she kept whispering, her own voice breaking. "She's never touching you again. I swear to God, Johnny, I've got you."

Less than two minutes later, the bathroom door flew open.

Victoria Vance stood in the frame. She was still wearing the tailored trousers and silk blouse from her lobby command center, but her face was carved from granite.

Sarah stood behind her, her eyes red, clutching the lapels of her robe.

Victoria stepped into the warm, humid room. She took one look at the Quantum laptop, then looked at Johnny, who was shaking violently against Amanda. The brilliant, terrifying legal mind of the Matriarch processed the entire crime scene in a millisecond.

She didn't gasp. She didn't cry. The absolute, unyielding power of the mountain seemed to coalesce in her posture.

She knelt down in the water next to Amanda, completely ruining her silk slacks, and placed a warm, firm hand on the back of Johnny’s neck.

"Johnny, look at me," Victoria said. Her voice was incredibly soft, but it carried the absolute weight of a sovereign guarantee.

He slowly turned his head, his face wet and terrified, searching his aunt's eyes for anger or disgust.

"You are not in trouble," Victoria stated, locking eyes with him. "You did absolutely nothing wrong. Do you understand me? Nothing. I am not mad at you. I am so incredibly proud of you for telling us."

Johnny let out a long, shuddering breath, the tension in his spine finally beginning to crack under the weight of her reassurance.

"Let's finish getting him warm," Victoria commanded gently, looking up at Ashleigh and Sarah. "I'm not leaving."

And she didn't. Victoria stayed in the bathroom, a silent, immovable pillar of safety, while the girls carefully, gently finished the routine.

Twenty minutes later, the lights in the main suite were dimmed to a soft amber.

Johnny lay in the center of the massive queen bed, wrapped in heavy quilts. The adrenaline crash had hit him hard, leaving him exhausted and hollow.

Usually, he and Amanda shared the bed, while Sarah and Ashleigh took the other. Not tonight.

Amanda crawled in beside him, wrapping her arms tightly around his waist, anchoring him to the mattress. A moment later, the mattress dipped on the other side as Ashleigh slid in, pressing her back firmly against his to box him in with warmth. Finally, Sarah climbed over the footboard, wedging herself horizontally across the bottom of the bed, her hands resting securely on his ankles.

It was cramped, awkward, and profoundly uncomfortable. But none of them moved a muscle. They formed a literal, physical barricade around him. The pack had closed ranks.

Victoria stood at the foot of the bed, watching the four of them breathe in unison. The softness that had been in her eyes in the bathroom was entirely gone. It had been replaced by a cold, calculating, apocalyptic rage.

"Lock the deadbolt," Victoria whispered to Sarah. "No one comes in until morning."

Victoria turned and walked out of the suite, the heavy oak door clicking shut behind her.

She didn't go to her bedroom. She walked straight to the third-floor executive office, turned on the single desk lamp, and booted up her encrypted terminal. Outside, the wind began to howl as the first snow flurries of the Centennial storm hit the reinforced glass.

She opened the Prosecutorial Evidentiary Dossier she had started in the back of the transport van. She bypassed the kidnapping charges and the medical fraud templates. She opened a blank page, her fingers hovering over the keyboard.

She typed RCW 9A.44.050 - Rape in the Second Degree (Incapable of Consent). It was time to end Vanessa Hayes.
