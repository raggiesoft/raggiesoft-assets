# Part 5: The Sanctuary (00:30)

The evacuation of the Grand Ballroom was a slow, elegant retreat. As the final notes of the jazz orchestra faded, the four hundred guests began their migration toward the lavishly appointed guest wings of the Keep. Victoria stood by the heavy mahogany doors, offering polite, ironclad farewells, ensuring every politician and VIP was safely cordoned off into their designated sectors of the fortress.

By twelve-thirty, the Great Hall was completely empty, save for the silent, efficient waitstaff clearing the tables.

The public performance was over. The family was finally alone.

Amanda pushed Johnny away from the dying embers of the Ballroom fireplace, bypassing the main elevators. They took the private switchback ramp up to the second floor, slipping through the heavy, unmarked security door that led to the Owner's Quarters.

The Core Four suite was exactly as they had left it—warm, softly lit, and smelling faintly of the pine garlands outside. It was a massive, extended-stay layout designed specifically for Johnny’s needs. A small, fully stocked kitchenette sat near the entrance. In the main space, two plush queen beds were arranged perfectly. Sarah and Ashleigh shared the bed near the window, while the bed closest to the ambient warmth of the radiator belonged to Amanda and Johnny.

Johnny took a deep breath. At sea level in Friday Harbor, the dense, humid air had always felt heavy and suffocating in his lungs. But here, at 7,000 feet, the crisp, thin alpine air was exactly what his body was adapted to. His chest relaxed. He could breathe.

He was home.

Amanda helped him transfer from his chair onto the edge of their bed. Sarah and Ashleigh quietly locked the deadbolts on the suite door. A moment later, there was a soft, rhythmic knock—the family code. Sarah opened it to let Victoria and Diana slip inside.

The Matriarch had shed her sapphire gown, now wearing comfortable silk pajamas and a thick robe, while Diana had finally taken off her chef's coat. The imposing, terrifying women who had just commanded the state's elite were gone; in this room, they were just his fiercely protective pack.

Amanda sat on the bed behind Johnny, wrapping her arms securely around his chest, anchoring him against her just as she had done that morning. It was the physical proof that he was safe.

The room fell into a heavy, waiting silence. They all knew what needed to happen next. Johnny had been locked away in that Friday Harbor apartment for three agonizing months before he finally broke free, navigating the island ferry to Anacortes where Victoria’s extraction team had been waiting at the docks. He had survived. But the emotional extraction was just beginning.

Johnny rested his hands on his Quantum laptop. He didn't use the text-to-speech engine. The synthetic voice felt too loud, too public for what he needed to say. Instead, he routed the text directly to the small secondary monitor resting on the kitchenette counter, where Victoria, Diana, Sarah, and Ashleigh were gathered.

He told them everything.

He detailed the absolute isolation. He explained how Vanessa would hide the charging cables, essentially turning off his voice. And then, his hands trembling slightly, he typed out the darkest reality of his captivity—the profound, manipulative abuse Vanessa had subjected him to. He explained the complete stripping of his autonomy, the things she did when he was physically unable to stop her or give informed consent, and the sick, twisted psychological cruelty she used to justify it by calling it "The Special."

Amanda didn't say a word. She just tightened her arms around him, pressing her cheek firmly against his shoulder, absorbing the tremors racking his body. Sarah and Ashleigh sat silently on the edge of the second bed, their expressions shifting from exhaustion to absolute, white-hot devastation.

By the kitchenette, Victoria Vance stared at the monitor. The Matriarch's face was completely devoid of color. The political mastermind who had just effortlessly threatened a state senator was gone. What replaced it was a cold, terrifying, apocalyptic fury.

Diana leaned against the counter, her hands gripping the granite edge so hard her knuckles were white.

"She called it a privilege," Ashleigh whispered, her voice cracking as the text scrolled across the screen. "She assaulted him and told him he should be grateful."

Johnny stopped typing. He closed his eyes, leaning his full weight back against Amanda. He had finally handed the nightmare over to them. He didn't have to carry it alone anymore.

Diana took a slow, deep breath, forcing the fury down into something clinical and actionable.

"We need the medical files," Diana said quietly, her voice steady in the silent room. She looked over at Victoria. "The full diagnostic workup from Cascade Pediatric. Specifically, the endocrinology reports detailing his non-obstructive azoospermia."

Victoria looked up from the screen, her eyes locking onto the chef. The legal architect immediately saw the angle.

"Because," Diana continued, her voice hardening, "the way Vanessa operated... the level of manipulation she used... it’s predatory. And predators lie. If she ever tries to claim anything happened down the line to extort this family, or if she tries to leverage 'The Special' into a long con, we need the irrefutable, biological proof that Johnny is physically incapable of what she might claim. We weaponize his medical history before she can even think about it."

Sarah nodded sharply from the other bed. "Diana’s right. Vanessa isolated him because she thought he was defenseless. She didn't know we have every single piece of his biology documented and legally sealed."

Victoria turned away from the monitor. She walked over to the bed, kneeling down so she was at eye level with Johnny. She reached out, placing a firm, warm hand over his trembling fingers on the keyboard.

"Listen to me, Johnny," Victoria said, her voice dropping into a register of absolute, unbreakable conviction. "Vanessa Hayes is currently sitting in a concrete cell in Skagit County. Saturday, when the WSDOT gate drops, she will be officially cut off from our world entirely. She is never going to touch you again. She is never going to speak to you again. I am going to build a legal wall around her so high she will spend the rest of her life suffocating behind it."

Victoria gently squeezed his hand.

"You survived her," Victoria whispered fiercely. "You got yourself on that ferry. You fought your way back to us. And now, you are in the Keep. You are surrounded by stone, iron, and a family that will burn the world down to keep you safe."

Johnny opened his eyes. He looked at Victoria, then at Diana, Sarah, and Ashleigh. He felt the steady, rhythmic heartbeat of Amanda pressing against his back.

He didn't need to type anything. He just nodded, letting out a long, shuddering exhale.

"Alright," Amanda murmured softly, kissing the top of his head. "We've got you. The perimeter is secure. Let's get some sleep."
