"Wait a second," Tay said, leaning forward on his knees toward the entertainment stand. "Where did this one come from?"

Ocea sat up on the carpet, brushing a lock of hair behind her ear as Tay picked up a heavy, matte-black sixteen-bit cartridge sitting beside their stack of plastic rental cases. Unlike their other games, it had no glossy sticker on the front—only a faint, geometric rune etched directly into the dark shell that seemed to shimmer between gold and deep violet when tilted toward the television light.

"Did you pick that up at the swap meet?" Ocea asked, scooting closer on her bare feet.

"No," Tay murmured, turning it over in his hands. The brass contact pins along the bottom gleamed as if they had been forged from solid gold rather than circuit board copper. "Must have been mixed in the bargain bin when we traded in those old sports titles. Let's see what's on it."

He clicked the power switch off, ejected *Super Pixel Plumbers*, and pressed the black cartridge into the slot. It seated with a strangely resonant, metallic *clack* that echoed through the floorboards.

When Tay flipped the power switch back on, no company logo appeared. Instead, the curved glass of the CRT screen flared with a deep, hypnotic indigo light. A cascade of crystalline arpeggios poured from the television speakers—far richer and more multidimensional than any sixteen-bit soundchip had a right to produce. Across the center of the glass, blocky arcade letters materialized in pulsing violet and gold:

*RUNEBREAKER // DREAM KINGDOM DIRECTORY v4.04*
*INSERT COIN TO OVERWRITE REALITY*

"Whoa," Ocea whispered, a chill prickling along her arms through her cotton t-shirt. The hair on the back of her neck stood straight up as the smell of hot dust and sharp electrical ozone filled the living room. "Tay, turn it off. The screen's bowing outward."

Tay reached for the power switch, but his fingers froze an inch from the plastic console. A blinding arc of purple lightning snapped from the glass tube, striking the console and racing up the controller cords. The power switch refused to budge.

"It's locked!" Tay shouted over the rising roar of the speakers. He lunged for the wall outlet to yank the plug, but before his hand could reach the baseboard, the glass surface of the television liquefied into a swirling, bottomless vortex of violet runes and blinding white scanlines.

A hurricane wind erupted inward toward the screen, lifting loose sheets of notebook paper, couch cushions, and plastic game cases into the air. Ocea cried out as her bare feet slid across the carpet, the gravitational pull of the CRT dragging her straight toward the glowing glass.

"Ocea!" Tay threw himself backward, wrapping one arm fiercely around her waist while his other hand locked onto the heavy oak leg of the coffee table. The oak groaned and splintered as the table was dragged across the floor with them. "Hold on to me! Don't let go!"

Ocea buried her fingers into the fabric of her twin brother's shirt as the purple flash swallowed the entire living room, and the glass opened wide to pull them both inside.
