There was no floor, no ceiling, and no air—only a roaring tunnel of static and pixels hurtling past at impossible speed.

Ocea clung to Tay's hand with everything she had as they tumbled through the fractured boundary between worlds. Around them, ribbons of hexadecimal code braided themselves into glowing stone battlements, and sixteen-bit synth arpeggios vibrated directly through her ribs like a second heartbeat. The air tasted of burning ozone and cold subterranean stone. Every time she blinked, her own fingers flickered between solid skin and shimmering matrices of golden light.

"Tay!" she screamed into the gale of digital snow. "Stay with me!"

"I've got you!" Tay yelled back, his fingers locked around her wrist in a white-knuckled grip. "I'm not letting go!"

Then the tunnel ahead tore open, revealing a sky of jagged, corrupted purple lightning crowning a monolithic fortress of black stone. Floating at the mouth of the rift stood a towering figure draped in robes of woven shadow and glowing violet command runes. Beneath his hood burned two eyes of cold, merciless crimson.

*Two unformatted variables from the outer directory,* a voice boomed—not through the air, but directly inside Ocea's skull, heavy and resonant as an iron bell. *How fortunate. The Blue Shard carries the raw voltage my core requires... and the Red Shard is a redundancy to be deleted.*

The Grand Wizard raised a gauntleted hand. A claw of jagged purple energy lanced into the slipstream, slamming straight between Ocea and her brother.

Tay cried out in agony as dark violet runes coiled around his chest, seizing the ocean-blue half of the clay medallion beneath his shirt. Ocea felt his body go ice-cold against hers. Before her horrified eyes, the Wizard projected a phantom illusion directly into the space between them—a cruel, fabricated mirage showing Ocea's form shattering into a million lifeless red shards that dissolved into the void.

"Ocea! *No!*" Tay's scream was raw, primal, and utterly shattered, his eyes locked on the false image of his twin sister's death even as the real Ocea clawed desperately at his sleeve inches away.

"I'm right here, Tay! Look at me, it's a lie!" Ocea sobbed, her fingers slipping across the freezing, static-slicked fabric of his forearm as the purple current wrenched them toward opposite horizons.

*Sleep in the ash of your grief, my new apprentice,* the Wizard's voice echoed as he dragged Tay's limp, trembling form toward the black spire of the Citadel. *Your sister is gone. Only the Decree remains.*

A final shockwave of violet static slammed into Ocea's chest, severing the last millimeter of contact between their fingertips and hurling her down through a bank of luminous clouds into the dark below.
