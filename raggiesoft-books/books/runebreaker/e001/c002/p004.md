"They always think the code is written in stone!" the Crystal Sage laughed, practically dancing across the laboratory as he unlocked a heavy chest of polished star-metal. As he lifted the lid, a groovy, energetic rhythm seemed to pulse through the observatory's sound-crystals—a fuzzy sixteen-bit organ groove and a driving bassline that made the glass vials on the shelves clink in time. "The Wizard thinks because you only hold half a medallion, your variable is incomplete!"

From the velvet lining of the chest, the Sage lifted a sleek, fingerless gauntlet crafted from lightweight silver-gold alloy and translucent crystal conduits. Unlike bulky medieval armor, it was compact—designed to strap cleanly over a bare forearm like a piece of retro-futuristic arcade hardware.

"Hold out your arm, Ocea," the Sage instructed.

Ocea extended her right arm. The Sage fastened the *Runebreaker* gauntlet over her wrist, leaving her fingers free and her casual t-shirt and jeans completely unencumbered. Right above the back of her hand sat an empty, recessed socket of bevelled quartz.

"Now," the Sage said, handing her the fire-engine red half of the clay medallion. "The Wizard's purple runes work by isolation—by convincing a mind that what is broken can never be made whole. If you want to break his rules and overwrite his dark magic without harming a hair on your brother's head, you can't just look at the jagged edge of what you're holding. You have to picture the whole thing in your mind!"

Ocea took a breath and pressed her red clay half into the gauntlet's crystal housing—just enough to channel its resonance before slipping the clay safely back into her pocket when she needed to stay hidden.

She closed her eyes. She didn't picture the dark rift or the black spire. Instead, she pictured the sunlit wooden picnic table at summer camp where she and Tay had painted the wet clay side by side, getting fire-engine red and ocean-blue acrylic smudges all over their thumbs. She pictured the two halves locked seamlessly together, and she felt the unmistakable warmth of Tay's arms around her shoulders.

*Crack-POP!*

Ocea snapped her eyes open. A dazzling stream of warm, golden pixel light erupted from her palm, swirling around her fingertips like living fireflies of pure, uncorrupted data. Across the laboratory, a test slate inscribed with the Grand Wizard's locking runes hissed as Ocea flicked her wrist; the golden pixels wove silently into the purple script, rewriting the curse into a harmless shower of glittering sparks.

"Look at that!" the Sage cheered, clapping his hands as the funky sixteen-bit organ solo echoed off the prism dome. "Golden data! Pure, analog sibling love compiled into executable light! With that, you can sabotage his scrolls, shatter his sentinels, and when the moment comes—reunite the two halves to crash his entire directory!"
