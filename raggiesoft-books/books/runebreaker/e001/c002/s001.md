Morning broke across the Dream Kingdom not with a single rising sun, but with a choir of prismatic light refracting through miles of towering, translucent quartz monoliths.

Ocea shielded her eyes with her forearm as she crested the high meadow ridge, the hem of her faded blue jeans brushing against clusters of bell-shaped wildflowers that chimed in soft major chords whenever the wind stirred them. Beneath her bare feet, the stone pathways of the Crystal Plains were surprisingly smooth and warm, heated by veins of liquid golden mana flowing just beneath the translucent surface like glowing circuit traces on a motherboard.

Everything in this world felt simultaneously ancient and programmed. When a flock of glass-winged songbirds lifted off from a nearby crystalline willow tree, the light trailing behind their feathers resolved for a split second into crisp, sixteen-bit dithering patterns before smoothing back into living color.

Yet the beauty of the plains was already fraying at the edges.

Halfway across an arched quartz bridge spanning a river of liquid starlight, Ocea stopped short. A jagged fissure of dead, static-filled black space had eaten right through the middle of the stone parapet. Purple runes—identical to the ones that had coiled around Tay in the rift—crawled along the edges of the crack like hungry insects, systematically deleting the color from the crystal and leaving cold, grey cinderblock in its wake.

Ocea crouched beside the fissure, careful not to let her bare toes touch the creeping violet static. When she drew the fire-engine red half of her summer-camp medallion from her pocket, a tiny spark of golden light leaped from the painted clay and struck the nearest purple rune.

With a sharp *pop*, the rune dissolved, and a three-inch patch of grey stone instantly bloomed back into living, turquoise quartz.

"Okay," Ocea breathed, her pulse quickening as she tucked the clay half safely back into her jeans pocket. "So you're not invincible."

Ahead, nestled inside a natural amphitheater of giant, sky-piercing mana crystals that acted as a shield against the creeping purple blight, rose the thatched roofs, spinning waterwheels, and cobblestone lanes of Sanctuary Village. Ocea broke into a jog, following the singing stone road down toward the gates.
