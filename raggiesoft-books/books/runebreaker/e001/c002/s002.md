Stepping through the timber arch of Sanctuary Village felt like walking into the warmest town theme of an RPG after hours in the wilderness. The air smelled of fresh-baked hearth bread, roasted chestnuts, and sweet pine resin. A gentle melody of wooden flutes and plucked acoustic strings drifted from an open-air pavilion near the central fountain, where villagers in embroidered linen tunics and cloaks of woven moss gathered to trade mana vials and bundles of glowing herbs.

Every conversation near the gate ground to an immediate halt the moment Ocea walked in.

Dozens of wide eyes dropped from her graphic cotton t-shirt down to her faded denim jeans, and finally to her bare feet standing on the sun-warmed cobblestones. In a world of hand-spun wool, leather greaves, and silver clasps, an eighteen-year-old girl from 1992 looked like a visitor from another star.

"By the First Crystal," whispered an elderly herbalist beside a cart of luminous blue roots. "No boots, no mail, and cloth dyed in a weave none of the looms of the Realm have ever spun. Are you... a Traveler from beyond the Glass?"

"I came through a television screen last night with my twin brother, Tay," Ocea said earnestly, stepping up to the cart and pulling the fire-engine red half of their summer-camp clay medallion from her jeans pocket. "The Grand Wizard took him toward that black spire in the north. All I have to track him is my half of the pendant we made together."

A collective gasp rippled through the market square. Several merchants dropped their baskets, staring in disbelief at the jagged, painted clay shard glowing warmly in Ocea's palm.

"The Sundered Crystal..." a weaver breathed, shaking his head in awe. "For four hundred years of the Era of the Crystal, our people were told of the Crimson and Sapphire Halves that would one day return to mend the Realm. After centuries of waiting in vain, everyone—even the elders—came to believe the two halves of the Crystal were nothing more than a children's myth!"

"None who enter the Obsidian Spire return unformatted, child," the herbalist said softly, pressing a small canvas satchel containing a loaf of warm bread and a flask of spring water into Ocea's hands. "Since the Grand Wizard seized the Directory, his Iron Sentinels patrol every gate, and his henchmen wear the drab grey wool of servitude. Go to the Crystal Sage in the prism observatory atop the upper tier—if the myth of the Twin Crystal has truly walked through our gates, he is the only one who knows the old code."

As Ocea thanked her and turned toward the spiral stone staircase leading up to the observatory, her eyes caught a clothesline strung behind the village guardhouse where confiscated gear from captured Spire scouts had been hung out to dry. Draped over the hemp rope was a coarse, drab grey-brown wool tunic—identical to the uniforms the herbalist had just described.

A plan clicked together in Ocea's mind. Fighting her way through the outer perimeter was one thing, but once she was inside the Citadel walls, standing out in a modern t-shirt and jeans would alert every sentry on the floor.

After asking the guardhouse watchman—who gladly waved her permission to take the abandoned rag—Ocea yanked the scratchy wool tunic off the clothesline, folded it tightly, and stuffed it into the bottom of her new canvas satchel before racing up the steps toward the observatory.
