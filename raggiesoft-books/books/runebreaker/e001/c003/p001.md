Five miles north of Sanctuary Village, the singing turquoise quartz of the plains abruptly gave way to the Jagged Wastes—a scarred expanse of grey basalt and exposed copper cabling where the Grand Wizard's formatting spells had already stripped the land to its raw wireframe bones.

Ocea stepped carefully across the cool, pixelated stone on her bare feet, her canvas satchel slung tight across her cotton t-shirt. Ahead, the Obsidian Spire loomed like a dagger thrust into a bruise-colored sky, its upper battlements wreathed in swirling purple static.

Suddenly, the ambient hum of the terrain spiked into a frantic, high-speed sixteen-bit arpeggio.

From three jagged fissures in the basalt rose a pack of Glitch Phantoms—shredded, flickering wraiths composed of torn sprite sheets, missing textures, and screeching magenta scanlines. They had once been woodland guardians of the Crystal Plains before the Grand Wizard deleted their names from the directory, leaving only erratic, hostile fragments of corrupted code.

The lead phantom lunged at Ocea, its claws trailing ribbons of razor-sharp violet static.

Ocea didn't flinch. Years of timing frame-perfect dodges beside Tay on the living room floor kicked into muscle memory. She pivoted sharply on the ball of her bare foot, letting the phantom's lunge whistle past her denim-clad knee, and raised her right arm.

Inside her pocket, the fire-engine red clay medallion half pulsed hot against her hip. Focusing on the mental image of the whole medallion, Ocea snapped her fingers. The *Runebreaker* gauntlet flared to life, launching a Whip-crack arc of golden pixel data straight through the phantom's flickering torso.

Where the golden light struck, the screeching magenta static didn't explode in violence—it *recompiled*. For a fleeting second, the wraith's jagged edges smoothed back into the serene, translucent form of a crystal stag, bowing its head in silent gratitude before dissolving into a shower of warm golden chimes.

The remaining two phantoms converged from left and right in an erratic pincer attack. Ocea dropped into a low crouch, swept her palm in a wide horizontal arc, and released a burst of golden particles that wove straight into their corrupted command loops. Both phantoms froze mid-leap, their purple runes unraveling into peaceful white light as an abrupt, triumphant victory fanfare echoed across the ravine.

Breathing hard, Ocea lowered her glowing gauntlet and looked up at the towering iron gates of the Citadel just ahead.
