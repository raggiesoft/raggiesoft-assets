*CLANG. CLANG. CLANG.*

The ground vibrated beneath Ocea's bare soles with the rigid, mechanical cadence of a marching drum machine. Guarding the sunken subterranean service grate at the base of the Obsidian Spire stood two Iron Sentinels—twelve-foot-tall suits of riveted black plate armor powered by spinning purple mana turbines in their chests.

There was no sneaking past their overlapping red scan-cones. The only way into the dungeon level where Tay was being held was straight through them.

Ocea stepped out from behind a basalt pillar, her hands raised and golden pixel sparks dancing across her knuckles.

*UNREGISTERED VARIABLE DETECTED,* the Sentinels bellowed in unison, their metallic voices grinding likestripped gears. *EXECUTING DELETION PROTOCOL.*

The first Sentinel brought its massive iron halberd crashing down in a vertical cleave that cracked the stone pavement. Ocea vaulted sideways, feeling the rush of displaced air whip her hair across her face. Before the automaton could wrench its blade free from the stone, Ocea sprinted up the flat of the iron shaft on nimble bare feet, leaped toward its chest plate, and slammed her glowing *Runebreaker* palm directly against the purple turbine housing.

Golden data flooded the Sentinel's core, overwriting its rigid marching rhythm with a chaotic burst of sixteen-bit syncopation. Its crimson visor flickered, sputtered, and went dark as the giant suit of armor locked into a harmless statue.

The second Sentinel swung a sweeping backhand blow that grazed Ocea's shoulder, sending her skidding across the smooth stone floor. She rolled to her feet, wincing as her knees scraped against the grit, and fired a concentrated beam of golden pixels straight into the exposed crimson hinge at the Sentinel's knee joint. The purple command rune shattered like glass; the second machine toppled forward with a thunderous crash, triggering a crisp, ringing fanfare that faded into the rumble of the fortress turbines.

Knowing the noise would draw patrols from the upper ramparts within minutes, Oceapried open the heavy iron grate leading into the subterranean dungeon ductwork. Before climbing down, she ducked into an alcove and pulled the coarse, scratchy wool tunic from her canvas satchel.

To keep the stiff blue cuffs of her denim jeans from giving her away beneath the hem of the shapeless servant garment, she quickly slipped out of her jeans, folded them with her t-shirt into the satchel, and pulled the rough wool tunic over her head. It scratched her skin like sandpaper, smelling of old lye and damp stone, but in the dim purple torchlight of the basement corridors, she looked just like another nameless drudge of the Citadel.

Clutching the fire-engine red half of the clay medallion in her pocket to track her brother's faint blue signal, Ocea slipped down into the dungeon dark.
