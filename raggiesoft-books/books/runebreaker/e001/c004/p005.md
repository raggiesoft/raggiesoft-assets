The iron portcullis slammed down with a deafening crash, and from the shadowed vault above dropped the *Guardian of the Gate*—a thirty-foot colossus of fused obsidian armor, six mechanical arms, and a roaring furnace of compressed purple runes in its chest.

High atop the interior balcony overlooking the courtyard stood the Grand Wizard, his crimson eyes blazing with cold fury as dissonant, overwhelming brass synths reverberated off the fortress walls.

"So," the Wizard's voice thundered across the stone courtyard. "The Red Shard survived the crossing after all. No wonder my apprentice's spellwork was contaminated with golden static!"

Beside Ocea, Tay cried out, dropping to one knee and clutching his chest. At a single gesture from the Wizard's hand, the dormant purple runes woven into Tay's neural pathways flared violently to life, locking his muscles in an agonizing vise of freezing static so he couldn't move to help her.

"Leave him alone!" Ocea stepped directly in front of her kneeling brother, planting her bare feet wide on the cold stone and raising her *Runebreaker* gauntlet toward the thirty-foot colossus.

The Guardian of the Gate brought three of its massive obsidian blades down in a simultaneous shockwave. Ocea dodged left, firing rapid volleys of golden pixel light into the creature's lower joints. Against the outer Iron Sentinels, a single burst had been enough to overwrite their code. Here, directly beneath the Grand Wizard's master compiler, every golden pixel she wove into the Guardian's armor was instantly flooded and overwritten by ten thousand lines of dark purple script pouring down from the balcony.

She was fighting a losing battle against the entire server of the Obsidian Spire.

A sweeping backhand from the Guardian's shield caught Ocea in the ribs, hurling her twenty feet across the courtyard. She hit the cobblestones hard, the breath knocked clean out of her lungs as the crystal housing of her *Runebreaker* gauntlet cracked with a sharp, sickening *snap*.

"Ocea!" Tay screamed, straining with every fiber of his body against the purple chains holding him down, tears of sheer helplessness streaming down his face. "Stop! Please, don't hurt her! I'll compile the scroll—I'll do whatever you want, just don't hurt my sister!"

Ocea tried to push herself up on trembling arms, summoning one last desperate spark of golden light to her fingertips, but the Grand Wizard descended from the balcony in a pillar of black lightning. With a flick of his wrist, a suffocating wave of violet gravity slammed Ocea flat against the stone.

"How touching," the Wizard sneered as a slow, tragic, dissonant chord echoed through the courtyard and the light in Ocea's gauntlet flickered out. "At ten o'clock, inside the Grand Cathedral, you will both participate in the Corrupted Ceremony—where the Blue Shard will watch the Red Shard be formatted before his very eyes, sealing the Grand Decree forever. Take them to the preparation cells!"

As iron-gauntleted wardens dragged her backward away from Tay's outstretched hand, the world around Ocea dissolved into static and darkness.
