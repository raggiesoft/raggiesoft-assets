The bronze doors of the Grand Cathedral swung open, and a slow, stately sixteen-bit ceremonial march began to echo from the towering pipe-organ crystals lining the nave.

With every step Ocea took down the central aisle between ranks of motionless Iron Sentinels, the traditional wedding-march melody detuned further—warping into eerie, out-of-time synth church bells and a heavy, oppressive bassline. Above the high altar hung the Great Directory, pulsing with angry purple runes that were ninety-nine percent compiled.

Standing beside the formatting pedestal was Tay. Collar-deep in chains of violet static that tethered his wrists to the altar, her twin brother stared straight ahead with pale, hollow eyes, the ocean-blue half of their clay medallion clamped into a crystal vise right in front of his chest. Beside him stood the Grand Wizard, his staff raised to bridge the final circuit.

"Behold the final union of the Directory!" the Grand Wizard's voice boomed across the cathedral as Ocea was marched to the foot of the altar steps. "When the Blue Shard witnesses the deletion of the Red Shard, the last vestige of human chaos will be purged, and the Era of the Crystal shall bow to my eternal order!"

Ocea stopped on the bottom step, her bare feet planted firmly against the cold violet marble.

Up on the dais, Tay's eyes flickered downward toward her hands. Even through the crushing weight of the purple static, his fingers twitched against his chains in that unmistakable four-beat rhythm: *tap-tap-pause-tap*.

"Any final words before your memory address is cleared, little glitch?" the Grand Wizard mocked, raising his staff as the dissonant church bells reached a deafening crescendo.

"Yeah," Ocea said clearly. "This dress is driving me crazy."

Before the two automaton guards flanking her could react, Ocea grabbed the stiff, pixelated silver-and-violet collar of the ceremonial gown with both hands and ripped it clean down the middle.

The corrupted static-fabric tore apart like wet tissue paper, pooling uselessly on the marble floor and leaving Ocea standing bare-footed in her real-world cotton t-shirt and faded 1992 denim jeans. On her right forearm, the cracked quartz lens of the *Runebreaker* gauntlet blazed like a miniature sun, fed by the fire-engine red clay medallion half locked in her palm.

"What?!" the Grand Wizard snarled, stepping back as the twisted ceremonial march dropped into a furious, high-voltage rival melody. "Seize her!"

Ocea vaulted straight up the altar steps, dodging between the swinging halberds of the Sentinels with the effortless agility of someone who wasn't weighed down by armor or a hideous gown. Planting her bare foot on the edge of the formatting pedestal, she leaped directly in front of her brother and slammed her glowing golden palm straight against the purple chains binding his chest.

"Wake up, Player One!" Ocea shouted over the roar of the cathedral. "I'm right here!"
