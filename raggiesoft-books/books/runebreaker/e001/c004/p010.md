A terrifying, massive wall of sixteen-bit sound erupted from the abyss beneath the platform, shaking the entire Obsidian Spire to its foundations as gothic choir synths and a relentless, driving dark waltz filled the air.

"If I cannot format the directory from without," the Grand Wizard's voice reverberated from a thousand fractured speakers at once, "then I shall become the operating system itself!"

From the boiling sea of purple static rose the Wizard's *True Form*—a gargantuan, multi-winged seraph of jagged violet polygons, spinning iron rings, and corrupted hexadecimal scripture that filled the entire vertical shaft of the tower. Every beat of its six pixel-shredded wings sent waves of deletion static washing across the platform, eating away the crystal tiles beneath Ocea's bare feet and Tay's sneakers.

"Hold on to the medallion!" Tay shouted over the deafening roar of the gothic synth choir, bracing his shoulder against Ocea's as they retreated to the final intact hexagon at the center of the ring.

The True Form opened its core—a blinding furnace of crimson and purple light—and unleashed a continuous torrent of corrupted code designed to erase their existence from both worlds at once.

Ocea raised her right hand; Tay raised his left. Between their clasped palms rested the united clay summer-camp medallion—half fire-engine red, half ocean-blue, scored down the middle by two kids who had promised never to let each other go.

As the blistering arpeggios of the final boss theme pushed the soundchip of the universe to its absolute limit, Ocea didn't look at the towering monster above them. She looked at her twin brother beside her, feeling the steady, unbreakable warmth of his hand around hers.

"Together?" Ocea asked.

"Ten minutes older, zero seconds apart," Tay grinned fiercely. "Let's finish the game."

They thrust their joined hands skyward.

From the united clay medallion and the *Runebreaker* gauntlet erupted a towering lance of intertwined golden and sapphire light, accompanied by a soaring, triumphant heroic melody that pierced straight through the gothic darkness. The beam tore upward through the barrage of purple static, rewriting every corrupted line of code in its path, and slammed dead-center into the crimson core of the True Form.

With a deafening, glass-shattering crescendo, the dark music fractured into a million pieces, giving way to a glorious, ringing victory fanfare as the monolithic boss dissolved into harmless white snow.
