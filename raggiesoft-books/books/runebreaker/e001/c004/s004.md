A piercing, sixteen-bit klaxon shattered the silence of the basement closet, and the heavy iron hinges of the oak door flared to a blinding, incandescent crimson.

Ocea bolted upright on the narrow cot, her heart leaping into her throat. Across the room, the cracked screen of the dead scrying terminal flickered violently to life, scrolling lines of blood-red warning script across the glass:

*SYSTEM ALERT: GOLDEN DATA ANOMALY DETECTED IN SECTOR 04*
*UNDELETED VARIABLE LOCATED // SEALING DUNGEON BULKHEADS*

"He knows," Tay gasped, his face draining of color in the pulsing red light. "The scroll from last night—when it backfired, the residual golden code must have logged in the Spire's audit buffer!"

"Get dressed! Now!" Ocea scrambled off the cot onto her bare feet, grabbing her faded denim jeans from the floor and yanking them on in record time while Tay pulled on his own jeans and threw the heavy grey apprentice tunic over his t-shirt only long enough to snatch the ocean-blue clay medallion half from underneath.

"Leave the grey tunic!" Ocea ordered, kicking the coarse stolen wool rags into the corner and slinging her canvas satchel across her cotton t-shirt. "You don't work for him anymore!"

Tay looked down at the drab grey uniform he had hated every second of wearing, tore it off over his head, and hurled it onto the stone floor, standing beside her in his real-world t-shirt and jeans.

Outside in the corridor, the rhythmic *thud-thud-thud* of iron boots echoed from the lower guard post, accelerating to a frantic chase tempo. Ocea aimed her *Runebreaker* gauntlet at the glowing crimson hinges of the oak door and fired a concentrated burst of golden pixel data. The locking runes shattered outward, blowing the heavy oak door clean off its frame into the hallway.

"Run!" Ocea shouted, grabbing Tay's hand.

Together, the twins sprinted up the winding subterranean staircase as purple laser barriers snapped shut inches behind their heels. Even with the lingering dark magic still warring inside his nervous system—making his breath hitch whenever the Spire's alarm bells pulsed—Tay matched her stride for stride, vaulting over fallen masonry and pulling Ocea clear when a ceiling conduit burst in a shower of violet sparks.

Three flights up, the cold stone tunnel opened into the cavernous courtyard of the Great Portcullis, where a sliver of pale morning sky shone beyond the outer iron bars.

"The gate!" Tay breathed, hope flaring in his eyes for the first time since they had been pulled through the television. "Ocea, we're gonna make it!"

Then the music of their escape cut out completely, replaced by a low, bone-rattling hum that shook the cobblestones beneath Ocea's bare feet.
