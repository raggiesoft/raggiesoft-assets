They landed side by side on a wide, hexagonal platform of translucent crystal suspended above the swirling purple vortex of the Spire's reactor core.

Across the chasm, hovering inside a storm of shattered directory slates and crackling violet lightning, the Grand Wizard tore off his tattered outer cloak. An explosive sixteen-bit drum fill echoed through the chamber, kicking off a blistering, fast-paced battle rhythm where a bright, fiery lead synth and a deep, resonant sapphire bassline traded solos in real time.

"You insolent little variables!" the Grand Wizard shrieked, sweeping his staff in a wide arc that launched a barrage of jagged purple rune-spears across the platform. "I built this compiler over four hundred years! I will delete you with my bare hands!"

"Left side, Ocea!" Tay called out, his eyes sharp and clear as he channeled the ocean-blue half of the united medallion into a shimmering shield of sapphire energy that deflected three spears in rapid succession.

"Got it!" Ocea sprinted right on nimble bare feet, leaping from a crumbling stone block onto an elevated crystal conduit. Now that the clay medallion was whole, the *Runebreaker* gauntlet on her wrist was operating at full capacity, linked wirelessly to the sapphire resonance glowing around Tay's hands.

When the Wizard teleported to the northern edge of the ring to summon a crushing gravity well, Tay slammed his palms onto the crystal floor, sending a rhythmic wave of ocean-blue shockwaves racing along the circuit traces beneath the sorcerer's boots to break his stance. In the exact frame the Wizard stumbled, Ocea dropped from above and loosed a blazing volley of golden pixel beams that sheared straight through the dark staff in the Wizard's grip.

They didn't need words or battle manuals. It was Friday night in the living room all over again—Player One and Player Two reading each other's timing by instinct, covering every blind spot, and pressing the advantage as a triumphant, soaring melody echoed off the core walls.

With a final synchronized strike—Tay launching a sapphire uppercut wave while Ocea wove a golden pixel net that detonated against the Wizard's breastplate—the sorcerer was hurled backward into the central mana conduit.

The fast battle tempo abruptly ground to a halt, slowing into a low, menacing, unresolved chord as the shattered halves of the Wizard's staff fell into the purple abyss below.
