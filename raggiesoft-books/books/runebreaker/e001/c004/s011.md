The roaring stadium drums and distorted power chords were gone. In their place played only a slow, distorted, broken sixteen-bit music box, plucking out the melody of *The Grand Decree* in a mournful, halting waltz that missed beats like a dying battery.

Collapsed upon the last fragment of the central altar, reduced once more to a fading silhouette of tattered shadow, the Grand Wizard stared down at his own hands in disbelief.

His gauntlets were unraveling. One pixel at a time, the iron-coded fingers he had used to rule the final decades of the Era of the Crystal were lifting off his wrists and drifting upward like white dandelion seeds.

"The directory is falling..." he whispered, his synth-modulated voice weak, stuttering, and pitching down as static crackled in his throat. "The bytes begin to tear... My perfect, flawless order... vanishing in air..."

Across the dissolving walls of the cathedral, the golden and sapphire light unleashed by the eighteen-year-old twins was cascading through every sector of the server. The Iron Sentinels were melting back into raw, uncorrupted star-metal; the purple fissures scarring the Crystal Plains were healing into lush emerald grass; and the oppressive ceiling of the Obsidian Spire was opening to reveal a sky of pure, brilliant morning sunlight.

He had calculated every variable except the one that could not be written in hexadecimal: that two halves of a cheap piece of summer-camp clay, held together by a brother and sister who refused to abandon each other in the dark, carried more voltage than an entire empire of fear.

"By the power... of the Grand... Decree..." the Wizard breathed, his voice slowing to a crawl as the last of his purple runes faded from crimson to pale grey, and finally to pure, empty white. "The Dream Kingdom... is deleting me..."

With a final, drawn-out glitching chord that snapped into complete, tranquil silence, the last pixel of the Grand Wizard vanished into the morning air—and with him ended the four-hundred-and-fourth and final year of the Era of the Crystal.
