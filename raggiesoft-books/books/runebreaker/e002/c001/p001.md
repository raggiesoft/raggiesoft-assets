Where the black needle of the Obsidian Spire had stood for generations, a towering fountain of living turquoise crystal now rose into a cloudless azure sky, ringing with the triumphant sixteen-bit horns and sweeping orchestral strings of a world reborn.

By unanimous proclamation of the Crystal Sage and the council of elders, the moment the last purple rune vanished from the sky marked the end of the Era of the Crystal and the beginning of the First Day of the First Dawn, Year One of the Era of the Restored Realm.

In the sunlit central plaza of Sanctuary Village, lanterns and garlands of chiming crystal wildflowers hung from every timber balcony. Ocea stood beside the fountain—still barefoot in her cotton t-shirt and faded 1992 denim jeans—while Tay stood right beside her in his own t-shirt and jeans, his arm draped warmly and protectively around her shoulders. Every few minutes, as if just to make sure neither of them was dreaming, Tay would pull her into another tight, rib-squeezing bear hug, completely free of the coldness that had haunted the dungeon closet.

"To the Twins of the Medallion!" the Crystal Sage announced, raising a goblet of sparkling mana spring water as the villagers cheered. "Who walked into the dark armed with nothing but casual cotton, denim, and a piece of summer-camp clay, and rewrote the fate of our Kingdom!"

Ocea laughed, her cheeks warming as she carefully unstrapped the *Runebreaker* gauntlet from her forearm and handed it back to the Sage. Now that the red and blue halves of their clay medallion were permanently fused into a single, unbroken circle—its zigzag seam glowing with a hairline thread of solid gold—the gauntlet's work was done.

As the triumphant fanfare softened into a gentle, nostalgic duet of harp and wooden flute, the Crystal Sage adjusted his spectacles and pointed his brass staff toward the center of the village fountain. The water parted, revealing a shimmering, six-foot oval of warm golden scanlines that hummed with the familiar sixty-hertz purr of their living room CRT television.

"A clean save-state conduit," the Sage explained softly, his eyes twinkling with fondness. "Calibrated to the exact coordinate and minute you left your world on October 30, 1992. Step through together, and the Directory will remember your names for as long as the crystals sing."

Ocea looked up at Tay. He smiled—that warm, steady, unmistakable smile that belonged to her older twin brother—and held out his hand.

"Ready to go home, Player Two?" Tay asked.

"Ready," Ocea smiled, lacing her fingers through his as they stepped together into the golden light.
