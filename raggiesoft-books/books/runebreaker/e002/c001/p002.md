With a soft pop of warm static that smelled faintly of roasted chestnuts and mountain pine rather than burning ozone, Ocea and Tay tumbled back onto the soft, worn carpet of their living room.

Outside the bay window, the same October wind was still rustling the dry autumn leaves along the street. On the wall above the fireplace, the round clock showed 8:50 PM—barely five minutes after Tay had first slotted the black cartridge into the console.

For a long moment, neither of them moved. Ocea wiggled her bare toes against the familiar plush pile of the rug, then looked over at Tay. He was sitting right beside her in his t-shirt and jeans, his chest rising and falling evenly, his eyes bright and completely clear of purple shadows.

Without a word, Tay reached out and pulled Ocea into his lap, wrapping both arms around her in a long, quiet, breathless hug, resting his chin on the top of her head just like he always had when they were kids and a thunderstorm finally rolled past the house. Ocea buried her face against his cotton shirt, letting out a long, shaky breath as the last of the dungeon's chill melted away for good.

When they finally pulled back a fraction of an inch to look at the entertainment stand, the matte-black cartridge was gone from the console slot—replaced once more by their familiar grey copy of *Super Pixel Plumbers*.

Yet on the curved glass screen of the CRT television, before the game title loaded, a single, peaceful music-box and soft piano lullaby drifted from the speakers. Against a tranquil backdrop of starlight and a spinning golden crystal, two lines of clean, uncorrupted text glowed softly on the screen:

*SAVE STATE COMPLETE // ERA OF THE RESTORED REALM*
*THANK YOU FOR PLAYING — UNTIL NEXT TIME*

Ocea looked down at their joined hands. Resting between their palms was their summer-camp clay medallion—no longer snapped into two jagged pieces, but fused permanently whole, the fire-engine red and ocean-blue halves united by a shimmering seam of real, solid gold.

Tay grinned, looping the leather cord of the united medallion over the lamp stand between them so it belonged to both of them at once, before picking up Player One's controller and tossing Player Two's controller into Ocea's hands.

"Come on," Tay said warmly, bumping his shoulder against hers on the living room floor. "World Four's waiting. And this time, you're taking lead."
