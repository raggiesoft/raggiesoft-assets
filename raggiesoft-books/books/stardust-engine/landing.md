title: "The Stardust Engine: The Story of the Iron Five"
---
Step into the thirty-year story of [**The Stardust Engine**](https://stardust.raggiesoft.com), told in third-person limited narrative form from the perspective of lead guitarist **Ryan O'Connell**.

In the autumn of **1986**, inside a cold cinder-block garage on **Progress Street** in Blacksburg, Virginia, siblings **Ryan** and **Cassidy O'Connell** and their identical twin maternal cousins **Evan** and **Tyler Wright** wrote their very first song together at **Commonwealth Polytechnic Institute (CPI)**: [**"The Stardust Engine"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/1987-electric-color?track=1-13-the-stardust-engine)—celebrating their shared love of the cosmos (**"Stardust"**) and the rockets to get you there (**"Engine"**), while sixteen-year-old youngest sister **Holly O'Connell** guarded their masters with her famous `$1.00` Perpetual Mutual Cross-License.

Across six chronological eras, follow Ryan from the 1980s college garages of Blacksburg and the corporate pop machinery of Apex Records, through the blinding snow of **Mile Marker 98** on December 10, 1990 that left him with a complete T10 spinal cord injury, the 1992 *Friction* walkout and the 1995 warehouse sessions where he yanked his own amplifier plug out of the wall to create the **Main Engine Cutoff** of Cassidy's magnum opus [**"Escape Velocity (Ad Astra)"**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible?track=escape-velocity-ad-astra-live-at-the-crucible) (known to fans simply as ***"Ad Astra"***), the quiet living-room sanctuary of [**Our Own Frequency**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2008-our-own-frequency), and finally back home to the 65,000-seat bowl of **The Crucible** on **May 21, 2016** for [**Live at the Crucible**](https://music.raggiesoft.com/artists/the-stardust-engine/2016-live-at-the-crucible)—crossing paths with sophomore [**Jordan Fuller**](https://books.raggiesoft.com/aubrie/spring-semester-2016/live-at-the-crucible-senior-commencement) sitting down in `Section 104, Row A, Seat 1`.
