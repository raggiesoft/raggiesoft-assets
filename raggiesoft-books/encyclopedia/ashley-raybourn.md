**Ashley Raybourn** is a pivotal character in the Stardust universe, operating primarily out of The Engine Room and The Hollow Square.

## Early Life
Born in the wake of the 1970s crash, Ashley developed an uncanny ability to navigate both physical and digital systems. She grew up deeply protective of the Manning Family legacy.

## The Wingman Briefing
Ashley serves as Michael's "Wingman," ensuring his operations remain secure.

### The Ad Astra Protocol
A specialized protocol that forces all surrounding systems into a dark mode isolation chamber, guaranteeing zero external interference.

## Current Whereabouts
She is currently stationed in **The Hollow Square**, continuing her work on the 1883 project.
