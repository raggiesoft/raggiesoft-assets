# Claire Hayes

Claire Hayes is the mother of fraternal twins Drew and Maggie. Like her daughter, Claire’s life was fractured into a "before" and "after" in 1995 when she entrusted her six-year-old son to the care of a seemingly reputable martial arts instructor, Master Curtis.

The horrific incident—in which Curtis crushed Drew, leading to emergency surgery and lifelong medical vulnerability—left Claire with her own profound scars. For years, she navigated the agonizing guilt of having signed her children up for the class, an action made with only the best intentions for their growth and confidence. 

Despite the tragedy, the Hayes household did not shatter into resentment or blame. Drew harbors no anger toward his mother or his sister. Instead, Claire has spent the decades since 1995 fostering an environment of absolute safety and unconditional support. She witnessed the immediate, visceral shift in her daughter Maggie, who took on the role of fierce protector, and rather than forcing her children to adhere to conventional expectations of independence, Claire respected the profound, life-saving trauma bond the twins formed. 

Now, as the twins navigate adulthood and the harrowing realities of the 2020 pandemic as an immunocompromised family unit, Claire remains their steady, unshakeable foundation, having learned the hardest possible way that family is not just about raising children—it is about keeping them alive against all odds.
