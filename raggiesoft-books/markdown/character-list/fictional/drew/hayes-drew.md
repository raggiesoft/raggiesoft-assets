# Drew Hayes

Born in 1989, Drew Hayes is a survivor of catastrophic childhood trauma. At the age of six, what was supposed to be his very first day of martial arts classes at the Dojang turned into a nightmare that would permanently alter the trajectory of his life.

In a horrifying abuse of power, Master Curtis weaponized a lethal adult pain-tolerance ritual against the children, deliberately stepping on Drew and crushing him under his adult weight. The blunt-force trauma was devastating. Drew suffered severe internal bleeding and a ruptured spleen, requiring emergency, life-saving surgery. As a result, he was left permanently immunocompromised.

But the physical scars were only half the story. The event left Drew with profound, lasting PTSD. Decades later, the visceral terror of the dark remains—the deep-seated fear that Curtis will step out of the shadows to crush him again.

Despite his immense vulnerability and the rigid calculus required to navigate the world as an immunocompromised adult—especially during the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic—Drew is not a resentful man. He is anchored by the fierce, unwavering love of his fraternal twin sister, Maggie. He relies on her not just for physical safety, but for profound emotional harbor, finding peace in the fact that, no matter what happens, he will never have to face the dark alone.
