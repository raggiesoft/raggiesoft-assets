# Maggie Hayes

Born eleven minutes before her fraternal twin brother Drew in 1989, Maggie Hayes (short for Margret) took the mantle of the "older sibling" with a terrifying, permanent seriousness after the fall of 1995.

Maggie and Drew were supposed to attend their first day of martial arts classes together, but a fever kept Maggie at home. In a fateful decision born of childhood innocence, she insisted that Drew go without her, promising to join him as soon as she was better. Instead, Drew was critically crushed by their instructor, Master Curtis.

The survivor's guilt Maggie harbors is immense and entirely uncompromising. Since that day, her life has been defined by an absolute refusal to ever leave her brother's side again. She is a lifelong caretaker and a human radar system, constantly scanning environments for microscopic and physical threats to her immunocompromised twin.

Their bond is so profound that it utterly defies conventional societal milestones. Even at 37 years old, the trauma of the Dojang continues to haunt Drew with terrifying night terrors. In response, Maggie still climbs into his bed every single night, cuddling him to sleep and physically standing between him and the monsters in the dark. For Maggie, there is no sacrifice too great, and no vigilance too extreme, when it comes to keeping her brother safe.
