# The Pelletier Twins: Naming Architecture & Lore

* **The Surname:** Pelletier
 * **Origin:** A classic French-Canadian name reflecting deep ancestral roots in Québec.
 * **The Americanized Pronunciation:** "PELL-uh-teer." This is the flattened, straightforward version commonly adopted over generations for convenience in the States.
 * **The Authentic Québécois Pronunciation:** "pell-TYAY" (or "pell-tee-AY"). This retains the traditional French phonetic structure, dropping the hard 'r' and placing the emphasis smoothly on the final syllable.
## The Telemarketer Lore & The Anchor Moment

 * **The Setup:** A telemarketer rapidly scanning a call sheet sees the name Pelletier, panics at the vowels, and auto-fills the closest English noun. They ask over the phone: "May I speak to Mr. or Mrs. Pelican, please?"
 * **The Loyalty Protocol:** Instead of treating her name as a punchline or settling for the Americanized convenience of "PELL-uh-teer," the protagonist makes a deliberate, high-effort choice. He looks her right in the eyes and says, "If you're going to be my best friend, I better know how to pronounce Pelletier correctly." He then perfectly executes the authentic Québécois "pell-TYAY," establishing himself as a safe, permanent fixture who deeply respects her identity.

## Elise Pelletier (The Anchor)

 * **The Reasoning:** The name was selected for its soft, safe, and approachable cadence. "Elise" feels incredibly protective and grounded, perfectly fitting a character designed to be a reliable, uncomplicated lifeline.
 
## Kiera Pelletier (The Catalyst)

 * **The Reasoning:** Chosen for the sharp "K" sound. It perfectly matches the jarring, deep-pressure energy of her unexpected arrival and the cinematic "Twin Magic" prank, contrasting beautifully with Elise's softer demeanor.
