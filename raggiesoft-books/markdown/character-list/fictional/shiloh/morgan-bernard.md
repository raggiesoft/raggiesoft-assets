# Bernard Morgan

## Overview
Bernard Morgan is the principal of Northwood High School. Unlike many strict administrators, Bernard is incredibly approachable and insists that students and staff call him by his first name. This approachability makes him a trusted figure within the school.

## Relationship with Matt Miller
Bernard has a strong, protective relationship with Matt. He has taken the time to learn Matt's non-verbal cues and can instantly recognize when Matt is in distress. When Courtney Evans attempted to corner Matt and solicit him in the hallway, Bernard was the one Matt trusted to communicate the incident to via his Quantum WritePad. Bernard’s immediate reaction was absolute fury toward the violation of Matt’s boundaries, and he swiftly ensured Matt's safety while preparing to initiate severe disciplinary action against Courtney.

## Personality
Warm, perceptive, and deeply caring, but possesses an ironclad sense of authority when students are threatened or exploited. He prioritizes student well-being over administrative bureaucracy.
