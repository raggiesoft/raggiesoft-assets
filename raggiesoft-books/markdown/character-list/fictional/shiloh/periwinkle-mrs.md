# Mrs. Periwinkle

## I. Core Demographics
*   **Name:** Mrs. Periwinkle
*   **Age:** 60 (in the 1999 academic year)
*   **Role:** Head Librarian, Northwood High School
*   **Aesthetic:** White-collar meticulousness. Characterized by perfectly coiffed gray hair, neatly pressed pastel cardigans, reading glasses on a chain, and an impossibly tight-lipped expression.

## II. The Dewey Decimal Terror
Mrs. Periwinkle rules the academic wing with absolute, uncompromising psychological warfare. 
*   **The Demand for Order:** She operates out of a highly structured library featuring endless rows of wooden bookshelves and a vintage 1999 desktop card catalog. She enforces a policy of absolute, uninterrupted silence.
*   **The Psychological Toll:** Like Mrs. DeMarco, she offers off-the-record rehabilitation to save a student's permanent transcript. However, her punishments are entirely mental. Offenders are forced to organize endless shelves of books in mathematically flawless Dewey Decimal order under her silent, unblinking supervision.
*   **The Exchange Rate of Consequences:** Her reputation for mind-numbing, meticulous torture is so severe that it alters student behavior. When given the choice, troublemakers like Josh Riley will actively choose the humiliating, physical disgust of Mrs. DeMarco's grease traps over facing Mrs. Periwinkle's agonizing library categorization.
