# Amy's Character Profile (Fall 2007 – Spring 2009)

* **Name:** Amy Marie Ouellette
* **Date of Birth:** 09-03-1988

**The Lifeline and the Anchor**
* She entered the picture in the aftermath of abuse, actively validating your worth and establishing a deep, emotional debt that anchored your absolute loyalty.
* The relationship was strictly spatial, confined entirely to the Community College of Hampton Roads campus. The Summer 2008 trip to the aquarium stands as the sole, monumental exception to this rule.

**The Pre-Diagnosis Sanctuary (The Autistic Mask)**
* **The Invisible Weight:** In 2007, you did not possess the clinical vocabulary for "Autistic Masking." The innocence of asking "Mask, what mask?" stemmed from the fact that she was the only environment safe enough to remove it. You only recognized the burden of the armor because of the relief of taking it off around her.
* **The First Safe Person:** Declaring her "my favorite" friend was the pre-diagnostic equivalent of designating her as your very first non-family "Safe Person." It was a functional, literal classification of safety, not just a social ranking. 
* **The Comfort Object Metaphor:** The physical dynamic of holding her tightly functioned as deep pressure sensory regulation. She provided the absolute safety and predictable grounding weight of a child holding a favorite stuffed animal—zero performance required, just pure, unmasked safety. 

**The "Shallow End" Rehabilitation (2026 Retrospective Realization)**
* **The Accidental Sanctuary:** While her strict boundaries were originally designed to manage her own anxiety, looking back from 2026 reveals they provided the exact low-stakes "shallow end" environment necessary after surviving an abusive relationship. 
* **The Proxy Romance:** Despite the lack of traditional romantic involvement, you spent entire weekdays together on campus, projecting the outward appearance of an established boyfriend and girlfriend dynamic to the rest of the world.
* **Absolute Loyalty:** She is classified historically as "the person I was with." The devotion was so absolute that you explicitly informed her you would reject anyone else asking you out because she was the priority in your life.
* **Protection from the "Deep End":** It is recognized in hindsight that a fully romantic, "all-in" relationship at that fragile stage of recovery might have caused a different kind of system overload. Her boundaries safely prevented you from drowning.

**The Strict Rules of Engagement**
* **The Verbal Shield:** Any expression of love triggered immediate panic. To keep her safe and manage her anxiety, the ultimate proof of devotion was looking her in the eyes and actively stating, "I do not love you."
* **The Touch Tightrope:** Deep hugs were heavily utilized, but holding hands was strictly forbidden.
* **The Cuddle Ban:** Cuddling was an absolute boundary. The physical dynamic was so fragile that she would sometimes misprocess a standard hug as a cuddle, requiring constant reassurance that the status quo was being respected.

**The Twin Dynamic**
* Identical to her sister Kristin (unmarried, retaining their French-Canadian surname).
* The twin relationship is introduced cinematically via the April 1, 2008 "Twin Magic" prank at the Susquehanna Drive bus stop, where Kristin's deep pressure hug contrasted with Amy's gentle embrace.

**The System Crash (End of Spring 2009)**
* The abrupt end occurs on the usual bus ride approaching Susquehanna Drive. A sudden, unprompted physical rejection of the familiar goodbye hug.
* A silent, absolute digital cutoff via a Facebook block upon returning home.
* **The Narrative Correction:** Instead of falling alone, Rachel is immediately there to hold you tightly, providing the safety net that reality failed to offer.
