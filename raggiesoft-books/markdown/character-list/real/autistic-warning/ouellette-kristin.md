# Kristin Ouellette (The Present-Day Catalyst)

* **Name:** Kristin Ouellette
* **Date of Birth:** 09-03-1988

**The Bookend Character**
* She exists as a literal ghost from the past, appearing in your narrative in only two distinct eras separated by an 18-year void.
* **The Anchor to the Past (April 1, 2008):** Her sole role in the early timeline is executing the "Twin Magic" prank at the Susquehanna Drive bus stop, delivering a deep pressure hug that temporarily disoriented you before Amy's arrival.
* **The 18-Year Void:** She completely ceases to exist in your world, entirely absent for the 2009 "System Crash" and the subsequent fallout.

**The 2026 Reappearance & The Autistic Overload**
* **The Catalyst:** A silent digital notification on the Scan Station log at the Kempsville Recreation Center for an Adult Resident Annual Membership.
* **The Biological Threat:** The immediate 1:1 facial match acts as an unblockable physical trigger. Because the brain misidentifies her as the violently removed "Safe Person" (Comfort Object) from 2009, her physical presence threatens an immediate autistic panic and meltdown response.
* **The Defense Mechanism (The Mask):** Triggered an intense, crushing need to compartmentalize the psychological shock to fulfill your duties as the front desk closing supervisor. Survival requires deploying the "Corporate We" and the “[[Ouellette Québécois Firewall|Québécois Firewall]]"—forcing the logical brain online by thinking of her exclusively as a patron named *well-ET* rather than the ghost named *oh-let*.
* **The Phantom Exit:** Anticipated to cross paths again at closing, she instead slips out unseen before 9:00 PM while you are counting the till in the back office, leaving the tension completely unresolved.

**The Asymmetry of Information**
* **The Keeper of the Secret:** She likely holds the true narrative and the answers behind Amy's sudden 2009 cutoff, possessing the truth of the past that you were never privy to.
* **The Stranger at the Desk:** Because your only prior interaction was a single prank in 2008, she has no conscious memory of you. You carry the entire emotional weight of the encounter, while to her, you are simply the employee scanning her card.
