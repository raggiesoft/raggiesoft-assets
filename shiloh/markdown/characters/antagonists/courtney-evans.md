# Courtney Evans

## I. Core Demographics
*   **Full Name:** Courtney Evans
*   **Date of Birth:** July 23, 1981 (Turning 18 in the summer of 1999)
*   **Gender:** Female
*   **Standing Height:** 5'3"
*   **Location:** Virginia Beach, Virginia 
*   **Affiliation:** Northwood High School (Class of 1999)
*   **Role:** Antagonist / Ex-Girlfriend to Matt Miller

## II. The "School Matt" Illusion & Genuine Affection
Courtney was Matt Miller's first genuine romantic connection. It is critical to note that before the bubble popped, she was not abusive; she was genuinely sweet and good for Matt.
*   **The Physical Comfort:** Inside their curated high school bubble, Courtney provided real, grounding physical affection. She liked hugging him and would gently kiss him on the cheek. To Matt, this affection felt incredibly normalizing and safe.
*   **The Bubble:** At Northwood High School, Matt’s complex medical and physical needs were quietly managed by Mr. Vance, a retired male paraprofessional. Courtney enjoyed Matt's personality and intelligence but compartmentalized his severe dyspraxia and non-verbal status as background noise.
*   **The Disconnect:** She loved the *idea* of having Matt as a boyfriend and genuinely cared for him in that controlled environment. However, she was completely unequipped to handle the heavy, clinical reality of his 24/7 care requirements outside of school.

## III. The Omni-Q Betrayal (The Translation Error)
Courtney’s superficial acceptance shattered when the relationship attempted to transition into the real world for a highly anticipated movie date to see *The Paradigm* on Matt's 18th birthday.
*   **The Trigger:** Matt utilized his Vanguard LogicPad to inform her via Omni-Q that his 21-year-old sister, Sarah, would be accompanying them. He logically explained that Sarah was acting in her necessary role as his Private Duty Nurse.
*   **The Teenage Panic:** Courtney lacked the maturity and vocabulary to process the clinical necessity of a care proxy. To her sheltered, 17-year-old brain, her boyfriend just announced that another, older woman was crashing their romantic date.
*   **The Attack:** Blinded by adolescent jealousy, Courtney panicked and lashed out, sending a barrage of cruel, ableist messages directly to Matt's hard drive. 
*   **The Failed Apology:** Realizing she had sabotaged his birthday, Courtney attempted to win Matt back at school by desperately offering a sexual encounter. This was a catastrophic miscalculation on three fronts: Matt had just crossed the legal 18-year-old boundary while she was still 17; he was legally incapacitated and unable to consent regardless; and most crucially, his binary logic had already locked her out because she had been mean to him.

## IV. The Exile
Courtney drastically underestimated Matt's boundaries and the terrifying physical and legal power of his family.
*   **The School Rejection:** Matt fiercely rejected her inappropriate apology, prompting Mr. Vance to physically step in and block her access to his wheelchair.
*   **The Mall Confrontation:** When Courtney attempted to corner Matt at a local mall, she was intercepted by Sarah Miller. Standing a full 13 inches shorter than Sarah's 6'4" bodybuilder frame, Courtney was completely dwarfed. 
*   **Permanent Ban:** Utilizing her newly activated joint-and-several legal guardianship, Sarah threatened Courtney with a restraining order. Courtney was forced into a humiliating retreat and is permanently locked out of the Miller and Brooks family ecosystem.

## V. Legacy & The Alternate Timeline
Courtney is not a malicious villain, but rather the great, tragic "What If" of Matt's life.
*   **The 2026 Grief:** Mirroring Matt’s own lingering grief over Heather Bouchard's sudden departure, Courtney is haunted by her own unhandled exception. To this day (as of 2026), she deeply regrets the misunderstanding, feels profound remorse for her teenage panic, and still misses him. She is permanently staring at a locked gate.
*   **The Lost Sanctuary:** If her 17-year-old brain had taken a breath instead of launching an Omni-Q attack, the timeline would have drastically shifted. Courtney would have eventually become Matt’s life partner—the role currently occupied by the Pelletier twins—and earned a permanent, coveted spot resting alongside him on the living room gymnastics crash mat.
