# Arthur Vance (Mr. Vance)

## I. Core Demographics & Affiliation
*   **Full Name:** Arthur Vance
*   **Gender:** Male
*   **Affiliation:** Northwood High School (Virginia Beach)
*   **Role:** One-on-One Paraprofessional Aide to Matt Miller

## II. The Clinical Anchor at School
While Sarah Miller serves as Matt's primary Private Duty Nurse and ultimate clinical protector, she cannot always be present inside the classroom. In her absence, Arthur Vance is the anchor.
*   **The Retired Professional:** Mr. Vance is a retired male paraprofessional who quietly and efficiently manages Matt's complex medical and physical needs throughout the school day.
*   **The Demeanor:** He is deeply lovable and genuinely good to the kids. He operates with a warm, grandfatherly presence that masks an incredibly sharp awareness of his environment.
*   **The Silent Guardian:** He is the primary reason the "School Matt" illusion existed for Courtney Evans—Mr. Vance absorbed and handled the intense clinical requirements so seamlessly that Matt was able to safely exist in the high school bubble.

## III. The Protective Instinct
Mr. Vance is not just an aide; he is fiercely protective of Matt's physical and emotional boundaries.
*   **The Wall:** When Courtney Evans attempted to corner Matt with an inappropriate apology following the Omni-Q betrayal, Matt fiercely rejected her. Recognizing the immediate threat to Matt's autonomy and safety, Mr. Vance did not hesitate. He physically stepped in, using his own body to form a wall and completely blocking Courtney's access to Matt's wheelchair, demonstrating his absolute loyalty to Matt's well-being.
