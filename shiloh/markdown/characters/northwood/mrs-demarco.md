# Mrs. DeMarco

## I. Core Demographics
*   **Name:** Mrs. DeMarco
*   **Age:** 55 (in the 1999 academic year)
*   **Role:** Cafeteria Supervisor, Northwood High School
*   **Aesthetic:** Blue-collar grit. Frequently seen wearing a hairnet, a heavy industrial kitchen apron, and a cross necklace, possessing a piercing glare that instantly silences the Commons.

## II. The Culinary Warden & The Shadow Law
Mrs. DeMarco is the undisputed ruler of the Northwood High School Commons, operating an entirely off-the-record justice system that keeps students out of the Principal's Office.
*   **The Rehabilitation Protocol:** When students commit infractions (bullying, rudeness, disrespect), Mrs. DeMarco intercepts them before it reaches formal administration. By handling punishments internally, she protects their permanent academic records.
*   **The Physical Toll (Trash Duty):** Her brand of justice is strictly physical. Lower-level offenses earn a week of "Trash Duty" at the dumpsters by the Service Entrance. Severe infractions result in "Advanced Sanitation," which involves the humiliating, grueling labor of scraping gum and cleaning the kitchen's grease traps.
*   **The "Sweet Angel" Proviso:** Despite her terrifying exterior, she possesses immense warmth for polite students and those using AAC devices (like Nichole Bennett), rewarding manners with extreme patience and larger food portions.

## III. "The DeMarco Decree"
Her legend is so entrenched in the school's culture that she was immortalized in song.
*   **The Paper Wall Tribute:** In 2026, the 1980s heartland rock prequel album *Guiding Stars* featured Track 4, "The DeMarco Decree". 
*   **The Target:** The upbeat rock anthem specifically chronicles Mrs. DeMarco raining swift, off-the-record justice down on notorious school bullies like Josh Riley, who is described as the "king of the obscene" making "another ugly little scene".
