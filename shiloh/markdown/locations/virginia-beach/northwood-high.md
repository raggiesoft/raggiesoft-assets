# Northwood High School: Master Lore Bible

*   **Version:** 3.1
*   **Setting Era:** Fall 1999 – Spring 2003
*   **Location:** Virginia Beach, VA (Alternate - Fictional replacement for Tallwood High School)
*   **Address:** 1668 Kempsville Road, Virginia Beach, VA 23464
*   **Mascot:** The Lion (Purple & Gold)

## I. The Protagonists: The Bennett Twins (Class of \'03)
*Note: Matt Miller graduated in the year 1999. This lore document primarily tracks the protagonists of a concurrent narrative who are Freshmen in 1999.*

### Biographical Stats
*   **Birthdate:** April 8, 1985 (Newport, RI). Family moved to Virginia Beach when they were 2 years old.
*   **Patrick ("Pat"):** Born 8:38 PM. Ambulatory with CP/Limp.
*   **Nichole ("Nikki"):** Born 8:50 PM. Manual Tilt-in-Space Wheelchair (Titan AeroMotion Z-5). Non-verbal (uses a VocaLink 3000).
*   **Nickname Rules:** Patrick is the only one allowed to call her "Nikki"; Nichole is the only one allowed to call him "Pat."

### The Dynamic
*   **Locomotion:** They function as an interdependent unit. Patrick uses Nikki’s wheelchair handles as a walker to stabilize his gait; Nikki anchors his pace.
*   **The Conflict:** The school administration frowns upon this, preferring they remain independent. The parents strongly support it (even in writing), but the school resists.
*   **The Dream (Fall 1999):** As freshmen, they share a naive dream of going to college together and sharing a dorm room with a queen-sized bed (mirroring how they prefer to sleep at home). In 1999, they do not yet realize that college dorms are strictly gender-segregated.

## II. Geography & Exterior

### The "Asphalt Sea" (Student Parking)
*   **Layout:** A massive grid of asphalt directly in front of the main building.
*   **The Hierarchy:** Seniors fight for spots near the single exit lane leading to Kempsville Road to escape the dismissal bottleneck. Underclassmen are relegated to the back rows.

### The Drop-Off Zone (Lions Way)
*   **The Shelter:** Blue metal awnings cover the walkway from the curb to the building towers, offering protection from the frequent Virginia rain.
*   **The "One Door" Protocol:** The main entrance block has multiple doors. Only the door on the far left (closest to the Main Office) remains unlocked during the day to funnel traffic past administration. All others are locked.

### The "Backstage" (Bus Loop)
*   **Visuals:** Located at the rear. Characterized by three brick towers with circular "porthole" windows.
*   **The "Timed" Doors:** Entrances A, B, and C are unlocked only during active bus unloading (approx. 7:00 AM – 7:20 AM). Once buses depart, these lock. Late students must circle the entire building.
*   **Stairway A (The Critical Entry):** Buses with wheelchair lifts must drop off here (far right tower) to access the building\'s only elevator.

### The Conflict Zone (Practice Field)
*   **Usage:** Shared territory for the Marching Band and Driver\'s Ed.
*   **Visual Confusion:** The asphalt is painted with conflicting lines—Blue lines for stadium 10-yard markers, and White lines for traffic lanes.
*   **Activity:** Students often perform donuts here; those caught face discipline.

### The Neighborhood Perimeter (Highland Way)
*   **Smoker\'s Corner:** A pedestrian gate connecting the school to The Highlands neighborhood.
*   **Protocol:** The SRO (Officer Whitehead) watches the property line. 18-year-old seniors may smoke on the public street side of the line. Crossing the threshold with a lit cigarette is a violation.
*   **The Curb Trap:** There are no ADA curb cuts at these gates, rendering this shortcut impossible for wheelchair users.

## III. Interior Architecture & Navigation

### The Commons (The Hub)
*   **Aesthetics:** High ceilings with exposed blue metal trusses.
*   **Flooring:** Wide, square bumpy tiles with deep grout lines.
*   **Sensory Experience:** High vibration and noise for wheelchair users. This is the holding area before the first bell.

### The "Forbidden" Courtyard
*   **Visible:** Through the windows at the back of the Commons. It houses a bronze Lion statue on a brick pedestal.
*   **Status:** Permanently locked. Following the Columbine massacre (April 1999), this area was deemed a security risk ("fishbowl") and is no longer accessible to students.

### The "Blue Mile" (Academic Wing)
*   **The Airlock:** Heavy fire doors separate the Commons from the hallway, held open by electromagnets. They slam shut if the fire alarm pulls or power fails.
*   **Flooring:** Thin industrial carpet. Transitioning from the Commons tile to this carpet creates immediate "drag" (rolling resistance).
*   **Traffic Physics:** The hallway is lined with blue lockers. When open, the navigable width shrinks, forcing a wheelchair user to stay in the "Center Lane," acting as a pace car for all traffic behind them.

### Vertical Access
*   **Location:** Stairway A alcove.
*   **The Machine:** A single Dover elevator (hydraulic model).
*   **Risk:** It is a single point of failure. If it breaks, the second floor is effectively deleted from the map for the twins.

## IV. The Administrative Zone & Hygiene

*   **The Main Office:** Located right of the Main Entrance. The checkpoint for late arrivals.
*   **The Schola:** A rarely used, 100-seat mini-auditorium next to the office. Usually dark and empty, creating a "dead zone."
*   **The Service Entrance:** Behind the cafeteria. Home to the dumpsters and the site of "Trash Duty."

### The Clinic (Hygiene Exile)
*   **Location:** Past the Schola, next to the "Morning Drop-off" doors.
*   **The Separation Policy:** Because the locker rooms are not accessible/safe for Nikki, she must change here.
*   **The Rule:** The school strictly prohibits the twins from helping each other here, regardless of parental permission.
*   **Nikki:** Is assisted by a school-provided aide in the Clinic.
*   **Patrick:** Is forced to use the Men\'s Locker Room alone.

## V. The Shadow Law (The Implied DeMarco Code)
*Note: Mrs. DeMarco (Cafeteria Supervisor) never verbally states these rules. They are implied, enforcing a culture of fear and reverence.*

### The Inferred Rules
*   **Civility:** Rude behavior results in immediate justice.
*   **The Mystery Meat Edict:** No verbal, facial, or olfactory critique of the food is permitted.
*   **The Polyglot Trap:** Mrs. DeMarco is fluent in multiple languages. Insulting her in a foreign tongue results in destruction in that language.
*   **The Annual Idiot:** Every year, some freshman idiot thinks they are the first to discover "The Penis Game" (whoever shouts the word the loudest without getting Mrs. DeMarco's attention wins). All attempts inevitably fail. The universal punishment is a 2:15 PM date with the dumpsters (Trash Duty).
*   **Zero Tolerance:** Bullying or harassment results in immediate assignment to the dumpsters.

### The Punishment Scale
*   **Level 0:** "The Look" (Disappointment).
*   **Level 1:** Trash Duty (1 week at the dumpsters).
*   **Level 2:** Advanced Sanitation (Grease traps and gum scraping).
*   **Level 3:** The Essay (5–20 pages on topics like "The Virtues of Silence").
*   **Level 4:** Principal Referral (The Nuclear Option/The Abyss).

### The "Sweet Angel" Proviso
*   Genuine politeness or necessary usage of AAC devices is rewarded with extreme patience and warmth. She is only a tyrant to the rude.

## VI. The Classroom Environment
*   **Visuals:** Green chalkboards (no whiteboards).
*   **The "Kidney Breakers" (Furniture):** Hard blue plastic chairs attached to laminate desks via a metal bar.
*   **Conflict:** Impossible for wheelchairs. Nikki is forced to sit at a separate, standalone table, often isolated at the back or side of the room.
*   **Technology:** TVs on rolling carts; must be reserved.
*   **Intercom Quirk:** Announcements come from the ceiling, but teachers must answer via a wall-mounted PBX phone. Substitute teachers frequently yell at the ceiling in confusion.

## VII. Athletics & Physical Education

### The Gymnasium
*   **Floor:** High-gloss polished wood. Slippery for crutches, fast for wheels.
*   **Bleachers:** Retractable and inaccessible. The twins must sit on the floor level during assemblies.

### The Wrestling Room
*   **Terrain:** Deep purple foam mats.
*   **Physics:** A "Soft Trap." Wheels sink into the foam, making movement exhausting.

### The Stadium
*   **Seating:** Metal bleachers with no ramps. Wheelchair users are relegated to the track/sidelines, physically separated from the student section ("The Zoo").

## VIII. Era Specifics (The "Shadow of Columbine")
*   **Security:** Exterior doors are strictly timed.
*   **Tech:** No Smartphones. Communication is via pagers, Nordic bricks, or payphones.
*   **Atmosphere:** Pre-9/11 security theater implies a world that is wary, but not yet fully locked down by TSA-style standards.
*   **Smoking:** Legal for 18-year-olds but strictly policed.
